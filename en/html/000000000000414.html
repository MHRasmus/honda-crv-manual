Ambient Light Test/Replacement
With sunroof/sunshade and/or navigation
NOTE: The ambient light is built into the sunroof switch.
  1. Remove the front individual map light.
  1. Disconnect the 10P connector (A) from the ambient light (B), then remove the ambient light from the map light housing (C).
  1. Connect battery power to the No. 5 [No. 4] terminal and ground to the No. 3 [No. 5] terminal. The ambient light should turn on. If the light does not turn on, replace the ambient light.
    NOTE: The ambient light is built into the sunroof/sunshade switch or the navigation microphone. If the ambient light is faulty, replace the sunroof/sunshade switch or the navigation microphone as an assembly.
    [ ]: With navigation and without sunroof/sunshade.
  1. Install the switch and light in the reverse order of removal.
