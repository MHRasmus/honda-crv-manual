Catalytic Converter (Warm-up) Replacement
  1. Without DPF system: Remove the catalytic converter.
  1. With DPF system: Remove the DPF.
  1. With DPF system: Remove EGT sensor 1.
  1. Remove the heat shield.
  1. Remove exhaust pipe A.
    NOTE: When installing exhaust pipe A, replace the gasket (B).
  1. Remove the lower torque rod mounting bolt (A).
  1. Remove the warm-up catalytic converter stay (A).
  1. Remove the nuts (A), then turn the warm-up catalytic converter (B).
  1. Turn the warm-up catalytic converter (A) as shown.
  1. Turn the warm-up catalytic converter (A), then take it out by turn it as shown.
  1. Remove the converter covers (A).
  1. Install the parts in the reverse order of removal with a new gaskets.
