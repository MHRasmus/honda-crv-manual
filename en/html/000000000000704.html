DTC Troubleshooting: B1000
DTC B1000:
 Communication Bus Line Error
  1. Clear the DTCs with the HDS.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF, and then back ON (II).
  1. Wait for 6 seconds or more.
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.

Are DTCs B1000, B1008, and B1011 all indicated?
YES -
Faulty MICU, replace the under-dash fuse/relay box.
NO -
Go to Step 12 .
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Check for continuity between the under-dash fuse/relay box connector Q (16P) No. 6 terminal and body ground.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Repair a short to ground in the wire between the under-dash fuse/relay box and the affected control unit.■
NO -
Go to Step 14 .
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Measure voltage between the under-dash fuse/relay box connector Q (16P) No. 6 terminal and body ground.
Is there voltage?
YES -
Repair a short to power in the wire between the under-dash fuse/relay box and the affected control unit.■
NO -
Go to Step 16 .
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Reconnect the under-dash fuse/relay box connector Q (16P).
  1. Reconnect the gauge control module connector A (36P).
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Clear the DTCs with the HDS.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF, and then back ON (II).
  1. Wait for 6 seconds or more.
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.
Are DTCs B1000, B1008, and B1011 all indicated?
YES -
Repair the gauge control module. ■
NO -
Go to Step 24 .
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Reconnect the immobilizer-keyless control unit 7P connector.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Clear the DTCs with the HDS.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF, and then back ON (II).
  1. Wait for 6 seconds or more.
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.
Are DTCs B1000, B1008, and B1011 all indicated?
YES -
Replace the immobilizer-keyless control unit .■
NO -
Go to Step 31 .
