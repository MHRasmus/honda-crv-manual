DTC Troubleshooting: 12, 14, 16, 18
DTC 12, 14, 16, 18:
 Wheel Sensor (Electrical Noise/Intermittent Interruption)
NOTE:
  • If the ABS indicator comes on because of electrical noise, the indicator will go off when you test-drive the vehicle at 15 km/h (10 mph) or more and noise is gone.
  • An incorrectly installed wheel bearing can cause one of these DTCs.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Check the appropriate wheel sensor and magnetic encoder on the front wheel bearing or magnetic encoder on the rear hub bearing unit.
