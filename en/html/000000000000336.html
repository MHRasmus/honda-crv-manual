Dashboard Front Console Cover Removal/Installation
NOTE: Take care not to scratch the dashboard and related parts.
  1. Remove the console cover (A).
    -1.
    Remove the clips (B).
    -2.
    Gently pull out the cover to detach the clips (C).
    -3.
    Driver's side: Pull the cover away to release the pin (D) from the holder (E).
    Passenger's side: Pull the cover to release the hook (F).
  1. Install the cover in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • Check if the clips are damaged or stress-whitened, and if necessary, replace them with new ones.
    • Push the clips and hook into place securely.
