Dashboard Passenger's Tray Removal/Installation
SRS components are located in this area. Review the SRS component locations and the precautions and procedures before doing repairs or service.
NOTE:
  • When removing components, use the trim tool or equivalent.
  • Take care not to scratch the dashboard and related parts.
  1. Remove the passenger's vent panel.
  1. Open the lid.
  1. Detach the hook of the glove box damper and release the glove box stop on each side from the dashboard by pushing them inside.
  1. Lift up the mat. Pull out the side edge of the side lid (A) by hand to release the hooks (B), then remove the side lid.
  1. Remove the access panel (A), then remove the screws from the tray (B).
  1. Remove the screws from the access panel opening (A).
  1. Pull out the right portion of the tray (A) to release the hooks (B).
  1. Slide the tray (A) toward the right, then pull out the right end of the tray, and remove it from the dashboard.
  1. Install the tray in the reverse order of removal.
