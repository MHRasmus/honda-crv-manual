EPS Motor Removal and Installation
NOTE: Do not allow dust, dirt, or other foreign materials to enter the steering gearbox.
Removal-LHD type
  1. Remove the steering gearbox.
  1. Remove the motor 1P connector (A), motor 2P connector (B), and motor angle sensor 8P connector (C) from the connector bracket (D).
  1. Remove the harness clip (E) from the connector bracket.
  1. Remove the motor (A) from the steering gearbox, then remove the O-ring (B) and discard it.
Installation-LHD type
  1. Clean the mating surface of the motor (A) and the steering gearbox.
  1. Apply a thin coat of silicone grease to the new O-ring (B), and carefully fit it on the motor.
  1. Apply silicone grease into the motor shaft (C).
  1. Install the motor on the steering gearbox by engaging the motor shaft and the worm shaft (D).
  1. Before tightening the bolts, turn the motor two or three times to the right and left about 45 degrees. Make sure the motor is evenly seated on the steering gearbox, and that the O-ring is not pinched between the mating surfaces.
  1. Install the harness clip (A) to the connector bracket (B).
  1. Install the motor 1P connector (C), motor 2P connector (D), and motor angle sensor 8P connector (E) to the connector bracket.
  1. Finish the installation, make sure the motor and the EPS wires are not caught or pinched by any parts.
  1. Install the steering gearbox.
Removal-RHD type
  1. Make sure you have the anti-theft code for the audio system (if equipped), then write down the audio presets.
  1. Make sure the ignition switch is OFF, then disconnect the negative cable from the battery.
  1. Disconnect the motor 1P connector (A), motor 2P connector (B), and motor angle sensor 8P connector (C) from the steering gearbox.
  1. Remove the connectors (D) from the connector bracket (E), then remove the harness clips (F).
  1. Remove the motor (A) from the steering gearbox, then remove the O-ring (B) and discard it.
Installation-RHD type
  1. Clean the mating surface of the motor (A) and the steering gearbox.
  1. Apply a thin coat of silicone grease to the new O-ring (B), and carefully fit it on the motor.
  1. Apply silicone grease into the motor shaft (C).
  1. Install the motor on the steering gearbox by engaging the motor shaft and the worm shaft (D).
  1. Before tightening the bolts, turn the motor two or three times to the right and left about 45 degrees. Make sure the motor is evenly seated on the steering gearbox, and that the O-ring is not pinched between the mating surfaces.
  1. Install the motor 1P connector (A), motor 2P connector (B), and motor angle sensor 8P connector (C) to the connector bracket (D).
  1. Install the harness clips (E), then connect the connectors (F) to the steering gearbox.
    NOTE: Make sure to push these connectors until you hear a click so that the connectors are secured.
  1. Finish the installation, make sure the motor and the EPS wires are not caught or pinched by any parts.
  1. Reconnect the negative cable to the battery.
  1. Enter the anti-theft code for the audio system (if equipped), then enter the audio presets.
  1. After installation, start the engine, and let it idle. Turn the steering wheel from lock-to-lock several times. Check that the EPS indicator does not come on.
