Transfer Output Shaft Bearing Removal/Installation
Special Tools Required

Driver, 40 mm I.D.
 07746-0030100

Driver attachment, 35 mm I.D.
 07746-0030400
  1. Remove the tapered roller bearing (A) from the transfer output shaft (B) with the bearing separator (C) and a press. Place a shaft protector (D) between the transfer output shaft and the press to prevent damaging the transfer output shaft.
  1. Install the 35 mm thrust shim (A) on the transfer output shaft (B).
  1. Install the tapered roller bearing (C) on the transfer output shaft with the special tools and a press.
