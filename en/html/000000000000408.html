Exterior Light Bulb Replacement - Taillight Part
  1. Remove the taillight assembly.
  1. Turn the bulb sockets (A) 45 ° counterclockwise to remove them from taillight (B).
    Brake Lights/Taillights:
    21/5 W
    Rear Turn Signal Light:
    21 W

    Taillight:
    5 W
    Back-up Light:
    18 W
    Rear Fog Light:
    21 W

  1. Remove the bulbs (C) from the bulb sockets.
  1. Install new bulb(s) in the reverse order of removal.
