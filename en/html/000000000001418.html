A/C Compressor Thermal Protector Replacement
R20A1 and R20A2 engines
  1. Remove the bolt and the holder (A). Disconnect the field coil connector (B), then remove the thermal protector (C).
  1. Replace the thermal protector (A) with a new one, and apply silicone sealant (B) to the bottom of the thermal protector.
  1. Install the thermal protector in the reverse order of removal.
