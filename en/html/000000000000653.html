DTC Troubleshooting: B2128
DTC B2128:
 Steering Angle Sensor Signal Error
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Remove the driver's dashboard lower cover.
  1. Disconnect the 40P connector from the adaptive front lighting control unit.
  1. Remove the steering column covers.
  1. Disconnect the 5P connector from steering angle sensor.
  1. With VSA: Disconnect the 46P connector from VSA modulator-control unit.
  1. Check for continuity between steering angle sensor 5P connector terminals No. 2, No. 4 and No. 3 and adaptive front lighting control unit 40P connector terminals No. 18, No. 38 and No. 17.
Is there continuity?
YES -
  • With VSA: Go to Step 8 .
  • Without VSA: Go to Step 9 .
NO -
Repair an open in the wire.■
  1. Check for continuity between steering angle sensor 5P connector terminals No. 2, No. 4 and No. 3 and VSA modulator-control unit 46P connector terminals No. 32, No. 5 and No. 17.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Go to Step 9 .
NO -
Repair an open in the wire.■
  1. Check for continuity between body ground and adaptive front lighting control unit 40P connector terminals No. 17.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Repair an open in the wire.■
NO -
Go to Step 10 .
  1. Reconnect the adaptive front lighting control unit 40P connector.
  1. Reconnect the steering angle sensor 5P connector.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Measure voltage between body ground and steering angle sensor 5P connector terminals No. 2 and No. 4 by analogue circuit tester during turn the steering wheel slowly.
Does the voltage change 0−5 V?
YES -
Go to Step 14 .
NO -
Faulty steering angle sensor, replace it.■
  1. Measure voltage between steering angle sensor 5P connector terminal No. 3 and No. 1.
Is there about 0.5−4.5 V?
YES -
Faulty adaptive front lighting control unit, replace it and do the adaptive front lighting control unit learning procedure. ■
NO -
Faulty steering angle sensor, replace it.■
