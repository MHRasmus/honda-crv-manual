A/T Drive Plate Removal and Installation
  1. Remove the transmission assembly:
    • K24Z1 engine model
    • R20A1 and R20A2 engine models
  1. Remove the drive plate (A) and washer (B) from the engine crankshaft.
    NOTE: The illustration shows K24Z1 engine model; R20A1 and R20A2 engine models are similar.
  1. Install the drive plate and washer on the engine crankshaft, and tighten the eight bolts in a crisscross pattern in two or more steps.
  1. Install the transmission assembly:
    • K24Z1 engine model
    • R20A1 and R20A2 engine models
