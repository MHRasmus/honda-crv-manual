Seat Heater Test
  1. Remove the driver's or front passenger's seat.
  1. Disconnect the seat heater connector A (3P) and B (2P).
  1. Check for continuity between the seat heater connector B (2P) No. 1 and No. 2 terminals.
    There should be continuity.
  1. Reconnect the seat heater connector B (2P) to the seat-back heater.
  1. Check for continuity between the seat heater connector A (3P) No. 1 and No. 2 terminals, No. 2 and No. 3 terminals, and No. 1 and No. 3 terminals.
    There should be continuity.
  1. If the continuity is not as specified, replace the appropriate seat heater.
