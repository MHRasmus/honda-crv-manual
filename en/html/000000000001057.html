A/T Differential Backlash Inspection
NOTE: The illustration shows the 4WD model; 2WD is similar.
  1. Install the drive shaft and intermediate shaft into the differential assembly.
  1. Secure the driveshaft (A) in a bench vise (B) with soft jaws (C). To prevent damage to the driveshaft, always use soft jaws or equivalent material between the driveshaft and the vise.
  1. Place the intermediate shaft (D) on a V-block (E), then fix the intermediate shaft using a vise (F) or equivalent.
  1. Measure the backlash of the final driven gear (G) with a dial indicator (H).
    Standard:
    0.4−1.3 mm (0.016−0.051 in.)

  1. If the backlash is out of standard, replace the differential carrier:
    • 4WD
    • 2WD
