Rear Differential Reassembly

Exploded View
Special Tools Required

Companion flange holder

  1. Apply differential fluid to the rubber portions of the ATF strainer (A), then install the ATF strainer, the magnet (B), the dowel pins (C), and the oil pump pin (D), in the differential carrier assembly.
  1. Align the grooves (A) of the rear oil pump with the oil pump pin (B), then install the oil pump body assembly (C) on the differential carrier assembly.
  1. Install the oil pump driveshaft (A) by aligning the projection (B) of the oil pump driveshaft with the groove of the front oil pump in the oil pump body assembly.
  1. Install the thrust needle bearing (C).
  1. Install the six steel balls (A), base cam (B), thrust needle bearing (C), and the pressure plate (D) on the clutch assembly (E).
    NOTE: Align the tabs (F) of the pressure plate with the grooves (G) of the clutch assembly.
  1. Make a mark (A) on the periphery of the clutch assembly (B) that corresponds to the position of the tab (C) of the pressure plate (D) as shown. Make a mark (E) on the periphery of the oil pump assembly (F) that corresponds to the position of the groove (G) in the oil pump driveshaft (H) as shown.
  1. Support the pressure plate and clutch assembly by hand so that they do not misalign. With the marks aligned, install the clutch assembly to the oil pump assembly. Install the 30 mm shim (I).
  1. Make sure that the clutch assembly and pressure plate are correctly installed. If the clutch assembly can be pushed down as in the figure, proceed to Step 9 . If not, return to Step 5 and repeat the process.
    NOTE: If incorrectly installed, the clutch assembly may be damaged when excessive force is applied.
  1. Remove the dirt and oil from the sealing surfaces. Apply liquid gasket (P/N 08718-0001) to the sealing surface. Make sure you seal the entire circumference of the bolt holes to prevent fluid leakage.
    NOTE:
    • You must assemble the housings within 5 minutes after applying the liquid gasket. If you do not, the sealing surface must be cleaned, and the liquid gasket reapplied.
    • Allow it to cure at least 30 minutes after assembly before filling the differential with fluid.
  1. Install the 6 x 1.0 mm dowel pins (A) and the torque control differential case (B). Torque the eight mounting bolts in a crisscross pattern in several steps.
  1. Install the companion flange (A), the O-ring (B), the back-up ring (C), the disc spring washer (D), and the locknut (E).
    NOTE: Apply differential fluid to the O-ring.
  1. Install the companion flange holder (A) to the companion flange, then tighten the new locknut to the specified torque.
    Torque:
    147 N·m (15.0 kgf·m, 108 lbf·ft)

  1. Stake the locknut tab (A) into the groove in the clutch guide.
  1. Install the drain plug (A) and the oil filler plug (B) with new washers (C).
