Headlight Replacement
  1. Remove the front bumper.
  1. Remove the three mounting bolts, clip and harness clips, and pull the headlight assembly (A) with the corner upper beam (B).
    Headlight (low beam):
    35 W (with HID)
    55 W (without HID)
    Headlight (high beam):
    60 W
    Front Position Light:
    5 W
    Front Turn Signal Light:
    21 W

  1. Disconnect the connectors (C) from the headlight.
  1. Remove the two mounting bolts and the corner upper beam from the headlight.
  1. Install the headlight in the reverse order of removal.
  1. After replacement, then do the headlights learning procedure (with HID) and adjust the headlights to local requirements.
