A/T 2nd Clutch Transmission Fluid Pressure Switch Replacement
  1. Remove the air cleaner housing and intake air duct.
  1. Disconnect the 2nd clutch transmission fluid pressure switch connector, and remove the switch.
  1. Install the new 2nd clutch transmission fluid pressure switch (A) and a new sealing washer (B), and tighten the switch.
  1. Make sure there is no water, oil, dust, or foreign particles inside the connector.
  1. Connect the connector securely.
  1. Install the intake air duct and air cleaner housing.
