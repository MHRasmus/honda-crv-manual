Cylinder Head Cover Removal
  1. Remove the air cleaner housing assembly.
  1. Remove the engine cover.
  1. Remove the turbocharger boost control solenoid (A).
  1. Remove the bolt (B) securing the fuel feed line, and remove two bolts (C) securing the fuel return line.
  1. Remove the breather hose (A), then remove two bolts (B) securing the vacuum line.
  1. Remove the four injectors.
  1. Remove the cylinder head cover.
