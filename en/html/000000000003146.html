Clutch Damper Replacement
NOTE:
  • Use fender covers to avoid damaging painted surfaces.
  • Do not spill brake fluid on the vehicle; it may damage the paint; if brake fluid does contact the paint, wash it off immediately with water.
  • R20A1 and R20A2 engine models manual transmission model is shown, K24Z1 engine model manual transmission model is similar.
  1. Make sure you have the anti-theft codes for the radio and the navigation system, then write down the customer's audio presets. Disconnect the negative cable from the battery first, then disconnect the positive cable. Remove the battery.
  1. Remove the air cleaner housing;
    • R20A1 and R20A2 engine models
    • K24Z1 engine model
  1. Remove the engine control module (ECM) stay (A), then remove it aside.
  1. Disconnect the clutch pipes (A) from the clutch damper (B).
  1. Remove the clutch damper bracket mounting bolts.
  1. Remove the clutch damper (A) from the clutch damper bracket (B).
  1. Install the clutch damper (A) to the clutch damper bracket (B).
  1. Install the clutch damper bracket mounting bolts.
  1. Connect the clutch pipes (A) to the clutch damper (B).
  1. Install the engine control module (ECM) stay (A).
  1. Install the battery. Connect the positive cable to the battery first, then connect the negative cable.
  1. Bleed the clutch hydraulic system.
  1. Check the clutch operation and check for leaking fluid.
  1. Install the air cleaner housing;
    • R20A1 and R20A2 engine models
    • K24Z1 engine model
  1. Install the battery.
  1. Enter the anti-theft code for the radio, then enter the customer's radio station presets.
  1. Perform the power window control unit reset procedure.
