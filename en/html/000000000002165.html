Transmission Mount Replacement
  1. Make sure you have the anti-theft code for the audio system and the navigation system (if equipped).
  1. Remove the P/S fluid reservoir from the holder.
  1. Remove the two bolts securing the expansion tank, then remove the expansion tank from the bracket.
  1. Loosen the upper torque rod mounting bolt (A).
  1. Remove the battery.
  1. Remove the air cleaner housing assembly.
  1. Remove the harness clamps, then remove the battery base.
  1. Remove the intake air ducts.
  1. Remove the intercooler outlet hose clamp.
  1. Remove the bolt securing the clutch line.
  1. Remove the two bolts, and loosen the two bolts, then remove the battery base bracket.
  1. Raise the vehicle on the lift to full height.
  1. Remove the engine undercover.
  1. Loosen the lower torque rod mounting bolt (A).
  1. Lower the vehicle on the lift.
  1. Support the transmission with a jack and wood block under the transmission.
  1. Remove the transmission mount (A).
  1. Install the transmission mount with the new mounting bolts (B).
  1. Tighten the new transmission mount mounting bolt and nuts (C).
  1. Raise the vehicle on the lift to full height.
  1. Remove the transmission mount (A).
  1. Install the transmission mount with the new mounting bolts (B).
  1. Tighten the new transmission mount mounting bolt and nuts (C).
  1. Raise the vehicle on the lift to full height.
  1. Tighten the lower torque rod mounting bolt.
  1. Install the engine undercover.
  1. Lower the vehicle on the lift.
  1. Tighten the upper torque rod mounting bolt.
  1. Install the expansion tank.
  1. Install P/S fluid reservoir to the holder.
  1. Install the battery base bracket, then install the bolt securing the clutch line.
  1. Install the intercooler outlet hose clamp, then clamp the intercooler outlet pipe.
  1. Install the intake air ducts.
  1. Install the battery base, then install the harness clamps.
  1. Install the air cleaner housing assembly.
  1. Install the battery.
  1. Install the battery. Clean the battery posts and cable terminals with sandpaper, then assemble them, and apply grease to prevent corrosion.
  1. Enter the anti-theft code for the audio system and the navigation system (if equipped).
  1. Set the clock.
