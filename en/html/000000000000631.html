Resetting the Power Window Control Unit (Master Switch)
Resetting the driver's power window is required when any of the following have occurred:
  • Power window regulator replacement or repair
  • Power window motor replacement or repair
  • Window run channel replacement or repair
  • Driver's (or front passenger's) * door glass replacement or repair
  • Power is removed from the power window control unit while the power window timer is ON.

  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Move the driver's (or the front passenger's) * window all the way down by using the driver's window DOWN switch.
  1. Open the driver's door.
    NOTE: Step 4 −7 must be done within 5 seconds of each other.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Push and hold the driver's (or the front passenger's) * window DOWN switch.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Release the driver's (or the front passenger's) * window DOWN switch.
  1. Repeat Step 4 −7 three more times.
  1. Wait 1 second.
  1. Confirm that AUTO UP and AUTO DOWN do not work. If AUTO UP and DOWN work, go back to Step 1 .
  1. Move the driver's (or the front passenger's) * window all the way down by using the driver's (or the front passenger's) * window DOWN switch.
  1. Pull up and hold the driver's (or the front passenger's) * window UP switch until the window reaches the fully closed position, then continue to hold the switch for 1 second.
  1. Confirm that the power window control unit is reset by using the driver's (or the front passenger's) * window AUTO UP and AUTO DOWN functions.
    • If the window still does not work in AUTO, repeat the procedure several times, paying close attention to the 5 second time limit between steps.
    • If the window still does not work, refer to the master switch input test.
    *: With front windows AUTO UP/AUTO DOWN function.
