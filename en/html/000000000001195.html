Radiator Replacement
  1. Drain the engine coolant.
  1. Remove the hood support rod, then use it as shown to prop the hood in the wide-open position.
  1. Remove the bulkhead cover.
  1. Remove the coolant reservoir hose (A) and upper radiator hose (B).
  1. Raise the vehicle on the lift to full height.
  1. Remove the splash shield.
  1. Disconnect the engine coolant temperature (ECT) sensor 2 connector (A), and remove the harness clamp (B).
  1. Remove the lower radiator hose (A).
  1. Remove the automatic transmission fluid (ATF) cooler hoses (B), then plug the hose and line.
  1. Lower the vehicle on the lift.
  1. Remove the millimeter wave radar unit (with adaptive cruise control).
  1. Disconnect the fan motor connectors (A) and hood switch connector (B), then remove the harness clamps (C).
  1. Remove the radiator upper brackets (A), then remove the bulkhead (B).
  1. Pull up the radiator, then remove the radiator fan shroud assembly (A), A/C condenser fan shroud assembly (B), radiator cap (C), ETC sensor 2 (D), and drain plug (E).
  1. Reassemble the radiator with new O-rings (F).
  1. Install the radiator. Make sure the lower cushions (G) are set securely.
  1. Install the front bulkhead (A), then install the radiator upper brackets (B).
  1. Apply body paint to the bulkhead mounting bolts.
  1. Connect the fan motor connectors (A) and hood switch connector (B), then install the harness clamps (C).
  1. Install the millimeter wave radar unit (with adaptive cruise control).
  1. Raise the vehicle on the lift to full height.
  1. Install the lower radiator hose (A) and ATF cooler hoses (B).
  1. Connect the ECT sensor 2 connector (A), and install the harness clamp (B).
  1. Remove the splash shield.
  1. Lower the vehicle on the lift.
  1. Install the coolant reservoir hose (A) and upper radiator hose (B).
  1. Install the bulkhead cover.
  1. Refill the radiator with engine coolant, and bleed the air from the cooling system with the heater valve open.
  1. Do the millimeter wave radar unit aiming (with adaptive cruise control).
