Dashboard Center Trim Removal/Installation
NOTE:
  • Put on gloves to protect your hands.
  • Take care not to scratch the dashboard and related parts.
  • When removing components, use the trim tool or equivalent.
  1. Remove these items:
    • Center dashboard upper panel
    • Dashboard center vent
    • HVAC control unit
    • CLIMATE control unit, with auto A/C
    • Driver's vent panel
    • Passenger's vent panel
    • Shift lever panel
    • Instrument panel
    • Front passenger's airbag
    • For some models: Navigation display unit, with navigation system
    • For some models: Audio unit, without navigation system
  1. Remove the screws.
  1. Gently pull out the dashboard center trim (A) to release the clips, then remove the trim.
  1. Install the trim in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • Check if the clips are damaged or stress-whitened, and if necessary, replace them with new ones.
    • Push the clips into place securely.
