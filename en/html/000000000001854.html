ECM/PCM Replacement
NOTE:
  • Make sure the HDS is loaded with the latest software version.
  • If you are replacing the ECM/PCM after substitute a known-good ECM/PCM, reinstall the original ECM/PCM, then do this procedure.
  1. Connect the HDS to the data link connector (DLC) (A) located under the driver's side of the dashboard.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Make sure the HDS communicates with the ECM/PCM. If it doesn't, go to the DLC circuit troubleshooting.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Jump the SCS line with the HDS.
  1. Remove the under-hood fuse/relay box (A).
  1. Disconnect the harness bracket (B).
  1. Loosen the battery hold down bolt (C) and re-postion the battery away from the ECM/PCM.
  1. Remove the ECM/PCM cover (D).
  1. Remove the bolts (D), then remove the ECM/PCM (E).
  1. Disconnect the ECM/PCM connectors A, B, and C.
    NOTE: ECM/PCM connectors A, B, and C have symbols (A=□, B=△, C=○) embossed on them for identification.
  1. Install the parts in the reverse order of removal.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
    NOTE: DTC P0630 ‘‘VIN Not Programmed or Mismatch'' may be stored because VIN has not been programmed into the ECM/PCM; ignore it, and continue this procedure.
  1. Manually input the VIN to the ECM/PCM with the HDS.
  1. Update the ECM/PCM if it does not have the latest software.
  1. Select IMMOBI SYSTEM with the HDS.
  1. Enter the immobilizer code with the ECM/PCM replacement procedure in the HDS; it allows you to start the engine.
  1. Select PGM-FI system and reset the ECM/PCM with the HDS.
  1. Do the ECM/PCM idle learn procedure.
  1. Do the CKP pattern learn procedure.
