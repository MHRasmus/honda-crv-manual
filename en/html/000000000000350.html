Interior Trim Removal/Installation - Center Pillar Upper/Lower Trim: With Side Curtain Airbags
Center Pillar Upper/Lower Trim
SRS components are located in this area. Review the SRS component locations and the precautions and procedures before doing repairs or service.
NOTE:
  • Put on gloves to protect your hands.
  • Take care not to bend or scratch the trim and panels.
  • When removing components, use the trim tool or equivalent.
  1. Remove these items:
    • Front door sill trim
    • Rear door sill trim
    • Front door opening seal, as needed
    • Rear door opening seal, as needed
  1. Slide the front seat forward fully, and put the rear seat in the normal upright position.
  1. With front seat belt outer lap tensioner: Detach the upper hooks (A) by pulling up the center pillar lower cover (B) back by hand, then pull the cover out to remove it.
  1. Remove the center piller lower trim (A).
    -1.
    Remove the rear seat strap anchor bolt (B).
    -2.
    Pull the upper portion of the trim back to release the upper hooks (C).
    -3.
    Detach the lower clips (D) by pulling the bottom of the trim back by hand.
  1. Remove the front seat belt upper anchor.
  1. Pull the bottom of the center piller upper trim (A) back by hand to release the hooks (B).
  1. Release the upper pin (A) by pulling the top of the center pillar upper trim (B). Pull the trim down to release the upper hooks (C) from the body holes (D).
  1. Install the trim in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • Check if the clips (A) are damage or stress-whitened, and if necessary, replace them with new ones.
    • If the side curtain airbag has deployed, replace the center pillar upper and lower trim and all clips on the trim with new ones.
    • To prevent the side curtain airbags from deploying improperly and possibly causing injury, inspect the trim and replace it if it has any of these types of damage:
      • -
      Any cracks or deformation in the center pillar upper trim (B) and the upper hooks (C)
      -
      Any cracks or deformation in the center pillar lower trim (D), and any breakage in the part (E) fitted with the center pillar upper trim
      -
      Any cracks or stress-whitened areas clips in the seating surface (F)
    • Replace any damaged parts with new ones.
    • Make sure the top of the trim overlaps with the headliner correctly.
    • Make sure the trim hook is installed into the body hole securely.
    • Push the clip and hooks into place securely.
    • Before installing the anchor bolt, make sure there are no twists or kinks in the seat belt.
