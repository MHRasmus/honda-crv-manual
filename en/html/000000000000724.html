DTC Troubleshooting: B1279
DTC B1279:
 DIMMER Switch Circuit Malfunction
  1. Clear the DTCs with the HDS.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF, and then back ON (II).
  1. Turn the headlight switch ON.
  1. Change the dimmer switch from low beam to high beam.
  1. Turn the headlight switch OFF.
  1. Pull and hold the combination light switch lever, and hold the passing switch position.
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.

YES -
Go to Step 8 .
NO -
