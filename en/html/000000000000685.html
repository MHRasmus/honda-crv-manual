DTC Troubleshooting: B1169
DTC B1169:
 Gauge Control Module Lost Communication with the ECM PCM (A/T message)
  1. Clear the DTCs with the HDS.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF, and then back ON (II).
  1. Wait for 6 seconds or more.
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC B1169 indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 5 .
NO -
Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time. Check for loose or poor connections.■
  1. Check for Fuel and Emission system DTCs with the HDS.
    • 2.0 L
    • 2.4 L
Is any DTCs indicated?
YES -
Go to the indicated DTCs, then recheck.
NO -
Go to Step 6 .
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect the gauge control module connector A (36P).
  1. Jump the SCS with the HDS.
  1. Disconnect the ECM/PCM connector A (44P).
  1. Connect the gauge control module connector A (36P) No. 33 and No. 34 terminals and body ground with jumper wires.
  1. Check for continuity between the ECM/PCM connector A (44P) No. 36 and No. 37 terminals and body ground individually.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Update the ECM/PCM if it does not have the latest software for 2.0 L or 2.4 L, or substitute a known-good ECM/PCM for 2.0 L or 2.4 L, and recheck. If the indication goes away, replace the original ECM/PCM. If the DTC is still present, replace the gauge control module. ■
NO -
Repair an open in the wire.■
