Suspension Stroke Sensor Circuit Troubleshooting
NOTE: Before troubleshooting, check the No. 8 (10 A) and No. 22 (7.5 A) fuse in the under-dash fuse/relay box.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Turn the combination (headlight) switch ON.
  1. Raise the vehicle, make sure it is securely supported.
  1. Disconnect the 3P connector (A) from the suspension stroke sensor (B).
Front
Rear
  1. Check for continuity between the suspension stroke sensor 3P connector No. 3 terminal and body ground.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Go to Step 6 .
NO -
Repair an open in the wire or poor ground (G401), if the wire is OK, replace the headlight auto leveling control unit.■
  1. Measure voltage between the suspension stroke sensor 3P connector No. 1 terminal and body ground.
Is there about 5 V?
YES -
Go to Step 7 .
NO -
Check for an open or short in the wire between the adaptive front headlight control unit and the front (or rear) suspension stroke sensor, if OK, replace the front (or rear) suspension stroke sensor.■
  1. Remove the bolt (A) and the suspension stroke sensor from the suspension side.
  1. Reconnect the suspension stroke sensor 3P connector (B).
Front
Rear
  1. At the adaptive front headlight control unit 40P connector side, measure voltage between the No. 16 and No. 23 terminals, and No. 14 and No. 23 terminals with the suspension stroke sensor arm (A) moved.
Is there about 0.5−4.5 V?
YES -
Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time. Check for loose poor connection between the adaptive front headlight control unit and the front (or rear) suspension stroke sensor, if OK, replace the front (or rear) suspension stroke sensor.■
NO -
Check for an open or short in the wire between the adaptive front headlight control unit and the front (or rear) suspension stroke sensor, if OK, replace the front (or rear) suspension stroke sensor.■
