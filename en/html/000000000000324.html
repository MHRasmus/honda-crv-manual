Dashboard Driver's Vent Panel Removal/Installation
NOTE:
  • Put on gloves to protect your hands.
  • When removing components, use the trim tool or equivalent.
  • Take care not to scratch the dashboard and related parts.
  1. Remove the driver's vent panel (A).
    -1.
    Apply protective tape (B) to the dashboard side panel (C) next to the driver's vent panel. Use the trim tool to pry the front left side of the panel partially out.
    -2.
    Pry the front bottom of the panel slightly toward you.
  1. Pull the lower portion of the driver's vent panel (A) slightly toward you to release the clips (B). Detach the clips (C) by pulling the middle and upper portions of the panel toward you by hand.
  1. Disconnect the power mirror switch connectors (D), VSA OFF switch or AFS OFF switch connectors (E) (if wquipped), then if necessary remove the switchs (F) from the panel.
  1. If necessary, remove the screws, then remove the driver's vent (A) from the driver's vent panel (B).
  1. Install the panel in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • Check if the clips are damaged or stress-whitened, and if necessary, replace them with new ones.
    • Push the clips into place securely.
    • Make sure the connectors are plugged in properly.
