Dashboard Shift Lever Panel Removal/Installation
M/T Model
NOTE:
  • When removing components, use the trim tool or equivalent.
  • Take care not to scratch the center console, dashboard, and related parts.
  1. Remove these items:
    • HVAC control unit
    • CLIMATE control unit, with auto A/C
  1. Lower the shift lever boot (A) to release the hooks (B) from the boot, then remove the shift knob (C).
  1. From the HVAC or CLIMATE control unit opening, detach the clips (A) and hooks (B) by pulling the shift center panel (C) up.
  1. Install the panel in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • Check if clips are damaged or stress-whitened, and if necessary, replace them with new ones.
    • Push the clips into place securely.
