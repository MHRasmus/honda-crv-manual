DTC Troubleshooting: 22-01
DTC 22-01:
 Engine Speed Signal (Initial diagnosis/Regular diagnosis)
  1. Start the engine.
  1. Check the ENGINE SPEED in the EPS DATA LIST with the HDS.
Is 0−300 rpm (min −1 ) indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 3 .
NO -
Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time. Check for poor connections or loose terminals at the ECM/PCM and the EPS control unit.■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Short the SCS line with the HDS.
  1. Disconnect ECM/PCM connector A (44P).
  1. Disconnect EPS control unit connector D (20P).
  1. Check for continuity between body ground and EPS control unit connector D (20P) terminal No. 12.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Repair short to body ground in the wire between the EPS control unit and the ECM/PCM.■
NO -
Go to Step 8 .
  1. Check for continuity between ECM/PCM connector A (44P) terminal No. 28 and EPS control unit connector D (20P) terminal No. 12.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Go to Step 9 .
NO -
Repair open in the wire between the EPS control unit and the ECM/PCM.■
  1. Connect ECM/PCM connector A (44P).
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Measure voltage between body ground and EPS control unit connector D (20P) terminal No. 12.
Is there battery voltage?
YES -
Check for loose terminals in the EPS control unit connectors, and repair if necessary. If no poor connections are found, replace the EPS control unit. ■
NO -
Check for poor connections or loose terminals at the ECM/PCM. If necessary, substitute a known-good ECM/PCM and recheck.■
