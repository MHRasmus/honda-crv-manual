DTC 45:
 Cal FL Brake Fluid Pressure Sensor Center Position Error

Troubleshoot the indicated DTC.■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect the 20P connector from the ACC unit.
  1. Check for continuity between the ACC unit 20P connector No. 5 terminal and body ground.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Repair a short to ground in the wire between the ACC unit and the left front brake fluid pressure sensor.■
NO -
Go to Step 9 .
  1. Disconnect the 3P connector from the left front brake fluid pressure sensor.
  1. Connect the left front brake fluid pressure sensor 3P connector No. 2 terminal to body ground with a jumper wire.
  1. Check for continuity between the ACC unit 20P connector No. 5 terminal and body ground.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Go to Step 12 .
NO -
Repair an open in the wire between the ACC unit and the left front brake fluid pressure sensor.■
  1. Remove the jumper wire from the left front brake fluid pressure sensor.
  1. Reconnect all connectors.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Check for voltage between the ACC unit 20P connector No. 3 and No. 5 terminals.
Is there about 5 V?
YES -
Go to Step 16 .
NO -
