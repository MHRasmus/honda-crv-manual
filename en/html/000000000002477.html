Steering Gearbox Removal
Special Tools Required

Ball joint remover, 28 mm
 07MAC-SL00201
Note these items during removal:
  • Using solvent and a brush, wash any oil and dirt off the valve body unit, it's lines, and the end of the steering gearbox. Blow dry with compressed air.
  • Be sure to remove the steering wheel before disconnecting the steering joint. Damage to the cable reel can occur.
  • The illustrations show LHD type, RHD type is same procedure.
  1. Drain the power steering fluid.
  1. Make sure the ignition switch is OFF, then disconnect the negative cable from the battery.
  1. Raise the front of vehicle, and support it with safety stands in the proper locations.
  1. Remove the front wheels.
  1. Remove the steering wheel.
  1. Remove the driver's dashboard undercover.
  1. Remove the steering joint cover (A).
  1. Remove the steering joint bolt (A), and disconnect the steering joint by moving the steering joint (B) toward the column. Hold the lower slide shaft (C) on the column with a piece of wire (D) between the joint yoke (E) on the lower slide shaft to the joint yoke on the upper shaft.
  1. Remove the center guide (A) (if equipped), and discard it. The center guide is for factory assembly only.
  1. Apply vinyl tape to the splines on the pinion shaft (B).
  1. Remove the cotter pin (A) from the 12 mm nut (B), and loosen the nut.
  1. Separate the tie-rod ball joint and knuckle using the ball joint remover.
  1. LHD type: Remove the air cleaner housing.
  1. Remove the front splash shield.
  1. Remove the exhaust pipe A.
  1. Remove the P/S heat shield (A), and move it aside.
  1. Remove the P/S heat shield bracket (B).
  1. Loosen the adjustable hose clamp (A), and disconnect the return hose (B).
  1. Remove the inlet line clamp bolt (C).
  1. LHD type: Remove the return hose clamp bolt (D).
  1. Loosen the 18 mm flare nut (E), and disconnect the inlet line (F).
  1. LHD type: Remove the return hose clamp bolt (A), and open the return hose clamp (B).
  1. LHD type: Remove the pump outlet hose clamp bolt (A), then open the return line clamps (B).
  1. Remove the 10 mm flange bolts (A) and washers (B) from the left side of the steering gearbox.
  1. Remove the 8 mm flange bolts (C), then remove the stabilizer bar bushing holder (D).
  1. Remove the 10 mm flange bolts (A) and washers (B) from the right side of the steering gearbox.
  1. Remove the 8 mm flange bolts (C), then remove the stabilizer bar bushing holder (D).
  1. Remove the 10 mm flange bolts (A), from both of the gearbox brackets (B).
  1. Move the steering gearbox toward the front, and remove the pinion shaft grommet (A) from the top of the valve housing.
  1. Move the steering gearbox to the left side of the vehicle, and rotate it so the pinion shaft (A) points toward the rear of the vehicle.
  1. Carefully move the steering gearbox (A) as an assembly toward the left side of the vehicle until the pinion shaft clears the wheelwell opening.
  1. Remove the steering gearbox through the wheelwell opening on the left side.
  1. After removing the steering gearbox, make sure that no power steering fluid gets on the gearbox mount cushions, gearbox housing, and the surface of the front subframe. Wipe off any spilled fluid at once.
