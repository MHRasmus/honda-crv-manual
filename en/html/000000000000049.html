Brake Master Cylinder Replacement
  • Do not spill brake fluid on the vehicle; it may damage the paint; if brake fluid gets on the paint, wash it off immediately with water.
  • Be careful not to damage or deform the brake lines during removal and installation.
  • To prevent the brake fluid from flowing, plug and cover the hose ends and joints with a shop towel or equivalent.
NOTE: The illustrations show LHD type, RHD type is similar.
NISSIN Type
  1. LHD type: Remove the air cleaner housing cover: R20A1 engine model, K24Z1 engine model.
  1. Remove the reservoir cap, then remove brake fluid from the master cylinder reservoir with a syringe.
  1. Disconnect the brake fluid level switch connector (A).
  1. Remove the reservoir tank mounting bolt (B), then remove the reservoir bracket (C).
  1. Disconnect the brake lines (A) from the master cylinder (B). To prevent spills, cover the hose joints with rags or shop towels.
  1. Remove the master cylinder mounting nuts (C) and washers (D).
  1. Remove the master cylinder from the brake booster (E). Be careful not to bend or damage the brake lines when removing the master cylinder.
  1. Remove the rod seal (F) from the master cylinder.
    NOTE: During installation, set the new rod seal onto the master cylinder with its grooved side (G) toward the master cylinder.
  1. Install the master cylinder in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • Use a new rod seal during reassembly.
    • Coat the inner bore lip and outer circumference of the new rod seal with the silicone grease in the master cylinder set.
    • Check the brake pedal height and free play after installing the master cylinder, and adjust it if necessary.
  1. Bleed the brake system.
  1. Spin the wheels to check for brake drag.
BOSCH Type
  1. LHD type: Remove the air cleaner housing cover.
  1. RHD type M/T model: Remove the clutch reservoir tank (A), and move it aside. Do not disconnect the clutch line from the reservoir tank.
  1. Remove the reservoir cap, then remove brake fluid from the master cylinder reservoir with a syringe.
  1. Disconnect the brake fluid level switch connector (A).
  1. Remove the reservoir tank mounting bolt (B), then remove the reservoir stay (C).
  1. Disconnect the brake lines (A) from the master cylinder (B). To prevent spills, cover the hose joints with rags or shop towels.
  1. Remove the master cylinder mounting nuts (C).
  1. Remove the master cylinder from the brake booster (D). Be careful not to bend or damage the brake lines when removing the master cylinder.
  1. Remove the O-ring (E) from the master cylinder.
    NOTE: During installation, set a new O-ring onto the master cylinder.
  1. Install the master cylinder in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • Be careful not to damage the secondary piston, when remove the master cylinder.
    • Use a new O-ring during reassembly.
    • Use the new master cylinder mounting nuts during reassembly.
    • Check the brake pedal height and free play after installing the master cylinder.
  1. Bleed the brake system.
  1. Spin the wheels to check for brake drag.
