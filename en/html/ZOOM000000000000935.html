Electrical Compass Calibration
NOTE: You should do this procedure in an open area away from buildings, power lines, and other vehicle.
  1. Check the No. 10 (7.5 A) fuse in the under-dash fuse/relay box.
  1. Enter the gauge control module self-diagnostic mode.
  1. Press and hold the SEL/RESET switch for about 3 seconds to enter Self-Diagnostic Customize mode.
  1. Press and hold the SEL/RESET switch unitl the display changes to Calibration mode (about 1 second).
    Then, Calibration will start blinking, and the direction display will change to ‘‘−−''
  1. Drive the vehicle slowly in 2 complete circles.
  1. Press and hold the SEL/RESET switch until the display changes to Reset mode (about 10 seconds).
  1. Complete the zone setting displayed to the map. If the zone is incorrect, turn the dash light brightness controller to the left or right to select the proper zone.
  1. Press the SET/RESET switch to complete the calibration and return to the normal mode.
    NOTE: Step 1 −6 is shown to calibrate. Step 7 −8 is shown to set the zone.
