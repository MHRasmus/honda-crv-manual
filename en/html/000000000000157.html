Rear Seat Belt Replacement
NOTE: Check the front seat belts for damage, and replace them if necessary. Be careful not to damage them during removal and installation.
Rear Seat Belt
  1. Fold the rear seat up.
  1. Pull the carpet back as necessary, and remove the lower anchor bolt (A).
  1. Remove the cargo area side trim panel.
  1. Remove the upper anchor cap (A), and remove the upper anchor bolt (B).
  1. Disconnect the seat belt tensioner connector (A). Remove the upper retractor mounting bolt (B), and the lower retractor mounting bolt (C), then remove the rear seat belt (D) and retractor (E).
  1. Install the seat belt in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • Apply medium strength type liquid thread lock to the anchor bolts before reinstallation.
    • Tighten the bolts by hand first, then tighten to specification with a torque wrench.
    • Check that the retractor locking mechanism functions as described.
    • Assemble the washer, collar, and bushing on the upper anchor bolt as shown.
    • If the seat belt tensioner has been deployed, replace the front seat belt protector with a new one.
    • Before installing the anchor bolts, make sure there are no twists or kinks in the seat belt.
    • Make sure the seat belt tensioner connector is properly plugged in.

Rear Center Seat Belt
  1. Remove the headliner.
  1. Remove the retractor mounting bolt (A), retractor mounting bolts (B), then remove the rear seat belt (C) and retractor (D).
  1. Install the center seat belt and retractor in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • Apply medium strength type liquid thread lock to the anchor bolts before reinstallation.
    • Tighten the bolts by hand first, then tighten to specification with a torque wrench.
    • Check that the retractor locking mechanism functions as described.
Rear Center Seat Belt Detachable Anchor and Right Rear Seat Belt Buckle
  1. Remove the rear seat.
  1. Remove the under cover from the rear seat.
  1. Remove the center anchor bolts (A), from under the seat cushion.
  1. Pull the rear center seat belt detachable anchor (A) and right rear seat belt buckle (B) out.
  1. Install the anchor and buckle in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • Apply medium strength type liquid thread lock to the anchor bolts before reinstallation.
    • Tighten the bolts by hand first, then tighten to specification with a torque wrench.
    • Assemble the washer on the upper anchor bolt as shown.
    • Make sure there are no twists or kinks in the anchor, center belt, and seat belt buckle.
Center anchor bolt installation
Rear Center Seat Belt Buckle and Left Rear Seat Belt Buckle
  1. Fold the rear seat up (one side)
  1. Remove the outer cover, right rear seat, and left rear seat.
  1. Remove the center anchor bolts (A).
  1. Pull the rear center seat belt detachable anchor (A) and right rear seat belt buckle (left rear seat) out.
  1. Install the anchor and buckle in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • Apply medium strength type liquid thread lock to the anchor bolts before reinstallation.
    • Tighten the bolts by hand first, then tighten to specification with a torque wrench.
    • Assemble the washer on the upper anchor bolt as shown.
    • Make sure there are no twists or kinks in the anchor, center belt, and seat belt buckle.
