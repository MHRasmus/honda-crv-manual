DTC Troubleshooting: B2146
DTC B2146:
 Right Adaptive Front Lighting Gearbox Motor Swing Error
  1. Clear the DTCs with the HDS.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF and then back ON (II).
  1. Turn the headlight switch ON.
  1. Turn the steering wheel 90 degrees or more from the center position to left or right position.
  1. While maintaining communication with the HDS, test-drive in a straight line for 30 m (98.4 feet) at 25 km/h (16 mph) or more.
  1. Check the AFS indicator in the gauge control module.
  1. Select the BODY ELECTRICAL menu from the HDS.
Does the AFS indicator come on?
YES -
Go to Step 11 .
NO -
Go to Step 7 .
  1. Select the AFS from the HDS, and enter the DATA LIST.
  1. Check the STEERING ANGLE LEARN information in the DATA LIST.
Is the steering angle learn finished?
YES -
Go to Step 10 .
NO -
Go to Step 6 , and repeat the procedure.
  1. Test-drive at 10 km/h (7 mph) for more than 3 minutes.
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC B2146 indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 12 .
NO -
Faulty adaptive front lighting control unit, replace it and do the adaptive front lighting learning procedure. ■
  1. Select the FUNCTION TEST from the HDS.
  1. Select the ADAPTIVE FRONT LIGHTING, and check the headlights operation with the HEADLIGHT-LEFT/RIGHT.
    NOTE: The left headlight should move 20 degrees to left side and return, and the right headlight should move 20 degrees to right side and return.
Do the headlights operate normally?
YES -
Go to Step 14 .
NO -
Go to Step 31 .
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Remove the driver's dashboard lower cover.
  1. Disconnect the adaptive front lighting control unit 40P connector.
  1. Disconnect the right adaptive front lighting gearbox 8P connector.
  1. Check for continuity between body ground and adaptive front lighting control unit 40P connector terminals No. 31, No. 32, and No. 33 individually.
Is there continuity?
YES -

NO -
