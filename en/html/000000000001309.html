Fuel Fill Pipe Removal/Installation
  1. Relieve the fuel pressure.
  1. Drain the fuel tank.
  1. Remove the fuel fill cap.
  1. Remove the fuel fill pipe cover (A) and fuel fill cap adapter (B).
  1. Remove the rear wheel and wheel sensors; with ABS model, with VSA model.
  1. Support the rear defferential (4WD model) and the rear axle beam with a transmission jack.
  1. Remove the flange bolts from the bottom of the rear damper/springs.
  1. Remove the rear defferential (4WD model) and the rear suspension subframe mounting bolts.
  1. Lower the transmission jack (A) and the rear differential or axle beam.
    NOTE: Be careful not to damage the connecting parts.
  1. Disconnect the fuel fill tube (A) and fuel vapor hose (B).
  1. Remove the fuel fill pipe (C).
  1. Install the parts in the reverse order of removal.
