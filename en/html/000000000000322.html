Center Console Removal/Installation
NOTE:
  • When removing components, use the trim tool or equivalent
  • Take care not to scratch the center console, dashboard, and related parts.
  1. Slide both from seats to the rearward to access the front console trim.
  1. From the front portion of the center console, detach the clips (A) by pulling out the front console trim (B)with a trim tool.
  1. Gently pull out the front console trim (A) to detach the clips (B, C) from the console, and remove the trim.
  1. Disconnect the subharness connector (D), and detach the harness clip.
  1. Remove the bolts.
  1. Open the shutter (A), then remove the console box mat (B) and bolts.
  1. Foot parking brake type: Lift up the center console (C), and remove it.
  1. Parking brake lever type: Wrap the parking brake lever grip (A) with a shop towel, then lift up on the console (B), and remove it as shown.
    NOTE: Take care not to scratch or damage the parking brake lever grip.
  1. Install the console in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • Check if the clips are damaged or stress-whitened, if necessary, replace them with new ones.
    • Push the clips into place securely.
    • Make sure all connectors are plugged in properly.
