Audio Remote Switch Test
  1. Remove the driver's airbag assembly.
  1. Remove the 12P connector (A) from the audio remote switch (B).
  1. Measure the resistance between the No. 3 and No. 7 terminals in each switch position according to the table.
    Position
    Resistance
    OFF
    About 10 kΩ
    MODE
    About 4.0 kΩ
    CH (+)
    About 2.0 kΩ
    CH (−)
    About 780 Ω
    ▲
    (VOL.UP)
    About 370 Ω
    ▼
    (VOL.DOWN)
    About 100 Ω

  1. If the resistance is not as specified, replace the audio remote switch.
  1. Use a diode tester between the terminals in each switch position according to the table.
  1. If the diode test is bad, replace the switch.
