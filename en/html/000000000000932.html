A/C Signal Circuit Troubleshooting
NOTE:
  • Use this troubleshooting procedure if the A/C compressor, the A/C condenser fan, and radiator fan are inoperative.
  • Before doing symptom troubleshooting, check for and resolve any powertrain DTCs.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Check if the blower motor operates at all speeds.
Does the blower motor operate at all speeds?
YES -
Go to Step 3 .
NO -
Repair the problem in the blower motor circuit.■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect the climate control unit 36P connector.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Measure the voltage between the climate control unit 36P connector terminal No. 6 and body ground.
Is there voltage?
YES -
Go to Step 13 .
NO -
Go to Step 7 .
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect the A/C compressor clutch 3P connector.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Measure the voltage between the A/C compressor clutch 3P connector terminal No. 3 (R20A1 and R20A2 engines) or No. 1 (K24Z1 engine) and body ground.
Is there battery voltage?
YES -
Go to Step 11 .
NO -
Repair open in the wire between the A/C compressor and the MICU. If the wire is OK, substitute a known-good MICU and recheck. If the symptom goes away, replace the original MICU .■
  1. Test the A/C compressor thermal protector for R20A1 and R20A2 engines or K24Z1 engine.
Is the A/C compressor thermal protector OK?
YES -
Go to Step 12 .
NO -
Replace the A/C compressor thermal protector for R20A1 and R20A2 engines or K24Z1 engine. ■
R20A1 and R20A2 engines
K24Z1 engine
  1. Check for continuity between the climate control unit 36P connector terminal No. 6 and the A/C compressor clutch 3P connector terminal No. 1 (R20A1 and R20A2 engines) or No. 2 (K24Z1 engine).
Is there continuity?
YES -
Check for loose wires or poor connections at the climate control unit 36P connector and at the A/C compressor clutch 3P connector.■
NO -
Repair open in the wire between the climate control unit and A/C compressor.■

K24Z1 engine
  1. Measure the evaporator temperature sensor resistance between the climate control unit 36P connector terminals No. 9 and No. 22.
Is resistance less than 24 kΩ?
YES -
Check for loose wires or poor connections at the climate control unit 36P connector. If the connections are good, substitute a known-good climate control unit and recheck. If the symptom goes away, replace the original climate control unit.■
