DTC Troubleshooting: 51
DTC 51:
 Motor Locked
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Clear the DTC with the HDS.
  1. Test-drive the vehicle. Drive the vehicle at 15 km/h (10 mph) or more, with the engine speed at 2,500 rpm (min −1 ) or more.
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC 51 indicated?
YES -
Replace the VSA modulator-control unit. ■
NO -
Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time. Check for a loose connection at G202.■
