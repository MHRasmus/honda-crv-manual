NOTE:
  • Coat all parts with MTF during reassembly.
  • Replace the tapered roller bearing and the bearing outer race as a set if either part is replaced.
  1. Remove the tapered roller bearing outer race (A) from transfer housing (B) by heating the cover to almost 212 °F (100 °C) using a heat gun (C). Do not heat the cover over 212 °F (100 °C).
Bearing Outer Race Locations
  1. Install the 52 mm tapered roller bearing outer race using the driver (A), attachment (B).
  1. Install the 62 mm tapered roller bearing outer race and 50 mm tapered roller bearing outer race using the installer nut (A), installer shaft (B), bearing installer attachment (C) and (D).
