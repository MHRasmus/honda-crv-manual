Engine Oil Pan Removal
NOTE: Make sure to inspect the oil pump chain, when removing the oil pan.
  1. If the engine is out of the vehicle, go to Step 18 .
  1. Remove the drive belt.
  1. Remove the engine undercover.
  1. Drain the engine oil
  1. Remove a bolt securing the P/S fluid line bracket, and unclamp the P/S fluid line clamps on the front subframe.
  1. Disconnect the headlight front leveling sensor connector (with HID model).
  1. Remove the bolts securing the left steering gearbox mounting bracket .
  1. Remove the bolts securing the right steering gearbox mounting brackets .
  1. Separate the right side knuckle from the lower arms.
  1. Loosen the bands (A) securing the turbocharger outlet hose and intercooler intake hose, and remove the intercooler intake hose (B), then remove the intercooler intake pipe mounting bolts (C).
  1. Remove the intercooler intake pipe bracket (D), then remove the intercooler intake pipe (E).
  1. Support the oil pan with a jack.
  1. Remove the lower torque rod .
  1. Remove a jack.
  1. Support the rear side of the subframe with a jack. Remove the subframe mounting bolts (A) on both sides, then loosen the four subframe mounting bolts (B) on the rear side of subframe so they are about 15 mm (0.6 in.) from the mounting surface. Remove a jack.
  1. Support the front side of the subframe with a jack. Remove the two subframe mounting bolts. Lower the subframe so it is about 50 mm (2.0 in.) from the body.
  1. Remove the A/C compressor without disconnecting A/C line .
  1. Remove the alternator.
  1. Disconnect the engine oil pressure switch connector.
  1. Remove the dipstick.
  1. Remove the dipstick tube.
  1. Remove the A/C compressor bracket .
  1. Remove the lower torque rod bracket.
  1. Remove the oil level sensor covers (A), then disconnect the oil level sensor connector (B).
  1. Remove the two bolts securing the harness holder (C).
  1. Remove the transmission mounting bolts.
  1. Remove the bolts securing the oil pan.
  1. Using a flat blade screwdriver, separate the oil pan from the lower block in the places shown.
  1. Inspect the oil pump chain.
