DTC Troubleshooting: 83
DTC 83:
 ECM/PCM Communication Error
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC 86 indicated with DTC 83 at the same time?
YES -
Do the DTC 86 troubleshooting. ■
NO -
Go to Step 3 .
  1. Check for the fuel and emissions systems DTCs with the HDS: R20A1 and R20A2 engine models, K24Z1 engine model.
Are any DTCs indicated?
YES -
Do the applicable troubleshooting for the ECM/PCM.■
NO -
Go to Step 4 .
  1. Clear the DTC with the HDS.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF, then turn it ON (II) again.
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC 83 indicated?
YES -
Check for loose terminals in the VSA modulator-control unit 46P connector. Check for a loose connection at G202. If necessary, substitute a known-good VSA modulator-control unit and retest.■
NO -
Intermittent failure, system is OK at this time. Check for loose terminals between the ECM/PCM connector A (44P), and the VSA modulator-control unit 46P connector. Check for a loose connection at G202. Refer to intermittent failures troubleshooting. ■
