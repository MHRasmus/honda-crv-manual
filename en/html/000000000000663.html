DTC Troubleshooting: B2142
DTC B2142:
 Adaptive Front Lighting Control Unit Sub CPU Communication Error
NOTE: Before troubleshooting, check the No. 8 (10 A) fuse and No. 22 (7.5 A) fuse in the under-dash fuse/relay box.
  1. Clear the DTCs with the HDS.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF, and then back ON (II).
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC B2142 indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 4 .
NO -
Intermittent failure, the adaptive front lighting system is OK at this time. Check for loose or poor connections at the adaptive front lighting control unit 40P connector.
  1. Measure voltage between body ground and adaptive front lighting control unit 40P connector terminals No.21 and No.22 individually.
Is there battery voltage?
YES -
Faulty adaptive front lighting control unit, replace it and do the adaptive front lighting control unit learning procedure. ■
NO -
Repair an open in the wire.■
