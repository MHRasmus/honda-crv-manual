DTC Troubleshooting: 68
DTC 68:
 Brake Pedal Position Switch Stuck ON (Hardware)
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC 86 indicated with DTC 68 at the same time?
YES -
Do the DTC 86 troubleshooting. ■
NO -
Go to Step 3 .
  1. Check the BRAKE SWITCH in the VSA DATA LIST with the HDS while moving the brake pedal.
Does it indicate ON when the pedal is pressed, and OFF when the pedal is released?
YES -
Go to Step 4 .
NO -
Go to Step 7 .
  1. Clear the DTC with the HDS.
  1. Test-drive the vehicle at 15 km/h (10 mph) for 60 seconds or more.
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC 68 indicated?
YES -
Check for loose terminals in the VSA modulator-control unit 46P connector. Check for a loose connection at G202. If necessary, substitute a know-good VSA modulator-control unit and retest.■
NO -
Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time. Check for loose terminals at the VSA modulator-control unit 46P connector. Check for a loose connection at G202. Refer to intermittent failures troubleshooting. ■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect the brake pedal position switch 4P connector.
  1. Do the brake pedal position switch test.
Is the switch OK?
YES -
Check the diesel engine management DTCs, and troubleshoot the indicated DTC.■
NO -
Replace the brake pedal position switch. ■
