AFS Gearbox Test
  1. Disconnect the adaptive front lighting gearbox 8P connector (A) from the headlight.
  1. Measure resistance between the adaptive front lighting gearbox 8P connector terminals No. 4 and No. 7, and between terminals No. 3 and No. 8. There should be about 7.8−9.4 Ω.
  1. If the resistance is not as specified, replace the adaptive front lighting gearbox.
  1. After replacement, do the adaptive front lighting control unit learning procedure.
