DTC Troubleshooting: 85-61, 85-62
DTC 85-61:
 No Signal From OPDS Unit
DTC 85-62:
 Non-Stipulated Response Data
NOTE: Before doing this troubleshooting procedure, review SRS Precautions and Procedures.
  1. Make sure nothing is on the front passenger's seat.
  1. Erase the DTC memory.
  1. Read the DTC.
Is DTC 85-61 or 85-62 indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 4 .
NO -
Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time. Go to Troubleshooting Intermittent Failures. If another DTC is indicated, go to the DTC Troubleshooting Index.
  1. Check the connection between the front passenger's seat wire harness 18P connector and the OPDS unit.
Is the connection OK?
YES -
Go to Step 5 .
NO -
Repair the poor connection and retest. If DTC 85-61 or 85-62 is still present, go to Step 5 .
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Check the No. 9 (7.5 A) fuse in the under-dash fuse/relay box.
Is the fuse OK?
YES -
Go to Step 7 .
NO -
Replace the fuse, then turn the ignition switch ON (II). If the fuse blows again, check for a short in the No. 9 (7.5 A) fuse circuit (dashboard wire harness, floor wire harness, front passenger's seat subharness or front passenger's seat wire harness).■
  1. Disconnect the front passenger's seat wire harness 18P connector (A) from the OPDS unit.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Check for voltage between the No. 1 terminal of the front passenger's seat wire harness 18P connector D and body ground. There should be battery voltage.
Is there battery voltage?
YES -
Go to Step 10 .
NO -
Open in the dashboard wire harness, floor wire harness, front passenger's seat subharness, or front passenger's seat wire harness; replace the faulty harness.■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Check resistance between the No. 12 terminal of front passenger's seat wire harness 18P connector D and body ground. There should be 0−1.0 Ω.
Is the resistance as specified?
YES -
Go to Step 12 .
NO -
Open in the front passenger's seat wire harness, front passenger's seat subharness harness or floor wire harness; replace the faulty harness.■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF. Disconnect the negative cable from the battery, and wait for 3 minutes.
  1. Disconnect SRS unit connector A (28P) from the SRS unit.
  1. Check resistance between the No. 14 terminal of SRS unit connector A (28P) and body ground. There should be an open circuit (ohmmeter reads OL) or at least 1 MΩ.
Is the resistance as specified?
YES -
Go to Step 15
NO -
Short to ground in the dashboard wire harness floor wire harness, front passenger's seat subharness or front passenger's seat wire harness; replace the faulty harness.■
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II), and measure the voltage between the No. 14 terminal of SRS unit connector A (28P) and body ground. There should be 0.5 V or less.
Is the voltage as specified?
YES -
Go to Step 16
NO -
Short to power in the dashboard wire harness, floor wire harness, front passenger's seat subharness or front passenger's seat wire harness; replace the faulty harness.■
  1. Check resistance between the No. 14 terminal of SRS unit connector A (28P) and the No. 7 terminal of the front passenger's seat wire harness 18P connector D. There should be 0−1.0 Ω.
Is the resistance as specified?
YES -
Faulty OPDS unit or SRS unit; replace the OPDS unit. If the problem is still present, replace the SRS unit. ■
NO -
Open in the dashboard wire harness, floor wire harness, front passenger's seat subharness or front passenger's seat wire harness; replace the faulty harness.■
