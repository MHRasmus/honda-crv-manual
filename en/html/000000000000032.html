ABS Symptom Troubleshooting - ABS indicator and brake system indicator do not go off at same time
ABS indicator and brake system indicator do not go off at same time
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Check the No. 4 (7.5 A) fuse in the under-dash fuse/relay box.
Is the fuse blown?
YES -
Install a new No. 4 (7.5 A) fuse, and recheck.■
NO -
Reinstall the checked fuse, then go to Step 3 .
  1. Disconnect the ABS modulator-control unit 26P connector.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Measure the voltage between ABS modulator-control unit 26P connector terminal No. 20 and body ground.
Is there battery voltage?
YES -
Go to Step 6 .
NO -
Repair open in the wire between the No. 4 (7.5 A) fuse in the under-dash fuse/relay box and the ABS modulator-control unit.■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Check for continuity between ABS modulator-control unit 26P connector terminal No. 23 and body ground.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Go to Step 8 .
NO -
Repair open in the wire the ABS modulator-control unit and body ground (G202).■
  1. Disconnect gauge control module connector B (36P).
  1. Check for continuity between the appropriate ABS modulator-control unit 26P connector terminals and gauge control module connector B (36P) terminals respectively (see table).

YES -
