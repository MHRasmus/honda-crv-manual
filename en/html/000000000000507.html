Front Seat-back Cover/Pad Replacement
NOTE:
  • Do not repair any tears or frayed spots of the seat-back cover. If necessary, replace the seat-back cover.
  • Take care not to tear the seams or damage the seat covers.
  • On the passenger's seat, do not touch the OPDS sensor in the seat-back pad, and keep it away from oil.
  • Put on gloves to protect your hands.
  • When prying with a flat-tip screwdriver, wrap it with protective tape to prevent damage.
  • Do not repair any tear or frayed spots of the seat cover.
  • When removing components, use the trim tool or equivalent.
  • LHD is shown; RHD is similar.
  1. Remove the front seat.
  1. Remove these items:
    • Front seat under tray (for some models)
    • Subwoofer (for some models)
    • Front seat armrest
    • Head restraint
  1. From under the seat cushion, release the hook (A) from the seat cushion frame spring (B). Pull the seat-back cover back (C).
Driver's seat
Passenger's seat
  1. Release the slits (A) in the seat cushion cover (B) from the seat cushion frame spring (C).
Driver's seat
Passenger's seat
  1. Pull the seat cushion cover back. Detach the connector(s), then pull it to out side of the seat.
    • Driver's seat:
      • -
      For some model: Side airbag connector (A)
      -
      For some model: Seat cushion heater connector (B)
    • Passenger's seat:
      • -
      For some model: Side airbag connector (A)
      -
      For some model: Seat cushion heater connector (B)
      -
      Seat belt reminder switch connector (C)
  1. Remove the wire ties (D). Driver's side: Pull the side airbag harness (E) out through slit (F) in the cover. Passenger's side: Remove the fastener (G), and release the side airbag harness from the seat cushion cover, and pull the cover back.
Driver's seat
Passenger's seat
  1. Using the appropriate trim tool, pry out the clips (A) to release the lower portion of the back cover.
  1. Detach the hooks (B) by puling the bottom of the back cover (C) back, then gently pull down the cover to release the hooks (D) from the seat frame, and pull out the cover to release the hooks (E).
  1. Release the fasteners (A) and hooks (B), then pull the seat-back cover (C) back.
Manual seat
8-way power seat
  1. With side airbag: Pull the side airbag harness (A) out through the harness holes (B) in the seat-back cover and seat frame. Disconnect the seat-back heater harness connector (C) if equipped. Driver's is shown; passenger's seat is similar.
  1. With side airbag: Remove the side airbag.
Manual seat
8-way power seat
  1. Release the hook strips (A), then loosen the seat-back cover (B). Driver's seat is shown; passenger's seat is similar.
Manual seat
8-way power seat
  1. With side airbag: Turn over the reinforcing cloth (A), then release the hooks (B) from the side airbag module holder (C). Passenger's seat is shown; driver's seat is symmetrical.
  1. With side airbag passenger's seat: Disconnect the OPDS sensor connector (A) and OPDS subharness connector (B) from the OPDS unit (C), and pull them in through the hole in the seat frame. Detach the harness clips (D), and remove the wire harness.
  1. Pinch the tabs on the ends of the head restraint guides (A), and remove them from the seat-back.
  1. Remove the seat-back cover/pad (A) from the seat (B).
  1. Pull back the edge of the seat-back cover all the way around, and release the clips (A), then remove the seat-back cover.
  1. Install the cover in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • Reinitialize the OPDS unit.
    • To prevent wrinkles when installing a seat-back cover, make sure the material is stretched evenly over the pad before securing the clips, hooks, and hook strips.
    • Replace any clips you removed with new ones (A).
    • Before installing the side airbag (B), make sure the reinforcing cloth (C) is fixed on the seat-back frame (D) securely.
    • Make sure the side airbag harness and OPDS subharness (passenger's seat) are routed properly.
