NOTE: Coat all parts with MTF during reassembly.
  1. Remove the tapered roller bearing (A) from the transfer driven gear (B) using a commercially available bearing separator (C), an adapter (D), and a press.
  1. Install the new tapered roller bearing (A) on the transfer driven gear (B) using the driver, 40 mm I.D. (C), attachment (D) and a press.
