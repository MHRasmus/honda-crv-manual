M/T Assembly Removal
NOTE: Use fender covers to avoid damaging painted surfaces.
  1. Make sure you have the anti-theft codes for the radio and navigation system, then write down the frequencies for the radio presets.
  1. Disconnect the negative (−) cable first, then the positive (+) cable from the battery. Remove the battery.
  1. Remove the air cleaner housing.
  1. Remove the harness clips (A) and the battery base (B).
  1. Remove the resonator chamber.
  1. Slide the tab (A) and pull up the lock tab (B) is shown, then remove the hose clamp (C).
  1. Remove the clutch line bracket (A) and battery base bracket (B).
  1. Disconnect the countershaft speed sensor connector (A), back-up light switch connector (B) and reverse lockout solenoid connector (C).
  1. Carefully remove the slave cylinder without bending the clutch line. Do not press the clutch pedal once the slave cylinder has been removed.
  1. Remove the air cleaner housing stay A.
  1. Disconnect the cables (A) from the top of the transmission housing, then remove the cable bracket (B). Carefully remove both cables and the bracket together to avoid bending the cables.
  1. Disconnect the harness clip (A), then remove the transmission upper mounting bolts (B) and the harness bracket (C).
  1. Remove the engine cover.
  1. Disconnect the harness holder clips (A), then remove the bolt (B) securing the fuel feed line, and remove two bolts (C) securing the fuel return line.
  1. Attach the engine hanger (D) to the threaded holes in the cylinder head.
    NOTE: The engine hanger should avoid and attach the fuel hose and the air hose.
  1. Attach the chain hoist (E) to the engine.
  1. Remove the transmission mount bolt (A), nuts (B) and the ground cable bolt (C).
  1. Remove the stud bolts (A).
  1. Raise the vehicle, and make sure it is securely supported.
  1. Remove the engine undercover.
  1. Drain the transmission fluid. Reinstall the drain bolt using a new washer.
  1. Remove the propeller shaft.
  1. Separate the knuckle from the lower arm.
  1. Remove the steering mount bracket bolts (A) and the stabilizer bush holder (B) from the front subframe (C).
  1. Remove the steering line bracket (A) and the clips (B) from the front suspension subframe (C).
  1. Disconnect the front suspension stroke sensor connector (A) (with HID).
  1. Remove the lower torque rod mounting bolt and nut.
  1. Support the subframe with a 4 x 4 x 40 in. Piece of wood and a jack as shown.
  1. Disconnect the exhaust pipe mounting rubber (A) from the subframe (B), then remove the subframe.
    NOTE: Suspend the steering gearbox with an appropriate size wire.
  1. Pry the left side driveshaft inboard joint (A) from the differential case with a prybar.
  1. Drive the right side driveshaft inboard joint (A) off of the intermediate shaft (B) with a drift and hammer.
  1. Remove the intermediate shaft (A).
  1. Remove the intercooler pipe bracket (A), then remove the intercooler pipe (B).
  1. Place the transmission jack under the transmission, and remove the lower transmission mounting bolts.
  1. Pull the transmission away from the engine until the transmission mainshaft clears the clutch pressure plate, then lower the transmission on the transmission jack.
  1. Remove the boot (A), release fork (B), and release bearing (C) from the transmission (D).
