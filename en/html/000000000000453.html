Defogger Test
  1. Check for continuity between the power mirror actuator 13P connector No. 2 and No. 7 terminals (or 6P connector No. 1 and No. 2 terminals).
    There should be continuity. If there is no continuity, remove the mirror holder, and check for an open in the wire between the mirror actuator 13P (or 6P) connector and the mirror defogger terminals (A). If the wire harness is OK, replace the mirror holder.
