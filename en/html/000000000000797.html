DTC Troubleshooting: 25, 26
DTC 25:
 Yaw Rate Sensor (Sensor Unit)
DTC 26:
 Lateral Acceleration Sensor (Sensor Unit)
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Substitute a known-good yaw rate-lateral acceleration sensor.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Clear the DTC with the HDS.
  1. Test-drive the vehicle. Drive the vehicle at 30 km/h (19 mph) or more, and go a distance of 300 m (985 ft) or more.
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC 25 or 26 indicated?
YES -
Check for loose terminals in the VSA modulator-control unit 46P connector. Check for a loose connection at G202. If necessary, substitute a known-good VSA modurator-control unit and retest.■
NO -
Replace the original yaw rate-lateral acceleration sensor .■
