Dashboard Driver's Lower Cover Removal/Installation
NOTE:
  • Put on gloves to protect your hands.
  • When removing components, use the trim tool or equivalent.
  • Take care not to scratch the dashboard and related parts.
  1. Adjust the steering column upward.
  1. Remove the driver's dashboard undercover.
  1. Gently pull out on the lower edge of the dashboard lower cover (A) to detach the lower clips.
  1. Detach the clips, and release the hooks (A) by pulling the driver's dashboard lower cover (B) back.
  1. If necessary, remove the screws, then remove the driver's lower pocket (A).
  1. Install the cover in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • Check if the clips are damaged or stress-whitened, and if necessary, replace them with new ones.
    • Push the clips and hooks into place securely.
