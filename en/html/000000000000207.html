Audio System Symptom Troubleshooting - No sound is heard from speaker(s) (display is normal) (with navigation)
No sound is heard from speaker(s) (display is normal) (with navigation)
NOTE:
  • Set the fader and balance positions to the center.
  • Before doing symptom troubleshooting, do the power switch will not turn ON troubleshooting.
  1. Check that the volume button is not set to the min level.
Is it at MIN level?
YES -
Raise the volume level, and recheck the function.■
NO -
Go to Step 2 .
  1. Check the NAVIGATION VOLUME MUTE COMMAND.
Is the NAVIGATION MUTE MODE set?
YES -
Cancel the NAVIGATION MUTE MODE by pressing the voice command BACK button, and recheck the function.■
NO -
Go to Step 3 .
  1. Check to see if there is a specific speaker(s) that has no sound.
Is there a specific speaker(s) with no sound?
YES -
Go to Step 4 .
NO -
Go to Step 7 .
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Check the speaker(s) with no sound for any damage.
Is there any damage?
YES -
Substitute a known-good speaker and recheck.■
NO -
Go to Step 6 .
  1. Remove the speaker(s) with no sound, and disconnect its connector.
  1. Measure the resistance between the No. 1 and No. 2 terminals of the speaker connector.
Is there about 4 Ω?
YES -
Go to Step 8 .
NO -
Faulty speaker(s).■
  1. Check the speaker 2P or 6P connector for a loose or poor connection.
Reconnect the speaker 2P or 6P connector and recheck the symptom; is there still no sound?
YES -
  • Subwoofer: Go to Step 13 .
  • Except subwoofer: Go to Step 9 .
NO -
Operation is normal at this time.■

Is there about 4 Ω?
YES -
Go to Step 11 .
NO -
If there is 1 MΩ or more, repair open in the wire pairs between the navigation unit and speaker.■
  1. Disconnect the speaker 2P connector.
  1. Check navigation unit connector A (17P) terminals No. 2, 3, 7, 8, 11, 12, 15, and 16 individually for continuity to body ground.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Repair short to body ground in the wire between the navigation unit and speaker.■
NO -
Substitute a known-good navigation unit and recheck. If the symptom/ indication goes away, replace the original navigation unit. ■
  1. Disconnect subwoofer 6P connector.
  1. Measure the voltage between subwoofer 6P connector No. 6 terminal and body ground.
Is there battery voltage?
YES -
Go to Step 15 .
NO -
Repair open in the wire between fuse No. 17 (15 A) under-hood fuse/relay box and subwoofer 6P connector No. 6 terminal.■
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II), and push the PWR button on the navigation unit.
  1. Measure the voltage between subwoofer 6P connector No. 5 terminal and body ground.
Is there battery voltage?
YES -
Go to Step 17 .
NO -
Repair open in the wire between the subwoofer 6P connector No. 5 terminal and navigation unit connector B (22P) No. 20 terminal.■
  1. Reconnect the subwoofer 6P connector.
  1. Measure the voltage between subwoofer 6P connector No. 2 terminal and body ground.
Is there less than 0.1 V?
YES -
Go to Step 19 .
NO -
Repair open in the wire between subwoofer 6P connector No. 2 terminal and body ground (G502). ■
