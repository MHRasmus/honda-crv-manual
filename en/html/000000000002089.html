Radiator and A/C Condenser Fan, Motor, and Shroud Removal and Installation
Removal
  1. Remove the hood support rod, then use it as shown to prop the hood in the wide-open position.
  1. Remove the front grille cover.
  1. Remove the millimeter wave radar unit (with adaptive cruise control).
  1. Remove the water bypass hose from the clamp (A), and remove the bolt (B) securing the A/C line.
  1. Disconnect the fan motor connectors (A) and hood switch connector (B), then remove the harness clamps (C).
  1. Remove the radiator upper brackets (A), then remove the bulkhead (B).
  1. Disconnect the engine coolant temperature (ECT) sensor 2 connector (A), then remove the A/C compressor clutch connector (B) and harness clamps (C) from the shroud.
  1. Remove the condenser fan shroud assembly (A), then remove the radiator fan shroud assembly (B) from condenser fan shroud side.
  1. Disassemble the fan shrouds.
Installation
  1. Assemble the fan shrouds.
  1. Install the radiator fan shroud assembly (A), then install the condenser fan shroud assembly (B).
  1. Connect the engine coolant temperature (ECT) sensor 2 connector (A), then install the A/C compressor clutch connector (B) and harness clamps (C) to the shroud.
  1. Install the bulkhead (A), then install the radiator upper brackets (B).
  1. Apply body paint to the bulkhead mounting bolts.
  1. Connect the fan motor connectors (A) and hood switch connector (B), then install the harness clamps (C).
  1. Install the bolt (A) securing the A/C line, and install the water bypass hose to the clamp (B).
  1. Install the millimeter wave radar unit (with adaptive cruise control).
  1. Install the front grille cover.
  1. Do the millimeter wave radar unit aiming (with adaptive cruise control).
