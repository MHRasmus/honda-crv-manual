Front Seat Removal/Installation
SRS components are located in this area. Review the SRS component locations and the precautions and procedures before performing repairs or service.
NOTE:
  • Put on gloves to protect your hands.
  • When prying with a flat-tip screwdriver, wrap it with protective tape to prevent damage.
  • Take care not to scratch the body or tear the seat covers.
  • When removing components, use the trim tool or equivalent.
  1. Passenger's seat: Remove the front seat under tray (for some models).
  1. Tilt the steering wheel all the way up, and telescope it all the way in.
  1. Slide the front seat forward fully, then remove the rear inner seat track end covers (A) and rear outer seat track end covers (B) from the rear of both seat tracks.
Manual seat
8-way power seat
  1. 8-way power seat: Slide the front seat rearward fully, then remove the front inner seat track end cover (A) and front outer seat track end cover (B) from the from of driver's seat tracks.
  1. Slide the seat about half way to the rear so you can access the seat mounting bolts. If you remove the 8-way power seat, fully raise the seat.
8-way power seat
  1. Remove the bolts.
  1. Slid the front seat rearward fully, and remove the bolts.
  1. If equipped, make sure you have the anti-theft code for the audio or the navigation system, then write down the audio presets.
  1. Disconnect the negative cable from the battery, and wait at least 3 minutes before beginning work.
  1. Wihtout front seat belt outer lap tensioner: Carefully pry up on the bottom edge (A) of the anchor cover (B) with the trim tool to release the hooks, and remove the cover.
  1. Without front seat belt outer lap tensioner: Remove the lower anchor bolt (A).
  1. Lift up the front seat, then disconnect the side airbag connector (A) (for some model), and seat subharness connector (B).
    On passenger's seat, disconnect the woofer connector (C) (for some models).
  1. With the help of an assistant, carefully remove the front seat through the front door opening.

Driver's seat
  1. Install the seat in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • Apply medium strength type liquid thread lock to the seat mounting bolts before reinstallation.
    • Tighten the seat mounting bolts to the specified torque in the sequence shown. Slide the seat all the way back and tighten (1) and (2), then slide it forward and tighten (3) and (4). The driver's seat is shown the passenger's seat is similar.
    • Before installing the anchor bolts, make sure there are no twists or kinks in the seat belt.
    • Make sure each connector is plugged in properly.
    • Tighten the bolts by hand first, then tighten them to specification with a torque wrench.
    • If equipped with a side airbag, do the following works:
      • -
      Reconnect the negative cable to the battery.
      -
      Enter the anti-theft code for the audio and navigation system (if equipped), then enter the audio presets.
      -
      Set the clock (without navigation system).
      -
      Check for any DTCs that may have been set during repairs, and clear them.
