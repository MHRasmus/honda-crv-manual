Crankshaft Pulley Removal and Installation
Special Tools Required

Handle
 07JAB-001020B

Crankshaft pulley holder
 070AB-RJA0100

Socket wrench, 19 mm
 07JAA-001020A
Removal
  1. Remove the front wheels.
  1. Remove the splash shield.
  1. Remove the drive belt.
  1. Hold the pulley with holder handle (A) and holder attachment (B).
  1. Remove the bolt with a 19 mm socket (C) and breaker bar, then remove the crankshaft pulley.
Installation
  1. Clean the crankshaft pulley (A), crankshaft (B), bolt (C), and washer (D). Lubricate as shown.
    ○:
    Clean
    •:
    Lubricate

  1. Install the crankshaft pulley, and hold the pulley with holder handle (A) and holder attachment (B).
  1. Tighten the bolt to 49 N·m (5.0 kgf·m, 36 lbf·ft) with a torque wrench and 19 mm socket (C). Do not use an impact wrench.
  1. Tighten the pulley bolt an additional 90 °.
  1. Install the drive belt.
  1. Install the splash shield.
  1. Install the front wheels.
