Front Stabilizer Bar Replacement
  1. Raise the front of the vehicle, and support it with safety stands in the proper locations.
  1. Remove the front wheels.
  1. Disconnect the steering joint from the pinion shaft.
  1. Disconnect both stabilizer links from the stabilizer bar.
  1. Support the front subframe (A) with the wood piece (B) and a jack.
  1. Loosen the front subframe front mounting bolt (A) on the right and left of the vehicle so they are about 30 mm (1.18 in.) from the mounting surface.
  1. Support the front subframe securely, then loosen the 14 mm special tools (A) so they are about 30 mm (1.18 in.) from the mounting surface.
  1. Lower the jack supporting the front subframe with the special tool slowly until the front subframe has dropped about 30 mm (1.18 in.).
  1. Remove the flange bolts (A) and bushing holders (B), then remove the bushings (C).
    NOTE: During installation align the paint marks (D) on the stabilizer bar with the sides of the bushings.
  1. Install the stabilizer bar in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • Note the right and left direction of the stabilizer bar.
    • Align the paint marks on the stabilizer bar with the sides of the bushings.
    • Note the fore/aft direction of the bushing holders.
    • Raise the front subframe up with the jack and special tool until it contacts the body frame, then tighten the mounting bolts to the specified torque.
    • Refer to Stabilizer Link Removal/Installation to connect the stabilizer bar to the links.
    • Check the front wheel alignment, and adjust it if necessary.
