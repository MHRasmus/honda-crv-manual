EVAP Canister Replacement
  1. Lit the vehicle, and support it with jackstands.
  1. Remove the cover (A).
KE, KG, KS models
Except KE, KG, KS models
  1. Remove the EVAP canister baffle cover (A).
  1. Disconnect the hoses (A).
  1. Remove the bolts (B) and the EVAP canister (C).
  1. Install the parts in the reverse order of removal.
