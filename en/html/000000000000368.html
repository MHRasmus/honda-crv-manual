License Plate Light Replacement
  1. Slide the license plate light (A) to the right, and remove the license plate light.
    License Plate Light:
    5 W

  1. Disconnect the 2P connector (B) from the license plate light.
  1. Separate the license plate light and the housing (C), then remove the bulb (D).
  1. Install the light in the reverse order of removal.
