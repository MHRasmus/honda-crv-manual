DTC Troubleshooting: 45-1x - SIEMENS SRS Unit
DTC 45-1x (‘‘x'' can be 0 thru 9 or A thru F):
No Signal from the Left Side Impact Sensor (second) (SIEMENS SRS unit)
Special Tools Required

SRS inflator simulator
 07SAZ-TB4011A

SRS simulator lead L
 070AZ-SNA0300
NOTE: Before doing this troubleshooting procedure, review SRS Precautions and Procedures.
  1. Erase the DTC memory.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II), and wait for 10 seconds.
  1. Read the DTC.
Is DTC 45-11 indicated?
YES -
Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time.
NO -
Go to Step 4 .
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Check the damage of a floor wire harness or side wire harness between the SRS unit and left side impact sensor (first), and left side impact sensor (first) and left side impact sensor (second).
Are the damages OK?
YES -
Go to Step 6 .
NO -
Replace the floor wire harness or side wire harness
  1. Disconnect the negative cable from the battery, and wait for 3 minutes.
  1. Substitute a known-good left side impact sensor (second).
  1. Erase the DTC memory.
  1. Reconnect the negative cable to the battery.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II), and wait for 10 seconds.
  1. Read the DTC.
Is DTC 45-1x indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 12 .
NO -
Faulty left side impact sensor (second); replace the left side impact sensor (second). ■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect the negative cable from the battery, and wait for 3 minutes.
  1. Disconnect both seat belt tensioner 4P connectors and both seat belt buckle tensioner 2P connectors.
  1. Disconnect SRS unit connector B (28P) from the SRS unit.
  1. Disconnect the floor wire harness 4P connector (A) from the left side impact sensor (first).
  1. Check resistance between the No. 1 terminal of left side impact sensor (first) 4P connector and body ground. There should be an open circuit (ohmmeter reads OL) or at least 1MΩ.
Is the resistance as specified?
YES -
Go to Step 18 .
NO -
Short to ground in the floor wire harness or side wire harness; replace the faulty harness.■
  1. Check resistance between the No. 1 and No. 4 terminal of the left side impact sensor (first) 4P connector. There should be an open circuit (ohmmeter reads OL) or at least 1MΩ.
Is the resistance as specified?
YES -
Go to Step 19 .
NO -
Short in the floor wire harness; replace the floor wire harness.■
  1. Connect the SRS inflator simulator (jumper connector) and the black lead (A) of simulator lead L to the left side impact sensor (second) 4P connector (B).
  1. Check resistance between the No. 1 and No. 4 terminal of the left side impact sensor (first) 4P connector. There should be 0−1.0 Ω.
Is the resistance as specified?
YES -
Reconnect the left side impact sensor (first) 4P connector. If DTCs indicated, replace the SRS unit. ■
NO -
Open in the floor wire harness; replace the floor wire harness.■
