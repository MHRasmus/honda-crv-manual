Radiator Replacement
  1. Drain the engine coolant.
  1. Remove the hood support rod, then use it as shown to prop the hood in the wide-open position
  1. Remove the front grille cover.
  1. Remove the millimeter wave radar unit (with adaptive cruise control).
  1. Remove the water bypass hose from the clamp (A), and remove the bolt (B) securing the A/C line.
  1. Disconnect the fan motor connectors (A) and hood switch connector (B), then remove the harness clamps (C).
  1. Remove the radiator upper brackets (A), then remove the bulkhead (B).
  1. Disconnect the engine coolant temperature (ECT) sensor 2 connector (A), then remove the A/C compressor clutch connector (B) and harness clamps (C) from the shroud.
  1. Clean any dirt off the quick connectors (A), radiator, upper radiator hose, and lower radiator hose.
  1. Pull out the lock (B) by hand, then wiggle the quick connector loose, and remove it from the radiator. Do not use any tools to remove the quick connector.
  1. Remove the water bypass hoses (C).
  1. Pull up the radiator, then remove the radiator fan shroud assembly (A), A/C condenser fan shroud assembly (B), ETC sensor 2 (C), and drain plug (D).
  1. Reassemble the radiator with new O-rings (E).
  1. Install the radiator. Make sure the lower cushions (F) are set securely.
  1. Check the quick connector (A) and set ring (B) for cracks or damage. If the connector and/or set ring are cracked or damaged, replace the connector.
  1. Make sure the set ring is in place inside the quick connector. If the set ring is displaced or not properly seated in the connector, replace the quick connector.
  1. Replace the O-ring (C) in the quick connector.
  1. Check the lock (D). If the lock is damaged or deformed, replace it. When installing the new lock on the connector, press it straight down along the groove.
  1. Clean the connecting surfaces of the radiator (E), then apply clean engine coolant around the connecting surfaces.
  1. Push down the lock (A), then push the quick connector (B) onto the radiator until you hear it click.
  1. Install the water bypass hoses (C).
  1. Connect the ECT sensor 2 connector (A), then install the A/C compressor clutch connector (B) and harness clamps (C) to the shroud.
  1. Install the bulkhead (A), then install the radiator upper brackets (B).
  1. Apply body paint to the bulkhead mounting bolts.
  1. Connect the fan motor connectors (A) and hood switch connector (B), then install the harness clamps (C).
  1. Install the bolt (A) securing the A/C line, and install the water bypass hose to the clamp (B).
  1. Install the front grille cover.
  1. Refill the expansion tank with engine coolant, and bleed air from the cooling system with the heater valve open.
