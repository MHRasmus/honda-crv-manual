Steering Rack Guide Removal/Installation
NOTE: During removal/installation, do not allow dust, dirt, or other foreign materials to enter the steering gearbox.
  1. Remove the steering gearbox.
  1. Loosen the locknut (A), then remove the rack guide screw (B), the spring (C), the conical springs (D), the plane washer (E), and rack guide (F).
  1. Replace the O-ring (G) from the rack guide. Wipe the grease off the sliding surface of the rack guide.
  1. Apply multipurpose grease to the new O-ring, then install it to the rack guide.
  1. Apply multipurpose grease to the sliding surface and the circumference of the rack guide, and install it onto the gearbox housing. Wipe the grease off the threaded section of the gearbox housing.
  1. Install the plane washer onto the gearbox housing.
  1. Install the conical spring onto the gearbox housing.
    NOTE: Be careful for the directions of installation as shown.
  1. Install the spring onto the gearbox housing.
  1. Remove the old sealant from the rack guide screw and apply new sealant (Seal End No. 22) to the middle of the threads. Loosely install the rack guide screw on the steering gearbox.
  1. Loosely install the locknut.
  1. Adjust the rack guide screw. After adjusting, check that the rack moves smoothly by sliding the rack right and left.
