Engine Oil Filter Feed Pipe Replacement
  1. Remove the oil filter base.
  1. Remove the oil filter holder.
  1. Install the two 20 x 1.5 mm nuts (A) onto the new oil filter feed pipe. Hold one nut with a wrench, then use a second wrench to tighten the other nut.
  1. Tighten the oil filter feed pipe to the block to 49 N·m (5.0 kgf·m, 36 lbf·ft), then remove the nuts from the oil filter feed pipe.
  1. Install the oil filter base.
