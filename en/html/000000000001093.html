DTC Troubleshooting: P0108 (12-8)
DTC P0108:
 Manifold Absolute Pressure (MAP) Sensor Circuit High Voltage Input
NOTE: Before you troubleshoot, record all freeze data and any on-board snapshot, and review General Troubleshooting Information.
  1. Clear the DTC with the HDS.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF, then turn it ON (II) again.
  1. Check whether DTC P0108 is indicated in the DTCs/Freeze Data in PGM-FI Mode Menu with the HDS.
Is DTC P0108 indicated in the PGM-FI system?
YES -
Troubleshoot for DTC P0108 in the PGM-FI System; R20A1 and R20A2 engine models, K24Z1 engine model. ■
NO -
Go to Step 4 .
  1. Check whether DTC P0108 is indicated in the DTCs/Freeze Data in A/T Mode Menu with the HDS.
Is DTC P0108 indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 5 .
NO -
Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time.■
  1. Update the PCM if it does not have the latest software, or substitute a known-good PCM.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF, then turn it ON (II).
  1. Check for DTC(s) in the DTCs/Freeze Data in A/T Mode Menu with the HDS.
Is DTC P0108 indicated?
YES -
Check for poor connections and loose terminals at the MAP sensor and the PCM. If the PCM was updated, substitute a known-good PCM, then recheck. If the PCM was substituted, go to Step 1 .
NO -
If DTC P0108 is not indicated, or if any other DTCs are indicated, go to Step 8 .
  1. Monitor the OBD STATUS for P0108 in the DTCs/Freeze Data in A/T Mode Menu for a pass/fail.
Does the HDS indicate PASSED?
YES -
If the PCM was updated, troubleshooting is complete. If the PCM was substituted, replace the original PCM; R20A1 and R20A2 engine models, K24Z1 engine model. If any other DTCs were indicated on Step 7 , go to the indicated DTC's troubleshooting.■
NO -
If the HDS indicates FAILED, check for poor connections and loose terminals at the MAP sensor and the PCM. If the PCM was updated, substitute a known-good PCM, then recheck. If the PCM was substituted, go to Step 1 . If the HDS indicates NOT COMPLETED, keep idling until a result comes on.
