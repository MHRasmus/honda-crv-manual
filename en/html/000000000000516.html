Rear Seat Armrest Cover Replacement
NOTE:
  • When removing components, use the trim tools or equivalent.
  • Take care not to tear the seams or damage the seat covers.
  1. Remove the armrest.
  1. Remove the beverage holder (A).
    -1.
    Using a trim tool, push on the bottom ribs (B) of the left side hooks (C) to pull up the holder, then release the hooks from the wire (D).
    -2.
    Release the right side hooks (E) from the wire, then remove the holder.
  1. Release the hook (A), and pull back the edge of the armrest cover (B) all the way around, and remove it.
  1. Install the cover in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • To prevent wrinkles, make sure the material is stretched evenly over the armrest before securing the hooks.
    • Make sure the right side hooks are installed securely to the wire, then push down on the beverage holder and install the left side hooks into the wire securely.
