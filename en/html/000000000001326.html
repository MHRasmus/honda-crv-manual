NOTE: Make sure the HDS and the DLC cable of the HDS is not defective.

  1. Connect the HDS to the DLC.
    NOTE: Make sure the HDS is properly connected to the DLC.

    • ABS modulator-control unit 26P connector (with ABS system)
    • VSA modulator-control unit 46P connector (with VSA system)
    • EPS control unit connector D (20P) (with electrical power steering)
    • Immobilizer-keyless control unit 7P connector
    • ACC unit 20P connector (with driver support system)
    • Audio unit 17P connector
    • Under-dash fuse/relay box (Q) (16P) connector
Does continuity go away when one of the above components is disconnected?
YES -
Replace the part that caused an open when it was disconnected.■
NO -
Repair short in the wire between the DLC (K-line) and the ABS modulator-control unit (with ABS system), the VSA modulator-control unit (with VSA system), the SRS unit, the EPS control unit (with electrical power steering), the ACC unit (with driver support system), the immobilizer-keyless control unit, the audio unit, or the under-dash fuse/relay box.■
  1. Connect DLC terminal No. 7 to body ground with a jumper wire.
  1. Check for continuity between body ground and these connector terminals:

    • Crankshaft position (CKP) sensor 3P connector
    • Electronic throttle control system (ETCS) control relay
Does continuity go away when one of the above components is disconnected?
YES -
Replace the component that made the short to body ground go away when disconnected. If the item is the ECM/PCM, update the ECM/PCM if it does not have the latest software, or substitute a known-good ECM/PCM, then recheck. If the symptom/indication goes away with a known-good ECM/PCM, replace the original ECM/PCM. Also replace the No. 19 FI MAIN (15 A) fuse.■
