DTC Troubleshooting: 7
DTC indicator 7:
 HVAC Control Unit Internal Error
NOTE: Check the battery condition and the charging system for R20A1 and R20A2 engines or K24Z1 engine.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF and then ON (II).
  1. Do the self-diagnostic with the HVAC control unit.
  1. Check for DTCs.
Is DTC 7 indicated?
YES -
The HVAC control unit is faulty, replace the HVAC control unit. ■
NO -
Intermittent failure, the HVAC control unit is OK at this time.■
