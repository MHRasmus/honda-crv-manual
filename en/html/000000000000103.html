Blower Unit Removal/Installation
  1. Remove the glove box.
  1. Disconnect the connector (A), then remove the connector clip (B), the wire harness clips (C), the bolts, and the glove box frame (D).
  1. Cut the plastic cross brace (A) in the glove box opening with diagonal cutters in the area shown.Retain plastic cross brace to be reinstalled later.
  1. Remove the wire harness clips (A), the self-tapping screws, and the passenger's heater duct (B).
  1. Disconnect the connector (A) from the blower motor. Remove the wire harness clip (B).
  1. Disconnect the connector (A) from the recirculation control motor. Remove the self-tapping screws, the bolt, the mounting nuts, and the blower unit (B).
  1. Install the unit in the reverse order of removal. Make sure that there is no air leakage.
