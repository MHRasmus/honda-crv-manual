DTC Troubleshooting: B1028
DTC B1028:
 Rear Window Wiper Motor (As) Signal Error
  1. Clear the DTCs with the HDS.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF, and then back ON (II).
  1. Operate the rear window wiper for 15 seconds or more, then turn the rear window wiper switch OFF.
Does the rear window wiper stop normally?
YES -
Go to Step 4 .
NO -
Go to Step 13 .
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC B1028 indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 5 .
NO -
Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time. Check for loose or poor connections.■
  1. Do the rear window wiper motor test.
Is the rear window wiper motor OK?
YES -
Go to Step 6 .
NO -
Replace the rear window wiper motor.■
  1. Make sure the ignition switch is OFF, and disconnect the under-dash fuse/relay box connector S (20P).
  1. Disconnect the rear window wiper motor 4P connector.
  1. Connect the rear window wiper motor 4P connector No. 2 terminal and body ground with a jumper wire.
  1. Check for continuity between the under-dash fuse/relay box connector S (20P) No. 2 terminal and body ground.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Go to Step 10 .
NO -
Repair an open in the wire.■
  1. Remove the jumper wire from the rear window wiper motor 4P connector.
  1. Check for continuity between the under-dash fuse/relay box connector S (20P) No. 2 terminal and body ground.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Repair a short to ground in the wire.■
NO -
Go to Step 12 .
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. With the rear window wiper motor 4P connector still disconnected, measure voltage between the rear window wiper motor 4P connector No. 2 terminal and body ground.
Is there voltage?
YES -
Repair a short to power in the wire.■
NO -
Faulty MICU ; replace the under-dash fuse/relay box. ■
  1. Check the No. 8 (7.5 A) fuse in the under-dash fuse/relay box.
Is the fuse OK?
YES -
Go to Step 15 .
NO -
Replace the blown fuse and recheck.■
  1. Do the rear window wiper motor test.
Is the rear window wiper motor OK?
YES -
Go to Step 16 .
NO -

  1. Do the rear window wiper motor relay test.
Is the rear window wiper motor relay OK?
YES -
Go to Step 17 .
NO -
Replace the rear window wiper motor relay.■
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Measure voltage between the under-hood fuse/relay box connector D (8P) No. 7 terminal and body ground, and the under-hood fuse/relay box connector F (20P) No. 15 terminal and body ground.
Is there battery voltage?
YES -
Go to Step 19 .
NO -
Repair an open in the wire.■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect the under-hood fuse/relay box connector E (10P).
  1. Disconnect the rear window wiper motor 4P connector.
  1. Connect the rear window wiper motor 4P connector No. 1 terminal and body ground with a jumper wire.
  1. Check for continuity between the under-hood fuse/relay box connector E (10P) No. 2 terminal and body ground.
Is there continuity?
YES -
