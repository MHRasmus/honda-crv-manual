Intermediate Shaft Removal
Procedure for K24Z1 engine model
  1. Drain the automatic transmission fluid. Reinstall the drain plug with a new washer.
  1. Remove the right driveshaft.
  1. Remove the flange bolt (A) and two dowel bolts (B).
  1. Remove the intermediate shaft (A) from the differential. Hold the intermediate shaft horizontal until it is clear of the differential to prevent damaging the differential oil seal (B).
