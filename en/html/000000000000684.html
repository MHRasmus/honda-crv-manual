DTC Troubleshooting: B1166
DTC B1166:
 Gauge Control Module Lost Communication with the Adaptive Front Lighting Control Unit (AFS Message)
NOTE: Before troubleshooting, check the No. 8 (10 A) fuse and No. 22 (7.5 A) fuse in the under-dash fuse/relay box.
  1. Clear the DTCs with the HDS.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF, and then back ON (II).
  1. Wait for 6 seconds or more.
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC B1166 indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 5 .
NO -
Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time. Check for loose or poor connections.■
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.
Are DTCs B1155, B1156, B1157, B1159, B1160, and B1188 all indicated at the same time?
YES -
Replace the gauge control module .■
NO -
Go to Step 6 .
  1. From the BODY ELECTRICAL menu, select UNIT INFORMATION, and then select CONNECTED UNIT.
  1. Check the condition of the automatic lighting/rain sensor from the CONNECTED UNIT list.
Is NOT AVAILABLE indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 8 .
NO -
Replace the automatic lighting/rain sensor.■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect the adaptive front lighting control unit 40P connector.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Measure voltage between the adaptive front lighting control unit 40P connector No. 21 and No. 22 terminals and body ground individually.
Is there battery voltage?
YES -
Go to Step 12 .
NO -
Repair an open in the wire.■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Check for continuity between the adaptive front lighting control unit 40P connector No. 24 and No. 25 terminals and body ground individually.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Go to Step 14 .
NO -
Repair an open in the wire or poor ground (G401).■
  1. Disconnect the under-dash fuse/relay box connector Q (16P).
  1. Check for continuity between the adaptive front lighting control unit 40P connector No. 15 terminal and the under-dash fuse/relay box connector Q (16P) No. 6 terminal.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Replace the adaptive front lighting control unit. ■
NO -
