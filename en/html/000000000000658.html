DTC Troubleshooting: B2136
DTC B2136:
 Left Adaptive Front Lighting Gearbox Motor Circuit Short
  1. Clear the DTCs with the HDS.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF, and then back ON (II).
  1. Enter the BODY ELECTRICAL, and then select the ADAPTIVE FRONT LIGHTING with the HDS.
  1. Enter the FUNCTIONAL TESTS, and then select the HEADLIGHT-LEFT/RIGHT.
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTCs B2136 indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 6 .
NO -
Intermittent failure, the adaptive front lighting system is OK at this time. Check for loose or poor connections, between left adaptive front lighting gearbox motor 8P connector and the adaptive front lighting control unit 40P connector.■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect the 8P connector from the left adaptive front lighting gearbox motor.
  1. Clear the DTCs with the HDS.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF, and then back ON (II).
  1. Enter the BODY ELECTRICAL, and then select the ADAPTIVE FRONT LIGHTING with the HDS.
  1. Enter the FUNCTIONAL TESTS, and then select the HEADLIGHT-LEFT/RIGHT.
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC B2136 not indicates and B2145 indicates?
YES -
Faulty left adaptive front lighting gearbox motor, replace it.■
NO -
Go to Step 13 .
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect the 40P connector from the adaptive front lighting control unit.
  1. Check for continuity between body ground and left adaptive front lighting control unit 40P connector terminal No. 7, No. 9, No. 27 and No. 29 individually.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Repair a short in the wire.■
NO -
Faulty adaptive front lighting control unit, replace it and do the adaptive front lighting control unit learning procedure. ■
