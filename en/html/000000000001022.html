A/T Shift Solenoid Valve, Shift Solenoid Harness Replacement
  1. Get the customer's radio (and navigation) anti-theft codes if equipped, and write down the radio presets.
  1. Raise the vehicle up on a lift, or apply the parking brake, block the rear wheels, and raise the front of the vehicle. Make sure it is securely supported.
  1. Remove the splash shield.
  1. Remove the drain plug (A), and drain the transmission fluid (ATF).
  1. Reinstall the drain plug with the new sealing washer (B).
  1. Disconnect the negative terminal from the battery, then disconnect the positive terminal.
  1. Remove the battery hold-down bracket, and remove the battery cover, battery, and battery tray.
  1. Remove the battery base.
  1. Remove the air cleaner housing and intake air duct.
  1. For K24Z1 engine model: Remove the ATF dipstick, and remove the bolts securing the ATF cooler line (A). Remove the harness clamp (B), and remove the ATF dipstick guide tube (C).
  1. For R20A1 and R20A2 engine models: Remove the ATF dipstick, remove the harness clamp (A) from its bracket (B), and remove the bracket. Remove the bolts securing the ATF cooler line (C). Remove the harness clamp (D), and remove the ATF dipstick guide tube (E).
  1. Remove the shift solenoid valve cover (A), dowel pins (B), and gasket (C).
  1. Disconnect the shift solenoid valve connectors.
    • If replacing shift solenoid valve(s), go to Step 14 .
    • If replacing the shift solenoid harness, remove the shift solenoid harness connector (A), and replace it. Install a new O-ring (B) on the shift solenoid harness connector, and install the connector in the transmission housing, then go to Step 19 .
  1. Remove the mounting bolts, then remove the solenoid valves.
  1. Install new O-rings (two O-rings per solenoid valve) (F) on the reused solenoid valves.
    NOTE: A new solenoid valve comes with new O-rings. If you install a new solenoid valve, use the O-rings provided on it.
  1. Install shift solenoid valve C (brown connector), shift solenoid valve D (black connector), and shift solenoid valve E (black connector) by holding the shift solenoid valve body; make sure the mounting bracket contacts the servo body.
    NOTE: Do not hold the solenoid valve by the connector when installing the solenoid valve. Be sure to hold the solenoid valve body.
  1. Install shift solenoid valve A (brown connector) by holding the shift solenoid valve body; make sure the mounting bracket contacts the bracket of shift solenoid valve D.
    NOTE: Do not install shift solenoid valve A before installing shift solenoid valve D. If shift solenoid valve A is installed before installing shift solenoid valve D, it may damage the hydraulic control system.
  1. Install shift solenoid valve B (brown connector) by holding the shift solenoid valve body; make sure the mounting bracket contacts the bracket of shift solenoid valve E.
    NOTE: Do not install shift solenoid valve B before installing shift solenoid valve E. If shift solenoid valve B is installed before installing shift solenoid valve E, it may damage the hydraulic control system.
  1. Connect the RED harness terminal to shift solenoid valve E, the GRN harness terminal to shift solenoid valve C, the ORN harness terminal to shift solenoid valve B, the BLU harness terminal to shift solenoid valve A, and the YEL, WHT, WHT harness terminal to shift solenoid valve D.
  1. Install the shift solenoid valve cover, dowel pins, and a new gasket.
  1. Install the new O-ring on the ATF dipstick guide tube, and install the guide tube then secure it with the bolts.
  1. Check the connector for rust, dirt, or oil, then connect the connector securely. Install the harness clamp in its clamp bracket on the ATF dipstick guide tube.
  1. Secure the ATF cooler line with the bolts.
  1. For R20A1 and R20A2 engine models: Install the harness clamp bracket, and install the harness clamp on its bracket.
  1. Refill the transmission with ATF.
  1. Install the intake air duct and air cleaner housing.
  1. Install the battery base.
  1. Install the battery tray, battery, battery cover, and battery hold-down bracket, then connect battery terminals.
  1. Install the splash shield.
  1. Enter the radio (and navigation) anti-theft codes if equipped, then enter the radio presets, and set the clock.
  1. Do the power window control unit reset procedure.
