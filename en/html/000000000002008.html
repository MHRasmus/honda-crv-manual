ATF Cooler Hose Replacement
K24Z1 Engine Model
  1. Slide the ATF cooler/filter hose (A) on the ATF filter (B) until the hose end is 5−7 mm (0.20−0.28 in.) (C) away from the filter housing, and secure the hose with the clip (D) at 10−12 mm (0.39−0.47 in.) (E) from the filter housing. Install the cooler hose (F) on the other side of the ATF filter in the same manner.
  1. Slide the ATF cooler/filter hose (G) over the ATF cooler line (H) until the hose end contacts the bulge (I), and secure the hoses with the clip (J) at 2−4 mm (0.08−0.16 in.) (K) from the hose end.
  1. Slide the ATF cooler hoses (L) over the ATF lines (M) until the hose ends contact the bulge (N), and secure the hoses with the clip (O) at 6−8 mm (0.24−0.31 in.) (P) from the hose end.
