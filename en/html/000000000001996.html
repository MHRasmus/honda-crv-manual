Transfer Installation
NOTE:
  • While installing the transfer assembly on the transmission, do not allow dust or other foreign particles to enter the transmission.
  • Be careful not to damage the clutch housing with transfer driven gear.
  1. Install a new O-ring (A) on the transfer (B), then install the dowel pin (C) on the transmission.
  1. Apply MTF to the transfer driven gear and transmission contact area, then install the transfer to the transmission.
  1. Install the propeller shaft (A) to the transfer assembly (B) by aligning the reference marks (C).
  1. Refill the transmission with MTF.
  1. Start the engine, and run it to normal operating temperature (the radiator fan comes on). Turn the engine off, and check fluid level.
