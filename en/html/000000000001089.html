Center Console Disassembly/Reassembly
Parking brake lever type
NOTE:
  • Put on gloves to protect your hands.
  • When removing components, use the trim tool or equivalent.
  • Take care not to scratch the center console, and related parts.
  1. Remove the center console.
  1. Remove the center console upper panel (A).
    -1.
    From the front console trim opening, gentry pull the front portion of the panel up, and remove the clips (B).
    -2.
    Pull the rear portion of the panel up, remove the clips (C), and release the hooks (D).
    -3.
    For some models: Disconnect the accessory power socket connector (E) and the auxiliary jack assembly connector (F).
  1. From the center console upper panel (A), disconnect the seat heater switch connectors (B) (for some models), and detach the harness clips (C), then remove the console subhaness (D).
  1. Remove the screws, and remove the beverage holder (E).
  1. Remove the screws, and remove the pocket or seat heater switch panel (A) from the center console upper panel (B).
  1. From the bottom of the center console (A), remove the screws.
  1. Gently pull out the center console rear cover (A), to detach the clips.
  1. Remove the screws and clips, then remove the console box (A).
  1. Remove the screws (A, B), then remove the bracket (C) and jack panel (D).
  1. Reassembly the center console in the reverse order of disassembly, and note these items:
    • Check if the clips are damaged or stress-whitened, if necessary, replace them with new ones.
    • Push the clips into place securely.
    • Make sure all connectors are plugged in properly.
