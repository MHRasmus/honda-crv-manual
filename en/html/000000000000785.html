DTC Troubleshooting: 76-61, 76-62
DTC 76-61, 76-62:
 Non-Stipulated Response Data
NOTE: Before doing this troubleshooting procedure, review SRS Precautions and Procedures.
  1. Erase the DTC memory.
  1. Read the DTC.
Is DTC 76-61 or 76-62 indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 3 .
NO -
Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time. Go to Troubleshooting Intermittent Failures. If another DTC is indicated, go to the DTC Troubleshooting Index.
  1. Check the No. 9 (7.5 A) fuse in the under-dash fuse/relay box and No. 5 (30 A) fuse and No. 16 (30 A) fuse in the under-hood fuse/relay box.
Are the fuses OK?
YES -
Go to Step 4 .
NO -
Replace the faulty fuse. If the problem is still present, short to ground in the under-dash fuse/relay box or under-hood fuse/relay box circuit.■
  1. Disconnect the floor wire harness 14P connector B from the e-pretensioner unit.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Check the voltage between the No. 8 terminal of e-pretensioner unit connector B (14P) and body ground. There should be battery voltage.
Is there battery voltage?
YES -
Go to Step 7 .
NO -
Open between the No. 9 (7.5 A) fuse in the under-dash fuse/relay box and the No. 8 terminal of e-pretensioner unit connector B (14P).■
  1. Check the voltage between the No. 12 terminal of e-pretensioner unit connector B (14P) and body ground. There should be battery voltage.
Is the battery voltage?
YES -
Go to Step 8 .
NO -
Open between No. 5 (30 A) fuse in the under-hood fuse/relay box and the No. 12 terminal of e-pretensioner unit connector B (14P).■
  1. Disconnect the floor wire harness 5P connector A from the e-pretensioner unit.
  1. Check voltage between the No. 2 terminal of e-pretensioner unit connector A (5P) and body ground. There should be battery voltage.
Is there battery voltage?
YES -
Go to Step 10 .
NO -
Open between No. 16 (30 A) fuse in the under-hood fuse/relay box and the No. 2 terminal of e-pretensioner unit connector A (5P).■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Check resistance between the No. 10 terminal of e-pretensioner unit connector B (14P) and body ground, and between the No. 11 terminal of e-pretensioner unit connector B (14P) and body ground. There should be 0−1.0 Ω.
Are the resistances as specified?
YES -
Go to Step 12 .
NO -
Open between the No. 10 terminal of e-pretensioner unit connector B (14P) and body ground, or between the No. 11 terminal of e-pretensioner unit connector B (14P) and body ground or poor connection G551. ■
  1. Check resistance between the No. 5 terminals of e-pretensioner unit connector A (5P) and body ground. There should be 0−1.0 Ω.
Is the resistance as specified?
YES -
Go to Step 13 .
NO -
Open between the No. 5 terminal of e-pretensioner unit connector A (5P) and body ground or poor connection G551. ■
  1. Disconnect the negative cable from the battery, and wait for 3 minutes.
  1. Disconnect SRS unit connect A (28P) from the SRS unit.
  1. Check resistance between the No. 14 terminal of SRS unit connector A (28P) and body ground. There should be an open circuit (ohmmeter reads OL) or at least 1 MΩ.
Is the resistance as specified?
YES -
Go to Step 16 .
NO -
Short to ground between the No. 14 terminal of SRS unit connector A (28P) and the No. 6 terminal of e-pretensioner unit connector B (14P).
  1. Check resistance between the No. 14 terminal of SRS unit connector A (28P) and the No. 6 terminal of e-pretensioner unit connector B (14P). There should be 0−1.0 Ω.
Is the resistance as specified?
YES -
Faulty e-pretensioner unit; replace the e-pretensioner unit. ■
NO -
Open between the No. 14 terminal of SRS unit connector A (28P) and the No. 6 terminal of e-pretensioner unit connector B (14P).■
