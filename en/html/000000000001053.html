A/T Shift Lever Installation
  1. Cover around the opening of the dashboard with tape to prevent damage to the dashboard.
  1. Install the shift lever assembly (A) in the dashboard.
    NOTE: The illustration shows the LHD model; RHD model is similar.
  1. Connect the shift lock solenoid connector (B) and D3 switch/park pin switch/A/T gear position indicator panel light connector (C).
  1. Install the shift cable on the shift lever, and adjust the shift cable.
