Front Driveshaft Removal
  1. Raise the vehicle on a lift, and remove the front wheels.
  1. Lift up the locking tab (A) on the spindle nut (B), then remove the nut.
  1. If the left driveshaft is to be removed, drain the transmission fluid. Reinstall the drain plug using a new washer.
  1. Remove the front suspension stroke sensor (with HID).
  1. Remove the nuts and bolt, then separate the lower arm using the prybar.
  1. Disconnect the driveshaft outboard joint from the front wheel hub using a plastic hammer.
  1. Pull the knuckle outward, and remove the driveshaft outboard joint from the front wheel hub.
  1. Left driveshaft: Install the prybar through the reference hole (A) of the front subframe, and pry the inboard joint (B) from the differential case with a screwdriver.
    Right driveshaft: Drive the inboard joint off of the intermediate shaft with a drift and hammer. Remove the driveshaft as an assembly. Do not pull on the driveshaft (C), because the inboard joint may come apart. Pull the driveshaft straight out to avoid damaging the oil seal.
