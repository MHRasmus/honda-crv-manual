Dashboard Replacement
NOTE:
  • Have an assistant help you when removing and installing the dashboard.
  • Put on gloves to protect your hands.
  • When removing components, use the trim tool or equivalent.
  • Take care not to scratch the dashboard and related parts.
  1. Remove the dashboard/steering hanger beam.
  1. Remove these items:
    • Driver's vent panel
    • Dashboard center vent
    • Passenger's vent panel
    • Passenger's airbag
    • Glove box
    • Instrument panel
    • Gauge control module
    • For some models: Navigation unit, with navigation system
    • For some models: Audio unit, without navigation system
    • Center upper dashboard panel
    • Shift lever panel
Driver's side
  1. For some models: Remove the screws, then remove the GPS antenna (A), then pull the antenna out through the hole in the dashboard.
Center
  1. Detach the wire harness clips (A) from the shift lever panel opening.
  1. From the back of the dashboard, disconnect the tweeter connectors (A) (if equipped), then detach the wire harness clips (B).
Passenger's side
  1. Carefully pry the glove box light (A) from the inside of the dashboard, and detach the wire harness clips (B).
  1. From the back of the dashboard, remove the screws, and center joint duct (A).
  1. From the front of the dashboard (A), remove the screws.
  1. Assemble the dashboard and steering hanger beam in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    NOTE:
    • Make sure the dashboard wire harness is not pinched.
    • Make sure the connectors are plugged in properly.
