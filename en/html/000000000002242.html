Vacuum Pump Replacement
  1. Remove the chain case and cam chain.
  1. Remove the cowl cover and the under-cowl panel.
  1. Remove the warm-up catalytic converter, the exhaust manifold cover, and the exhaust joint cover.
  1. Disconnect the vacuum hose.
  1. Remove the bolts (A).
  1. Remove the vacuum pump (B).
  1. Install the parts in the reverse order of removal with a new O-ring (C).
    NOTE: When remove the vacuum pump, make sure the O-ring (D) is in place.
