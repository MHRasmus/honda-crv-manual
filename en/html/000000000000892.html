Propeller Shaft Removal
  1. Raise the vehicle on a lift.
  1. Remove the No. 1 propeller shaft protector.
  1. Remove the No. 2 propeller shaft protector.
  1. Make a reference mark (A) across the No. 1 propeller shaft (B) and the transfer companion flange (C).
  1. Separate the No. 1 propeller shaft from the transfer assembly.
  1. Remove the center support bearing mounting bolts.
  1. Make a reference mark (A) across the No. 2 propeller shaft (B) and rear differential companion flange (C).
  1. Separate the No. 2 propeller shaft from the rear differential, then remove the propeller shaft assembly.
