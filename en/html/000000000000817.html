DTC Troubleshooting: 132, 133
DTC 132:
 Lateral Acceleration Sensor (Software)
DTC 133:
 Lateral Acceleration Sensor (Joint Failure)
NOTE: Driving on an extremely angled road can cause this DTC.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC 91 indicated with DTC 132 or 133 at the same time?
YES -
Do the DTC 91 troubleshooting. ■
NO -
Go to Step 3 .
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Check that the yaw rate-lateral acceleration sensor is properly mounted.
Is the yaw rate-lateral acceleration sensor installation OK?
YES -
Go to Step 5 .
NO -
Reinstall the yaw rate-lateral acceleration sensor , and recheck by test-driving.■
  1. Put the vehicle on a level surface.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Check the LATERAL ACCELERATION SENSOR in the VSA DATA LIST with the HDS.
Is the indicated within ±1 m/s?
YES -
Go to Step 8 .
NO -
Replace the yaw rate-lateral acceleration sensor. ■
  1. Clear the DTC with the HDS.
  1. Test-drive the vehicle around a number of corners.
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC 132 or 133 indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 11 .
NO -
Intermittent failure; the most probable cause is that the vehicle has been driven on an extremely angled road. The system is OK at this time.■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Substitute a known-good yaw rate-lateral acceleration sensor.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Clear the DTC with the HDS.
  1. Test-drive the vehicle around a number of corners.
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.

YES -
