Engine Removal
NOTE:
  • Use fender covers to avoid damaging painted surfaces.
  • To avoid damaging the wiring and terminals, unplug the wiring connectors carefully while holding the connector portion.
  • Mark all wiring and hoses to avoid misconnection. Also, be sure that they do not contact other wiring or hoses, or interfere with other parts.
  1. Make sure you have the anti-theft code for the audio system and the navigation system (if equipped).
  1. Remove the hood, then use it as shown to prop the hood in the wide open position.
  1. Disconnect the negative cable from the battery first, then disconnect the positive cable.
  1. Remove the battery.
  1. Remove the air cleaner housing assembly.
  1. Remove the under-cowl cover, then remove the strut brace.
  1. Remove the harness clamps (A), then remove the battery base (B).
  1. Remove the intake air ducts.
  1. Remove the intercooler outlet hose clamp.
  1. Remove the bolt (A) securing the clutch line.
  1. Remove the two bolts (B), and loosen the two bolts (C), then remove the battery base bracket (D).
  1. Remove the engine cover.
  1. Remove the air flow tube.
  1. Remove the harness clamp (A), then remove the air cleaner housing bracket (B).
  1. Remove the battery cables from the under-hood fuse/relay box.
  1. Remove the intercooler outlet hose.
  1. Remove the clutch slave cylinder and clutch line bracket mounting bolt.
  1. Remove the shift cables.
  1. Remove the fuel hose (A), and fuel return hose (B), and brake booster vacuum hose (C).
  1. Remove the harness clamps (A), then disconnect the engine wire harness connectors (B).
  1. Remove the drive belt.
  1. Remove the power steering (P/S) pump (A) without disconnecting the P/S hoses, then remove the P/S hose from the clamp (B).
  1. Remove the expansion tank cap.
  1. Raise the vehicle on the lift to full height.
  1. Remove the front wheels.
  1. Remove the engine undercover.
  1. Loosen the drain plug in the radiator, and drain the engine coolant.
  1. Drain the engine oil.
  1. Drain the transmission fluid.
  1. Remove exhaust pipe A.
  1. Separate the knuckles from the lower arms.
  1. Remove the driveshafts. Coat all precision-finished surfaces with clean engine oil. Tie plastic bags over the driveshaft ends.
  1. Loosen the bands (A) securing the turbocharger outlet hose and intercooler intake hose, and remove the intercooler intake hose (B), then remove the intercooler intake pipe mounting bolts (C).
  1. Remove the intercooler intake pipe bracket (D), then remove the intercooler intake pipe (E).
  1. Lower the vehicle on the lift.
  1. Remove the radiator.
  1. Remove the A/C compressor.
  1. Remove the P/S fluid reservoir from the holder.
  1. Remove the expansion tank.
  1. Remove the expansion tank bracket.
  1. Remove the heater hoses.
  1. Raise the vehicle on the lift.
  1. Remove the lower torque rod.
  1. Lower the vehicle on the lift.
  1. Attach the chain hoist using the sling belt and karabiner to the engine/transmission as shown.
    NOTE:
    • Take care not to damage the A/C pipes.
    • The sling belt must use the one that the load of 1,000 kg (2,204 lbs) or more can be endure.
    • The karabiner must use the one that the load of 500 kg (1,102 lbs) or more can be endure.
  1. Remove the upper torque rod.
  1. Remove the ground cable (A), then remove the side engine mount bracket (B).
  1. Remove the ground cable (A), then remove the transmission mount (B).
  1. Check that the engine/transmission is completely free of vacuum hoses, fuel and coolant hoses, and electrical wiring.
  1. Slowly raise the engine/transmission assembly about 150 mm (6 in.). Check once again that all hoses and electrical wiring are disconnected and free from the engine/transmission.
  1. Raise the engine all the way, and remove it from the vehicle.
