DTC Troubleshooting: B2125
DTC B2125:
 Steering Angle Sensor Signal Error
  1. Clear the DTCs with the HDS.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF, and then back ON (II).
  1. Turn the headlight switch ON.
  1. Turn the steering wheel straight ahead, and keep it in that position.
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC B2125 indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 6 .
NO -
Intermittent failure, the adaptive front lighting system is OK at this time. Check for loose or poor connections at the steering angle sensor connector and at the adaptive front lighting control unit connector.■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Remove the steering column covers.
  1. Disconnect the 5P connector from the steering angle sensor.
  1. Remove the driver's dashboard lower cover.
  1. Disconnect the 40P connector from the adaptive front lighting control unit.
  1. With VSA: Disconnect the 46P connector from the VSA modulator-control unit.
  1. Check for continuity between body ground and the steering angle sensor 5P connector terminals No. 2, No. 3, No. 4, and No. 5 individually.
Is the continuity?
YES -
Repair a short in the wire.■
NO -
Go to Step 13 .
  1. Check for continuity between steering angle sensor 5P connector terminals No. 2, No. 3, No. 4, and No. 5 * and adaptive front lighting control unit 40P connector terminals No. 18, No. 17, No. 38, and No. 19 * respectively.
    *:
    Without VSA

Is there continuity?
YES -
  • With VSA: Go to Step 14 .
  • Without VSA: Go to Step 15 .
NO -
Repair an open in the wire.■
  1. Check for continuity between steering angle sensor 5P connector terminal No. 5 and VSA modulator-control unit 46P connector terminal No. 33.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Go to Step 15 .
NO -
Repair an open in the wire.■
  1. Reconnect the adaptive front lighting control unit 40P connector.
  1. Reconnect the steering angle sensor 5P connector.
  1. Measure voltage between body ground and adaptive front lighting control unit 40P connector terminal No. 18 and No. 38 by analog circuit tester during turning the steering wheel slowly.
Does the voltage change 0−5 V?
YES -
Go to Step 18 .
NO -
Faulty steering angle sensor, replace it.■
  1. Measure voltage between body ground and adaptive front lighting control unit 40P connector terminal No. 17.
Is there about 0.5−4.5 V?
YES -
Faulty adaptive front lighting control unit, replace it and do the adaptive front lighting control unit learning procedure. ■
NO -
Faulty steering angle sensor, replace it.■
