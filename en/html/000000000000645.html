DTC Troubleshooting: 88
DTC 88:
 Difference in Wheel Speed
NOTE: A wheel bearing that is installed backwards can cause this DTC.
  1. Check the all four tyres.
Are all four tyres the correct size and properly inflated?
YES -
Go to Step 2 .
NO -
Install the correct tyres, a known-good set of tyres, or set the tyres to the correct pressure, and retest by testdriving.■
  1. Test-drive the vehicle.
  1. Check the RF, LF, RR, LR, WHEEL SPD in the ABS DATA LIST with the HDS.
Are all four the same indicated value?
YES -
Go to Step 4 .
NO -
Reinstall or replace the wheel sensor(s), and recheck by test-driving.■
  1. Check the magnetic encoders on the front wheel bearings or rear magnetic encoders on the hub bearing units.
Are they installed correctly and good in condition?
YES -
Check for loose terminals in the ABS modulator-control unit 26P connector. Check for a loose connection at G202. If necessary, substitute a known-good ABS modulator-control unit and retest.■
NO -
Replace the front wheel bearing(s) or the rear hub bearing unit(s) and recheck by test-driving:
  • Front: Replace the front wheel bearing. ■
  • Rear: Replace the rear hub bearing unit. ■
