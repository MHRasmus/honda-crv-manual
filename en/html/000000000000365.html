Multi-information Display Select/Reset/Information/Distance Switch Test/Replacement
  1. Remove the driver's airbag assembly.
  1. Remove the cover (A) and screws, and disconnect the 6P connector (B) from the select/reset/information/distance switch (C).
  1. Measure resistance between the select/reset/information/distance switch 6P connector No. 6 and No. 5 terminals in each switch position according to the table.
    Position
    Resistance
    INFO
    About 33 Ω
    SEL/RESET
    About 1.043 Ω

  1. If the resistance is not as specified, remove the two screws and replace the switch.
  1. If the switch is OK, but there is a problem with the switch function, check the steering switches harness and cable reel.
