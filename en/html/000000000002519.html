Audio System Symptom Troubleshooting - Power switch will not turn ON (No information display and no sound) (without navigation)
Power switch will not turn ON (No information display and no sound) (without navigation)
  1. With the ignition switch ON (II), push the power switch ON to see if audio unit turns ON.
Does the audio unit operate properly, and does the audio sound normal?
YES -
Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time.■
NO -
Go to Step 2 .
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Check the No. 14 (15 A) *1 or No. 16 (10 A) *2 fuse in the under-hood fuse/relay box and No. 34 (7.5 A) fuse in the under-dash fuse/relay box.
    *1: With subwoofer
    *2: Without subwoofer
Are the fuses OK?
YES -
Go to Step 4 .
NO -
Replace the fuse(s), and recheck.■
  1. Remove the audio unit. Check that the audio unit is properly connected.
Is it connected properly?
YES -
Go to Step 5 .
NO -
Reconnect the connector, and recheck the function.■
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Measure the voltage between audio unit connector A (17P) terminal No. 14 and body ground, and between terminal No. 17 and body ground.
Is there battery voltage on the both terminals?
YES -
Go to Step 7 .
NO -
Repair open in the wire(s) between the No. 14 (15 A) *1 or No. 16 (10 A) *2 fuse in the under-hood fuse/relay box and No. 34 (7.5 A) in the under-dash fuse/relay box and the audio unit.■
  1. Measure the voltage between audio unit connector A (17P) No. 9 terminal and body ground.
Is there less than 0.1 V?
YES -
Replace the audio unit. ■
NO -
Repair open or high resistance in the wire between audio unit connector A (17P) No. 9 terminal and body ground (G504). ■
