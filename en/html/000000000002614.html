Normally-open type (Except PGM-FI main relay 1, Fuel heater relay, ACC/TSA relay, ISV relay, Power mirror defogger relay, Rear window defogger relay, Radiator fan relay, A/C condenser fan relay and blower motor relay)
Check for continuity between the terminals.
  • There should be continuity between the No. 1 and No. 2 terminals when power and ground are connected to the No. 3 and No. 4 terminals.
  • There should be no continuity between the No. 1 and No. 2 terminals when power is disconnected.
PGM-FI main relay 1, Main ECU relay, A/C condenser fan relay, Rear window defogger relay
  1. Disconnect the under-hood fuse/relay box connector J (10P) and K (5P).
  1. At the under-hood fuse/relay box side, do the following test.
    PGM-FI main relay 1:
    • Connect power to the connector J No. 10 and ground to the connector J No. 3 terminals. There should be battery voltage between the connector J No. 4 terminal and the body ground.
    • Disconnect power from the connector J No. 10 and ground from the connector J No. 3 terminals. There should be no voltage between the connector J No. 4 terminal and the body ground.
    Main ECU relay:
    • Connect power to the connector J No. 10 and ground to the connector J No. 3 terminals. There should be battery voltage between the connector J No. 5 terminal and the body ground.
    • Disconnect power from the connector J No. 10 and ground from the connector J No. 3 terminals. There should be no voltage between the connector J No. 5 terminal and the body ground.
    A/C condenser fan relay:
    • Connect power to the connector J No. 2 and ground to the connector J No. 1 terminals. There should be battery voltage between the connector K No. 5 terminal and the body ground.
    • Disconnect power from the connector J No. 2 and ground from the connector J No. 1 terminals. There should be no voltage between the connector K No. 5 terminal and the body ground.
    Rear window defogger relay:
    • Connect power to the connector J No. 6 and ground to the connector J No. 8 terminals. There should be battery voltage between the connector J No. 7 terminal and the body ground.
    • Disconnect power from the connector J No. 6 and ground from the connector J No. 8 terminals. There should be no voltage between the connector J No. 7 terminal and the body ground.
PGM-FI main relay 1, Ignition coil relay, PGM-FI subrelay, ETCS relay, Rear window defogger relay, ISV relay, Power mirror defogger relay
  1. Disconnect the under-hood fuse/relay box connector D (20P).
  1. At the under-hood fuse/relay box side, do the following test.
    ISV relay:
    • Connect the No. 14 terminal and the body ground. There should be battery voltage between the No. 2 terminal and the body ground.
    • Disconnect the No. 14 terminal and the body ground. There should be no voltage between the No. 2 terminal and the body ground.
    Power window defogger relay:
    • Connect power to the No. 6 and ground to the No. 20 terminals. There should be battery voltage between the No. 9 terminal and the body ground.
    • Disconnect power from the No. 6 and ground from the No. 20 terminals. There should be no voltage between the No. 9 terminal and the body ground.
    Rear window defogger relay:
    • Connect power to the No. 6 and ground to the No. 5 terminals. There should be battery voltage between the No. 13 terminal and the body ground.
    • Disconnect power from the No. 6 and the ground from No. 5 terminals. There should be no voltage between the No. 13 terminal and the body ground.
Radiator fan relay, Fuel heater relay, ACC/TSA relay
  1. Disconnect the under-hood fuse/relay box connector E (20P).
  1. At the under-hood fuse/relay box side, do the following test.
    Radiator fan relay:
    • Connect power to the No. 4 or No. 5 and ground to the No. 7 terminals. There should be battery voltage between the No. 2 terminal and the body ground.
    • Disconnect power from the No. 4 or No. 5 and ground from the No. 7 terminals. There should be no voltage between the No. 2 terminal and the body ground.
    Fuel heater relay:
    • Connect power to the No. 4 or No. 5 and ground to the No. 16 terminals. There should be battery voltage between the No. 17 terminal and the body ground.
    • Disconnect power from the No. 4 or No. 5 and ground from the No. 16 terminals. There should be no voltage between the No. 17 terminal and the body ground.
    ACC/TSA relay:
    • Connect power to the No. 8 and ground to the No. 9 terminals. There should be battery voltage between the No. 10 terminal and the body ground.
    • Disconnect power from the No. 8 and ground from the No. 9 terminals. There should be no voltage between the No. 10 terminal and the body ground.
Five-terminal type
Check for continuity between the terminals.
  • There should be continuity between the No. 1 and No. 2 terminals when power and ground are connected to the No. 3 and No. 5 terminals.
  • There should be continuity between the No. 1 and No. 4 terminals when power is disconnected.
