AFS Gearbox Replacement
  1. Remove the headlight assembly.
  1. Remove the HID unit from the headlight.
  1. Turn the cover (A) 45 ° counterclockwise to remove it from the headlight housing.
  1. Remove the adaptive front lighting gearbox cover (A) and gasket (B).
    NOTE: Check the gasket (B) of the gearbox cover, and replace it if it is destroyed or compressed.
  1. Turn the adaptive front lighting gearbox connector (A) 45 ° clockwise, and push the connector into the headlight housing (B).
  1. Remove the three screws from the adaptive front lighting gearbox (C).
  1. Install the gearbox in the reverse older of removal.
  1. After installation, do the adaptive front lighting control unit learning procedure.
