Brake Booster Replacement
LHD Type
  1. Remove the air cleaner.
  1. Remove the master cylinder.
  1. Disconnect the vacuum hose (A) from the brake booster (B).
  1. Remove the brake lines (A) from the clamps (B).
  1. Remove the flange nuts (A), and move the fuel filter (B) aside. Do not disconnect the fuel hoses.
  1. Remove the flange bolt (A), and move the relay (B) aside.
  1. Remove the driver's dashboard undercover.
  1. Remove the lock pin (A) and the joint pin (B), then disconnect the yoke (C) from the brake pedal.
  1. Remove the brake booster mounting nuts (D).
  1. Pull the brake booster (A) forward, then pull up on the bottom of the brake booster to turn it.
  1. Remove the brake booster from the engine compartment.
    • Be careful not to damage the booster surfaces and threads of the booster stud bolts.
    • Be careful not to bend or damage the brake lines or other components hoses and lines.
  1. Remove the vacuum pipe (A) and the check valve grommet (B) from the brake booster (C).
    NOTE: During installation, be careful not to drop the grommet into the brake booster.
  1. Install the check valve grommet to the brake booster first, then install the vacuum pipe to the check valve grommet correctly.
  1. Install the brake booster in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • Tighten all mounting hardware to the specified torque values.
    • Install the master cylinder after installing the brake booster.
    • Check the brake pedal height and free play after installing the master cylinder if necessary.
    • Bleed the brake system.
    • Keep grease off the vacuum hose, the vacuum pipe, and the check valve grommet.
