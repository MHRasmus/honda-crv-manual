DTC Troubleshooting: 76-3x
DTC 76-3x (‘‘x'' can be 0 thru 9 or A thru F):
 Faulty F-CAN communication or Internal Failure of E-pretensioner Unit
NOTE: Before doing this troubleshooting procedure, review SRS Precautions and Procedures.
  1. Erase the DTC memory.
  1. Read the DTC.
Is DTC 76-3x indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 3 .
NO -
Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time. Go to Troubleshooting Intermittent Failures. If another DTC is indicated, go to the DTC Troubleshooting Index.
  1. Check for DTCs in the ECM/PCM.
Are any DTCs indicated?
YES -
Troubleshoot the ECM/PCM DTCs.
NO -
Go to Step 4 .
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect the floor wire harness 14P connector B from the e-pretensioner unit.
  1. Disconnect ECM/PCM connector A (31P), for K24Z1 engine , for except K24Z1 engine.
  1. Check resistance between e-pretensioner unit connector B (14P) terminal No. 3 and ECM/PCM connector A (44P) terminal No. 36, and between e-pretensioner unit connector B (14P) terminal No. 5 and ECM/PCM connector A (44P) terminal No. 37. There should be 0−1.0Ω
Is the resistance as specified?
YES -
Check the loose terminals and poor connections at e-pretensioner control unit connector B (14P) terminals No. 3 and No. 5. If the terminals and connections are OK, replace the e-pretensioner unit , and recheck.
NO -
Repair high resistance or an open circuit between junction box and the e-pretensioner unit connector B (14P).■
