Front Door Outer Handle Replacement
NOTE: Put on gloves to protect your hands.
  1. Raise the glass fully.
  1. Remove the door panel.
  1. Remove the plastic cover (A).
    -1.
    Remove the plug caps (B), and detach the harness clips (C).
    -2.
    Disconnect the power door lock actuator connector (D), and pass the harness (E) and cables (F) through the holes (G) in the plastic cover.
  1. Detach the rod fastener (A).
  1. Remove the maintenance cap (A). With a clip remover, disconnect the outer handle rod (B).
  1. Pull both side flanges (A) of the retainer (B) outward, and pull the middle flange portion (C) of the outer casing cover (D) out, then disconnect the cylinder cable (E) from the latch (F).
  1. Remove the bolt, then remove the spacer (A).
  1. Remove the bolt securing the outer handle protector (A), then remove the protector by releasing the hook (B).
  1. While pulling the outer handle (A), remove the handle from the holes in the door panel. Take care not to scratch the door.
  1. If necessary, remove the special screws, then separate the door cylinder (A) and outer handle protector (B). If the retainer (C) is damaged, release the hooks (D), and replace it.
    NOTE: If removed, the special screws must be replaced.
  1. Remove the rod fastener (A) from the outer handle (B), then replace it with a new one.
