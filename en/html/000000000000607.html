Wiper Motor Replacement
Windshield: Removal
  1. Open the hood. Remove the caps, nuts (A), and the windshield wiper arms (B).
    NOTE: Carefully remove the wiper arms so that they do not touch the hood.
  1. Remove the hood seal and cowl covers (C).
  1. Disconnect the 5P connector (D) from the wiper motor.
  1. Remove the bolts and the under-cowl panel (A).
  1. Remove the four bolts and wiper linkage assembly (B).
  1. Remove the nut (A), and separate the link B and windshield wiper linkage (C).
  1. Disconnect the windshield wiper motor subharness (D), then remove the three bolts, and separate the windshield wiper linkage from the wiper motor (E).
Windshield: Installation
  1. Plug in motor alone, turn the wiper/washer switch (LO) or (HI) ON, then OFF to return the motor shaft to the park position.
    NOTE:
    • Does not use the wiper/washer switch (INT) position in this step.
    • If necessary, replace any damaged clips.
    • Apply multipurpose grease to the moving parts.
  1. Install the wiper motor to the wiper linkage assembly.
  1. Install the link to the wiper motor shaft, then align the mark (A) of the link and the mark (B) of the wiper linkage assembly.
  1. Before installing the wiper linkage assembly to the vehicle, align the mark (A) of the link and the mark (B) of the linkage assembly.
  1. After installation, adjust the wiper arms.
Rear Window
  1. Open the tailgate and remove the tailgate lower panel.
  1. Remove the cap (A), mounting nut (B), wiper arm (C), and special nut (D).
  1. Disconnect the 4P connector (A) from the rear window wiper motor (B).
  1. Remove the three bolts and wiper motor.
  1. Install in the reverse order of removal.
