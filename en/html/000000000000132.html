ACC Distance Switch Test/Replacement
  1. Remove the steering wheel.
  1. Remove the screws, then remove the steering wheel rear cover (A).
  1. Remove the lid (A), screws, then disconnector the 6P connector (B). Remove the distance switch (C).
  1. Check for continuity between the 8P connector No. 1 and No. 6 terminals.
    • There should be continuity with the distance switch pressed.
    • There should be no continuity with the distance switch released.
  1. If the continuity check is not as specified, replace the distance switch.
  1. Install the parts in the reverse order of removal.
