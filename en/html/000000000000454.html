Power Mirror Actuator Replacement
Removal
  1. Remove the mirror holder.
  1. Remove the power mirror, and disconnect the power mirror 13P (6P) connector from the door wire harness.
  1. Record the power mirror 13P (6P) connector (A) terminal locations and wire colors.
  1. Disassemble the power mirror 13P connector, and remove all terminals from the connector.
  1. Remove the screw and the gasket (B).
  1. Remove the screw and the harness clip (C).
  1. Remove the cover (A).
  1. Remove the three screws, and separate the mirror housing (B) from the bracket (C).
  1. Remove the screw, visor (A) and actuator (B).
  1. Disconnect the 2P connector (C) from side turn signal light (D).
Installation
  1. Route the wire harness (A) of the new actuator through the hole in the bracket (B) and gasket (C).
  1. Install the parts in the reverse order of removal.
  1. Insert the new actuator terminals into the connector in the original arrangement.
  1. Apply tape to seal intersection of the wire harness and the gasket.
  1. Reassemble in the reverse order of disassembly.
    NOTE: Be careful to break the mirror when reinstalling it to the actuator.
  1. Reinstall the mirror assembly on the door.
  1. Operate the power mirror to ensure smooth operation.
