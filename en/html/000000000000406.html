Reminder System (MICU, Gauge Control Module) Input Test
MICU
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Remove the left [right] kick panel.
    [ ]: RHD type
  1. Disconnect the under-dash fuse/relay box connectors E, R, and S.
    NOTE: All connector views are wire side of female terminals.
  1. Inspect the connector and socket terminals to be sure they are all making good contact.
    • If the terminals are bent, loose or corroded, repair them as necessary, and recheck the system.
    • If the terminals are OK, go to Step 5 .
  1. Reconnect the connectors, turn the ignition switch ON (II) and make these input tests at the connectors.
    • If any test indicates a problem, find and correct the cause, then recheck the system.
    • If all the input tests prove OK, go to Step 6 .
    Cavity
    Wire
    Test condition
    Test: Desired result
    Possible cause if result is not obtained

    Driver's door closed
    Check for voltage to ground: There should be 5 V or more.
    • Faulty driver's door switch
    • Short to ground in the wire
    R14
    GRY

    Check for voltage to ground: There should be less than 1 V.
    • Poor ground (G501)
    • Faulty ignition key switch
    • An open in the wire

    Check for voltage to ground: There should be 5 V or more.
    • Faulty ignition key switch
    • Short to ground in the wire

Gauge Control Module
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Remove the gauge control module, and disconnect the connector A (36P).
  1. Inspect the connector and socket terminals to be sure they are all making good contact.
    • If the terminals are bent, loose or corroded, repair them as necessary, and recheck the system.
    • If the terminals are OK, go to Step 9 .
  1. With the connector still disconnect, make these input tests at the connector.
    • If any test indicates a problem, find and correct the cause, then recheck the system.
    • If all the input tests prove OK, go to Step 10 .
    Cavity
    Wire
    Test condition
    Test: Desired result
    Possible cause if result is not obtained
    A18
    ORN
    Under all conditions
    Check for voltage to ground: There should be battery voltage.
    • Blown No. 23 (10 A) fuse in the under-hood fuse/relay box
    • An open in the wire
    A36
    YEL
    Ignition switch ON (II)
    Check for voltage to ground: There should be battery voltage.
    • Blown No. 10 (7.5 A) fuse in the under-dash fuse/relay box
    • An open in the wire
    A15
    BLK
    Under all conditions
    Check for continuity to ground: There should be continuity.
    • Poor ground (G501) [G502]
    • An open in the wire

    [ ]:
    RHD type

  1. Reconnect the gauge control module connector A (36P), and make these input tests at the connector.
    • If any test indicates a problem, find and correct the cause, then recheck the system.
    • If all the input tests prove OK, go to Step 11 .
    Cavity
    Wire
    Test condition
    Test: Desired result
    Possible cause if result is not obtained
    A29
    PUR
    Parking brake applied
    Check for voltage to ground: There should be less than 1 V.
    • Faulty parking brake switch
    • An open in the wire
    Parking brake released
    Check for voltage to ground: There should be 5 V or more.
    • Faulty parking brake switch
    • Short to ground in the wire

  1. Do the gauge control module self-diagnostic function procedure.
    • If the beeper sounds and the seat belt reminder indicator flashes, go to Step 12 .
    • If the beeper does not sound or the seat belt reminder indicator does not flash, replace the gauge control module.
  1. KY model: Do the Speed Alarm Chime from the Function Test with the HDS.
    • If the chime sounds, go to Step 13 .
    • If the chime does not sound, replace the gauge control module.
  1. Substitute a known-good gauge control module and recheck the system.
    • If the symptom goes away, the gauge control module is faulty; replace it.
    • If the symptom is still present, the MICU is faulty; replace the under-dash fuse/relay box.
