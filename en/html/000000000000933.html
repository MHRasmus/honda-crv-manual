Climate Control Unit Removal/Installation
  1. Move the shift lever to position 1 (A/T) or the change lever to position 4th (M/T).
  1. Place protective tape (A) on both sides of the dashboard next to the climate control unit.
  1. Insert a flat-tip screwdriver in the groove on each side, then pull straight out on both screwdriver shaft .
    NOTE: Do not pry with the flat-tip screwdrivers.
  1. Remove the climate control unit (A). Disconnect the connector (B).
  1. Install the control panel in the reverse order of removal. After installation, operate the various functions to make sure they work properly.
  1. Run the self-diagnosis function to confirm that there are no problems in the system.
