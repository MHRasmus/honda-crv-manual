M/T Clutch Housing Mainshaft Bearing and Oil Seal Replacement
Special Tools Required

 07JAC-PH80100

 07JAC-PH80200

Oil seal driver
 07JAD-PL90100

Driver
 07749-0010000

Attachment, 42 x 47 mm
 07746-0010300
  1. Remove the differential assembly.
  1. Remove the ball bearing (A) from the clutch housing (B) using the bearing remover shaft (C), slide hammer (D), adjustable bearing puller (E).
  1. Remove the oil seal (A) from the clutch side.
  1. Drive the new oil seal in from the transmission side using the driver (A), attachment (B).
  1. Drive the new ball bearing (A) in from the transmission side using the oil seal driver (B).
