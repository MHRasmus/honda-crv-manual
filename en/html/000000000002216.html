ECM Replacement
  1. Connect the HDS to the data link connector (DLC) (A) located under the driver's side of the dashboard.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Make sure the HDS communicates within the ECM. If it doesn't, go to the DLC circuit troubleshooting.
  1. Select READ DATA in the REPLACE ECM menu with the HDS.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF, and wait 2 minutes.
  1. Remove the glove box.
  1. Remove the passenger's kick panel.
  1. Remove the blower-heater unit stay (A).
  1. Remove the ECM with bracket (B).
  1. Disconnect the ECM connectors (C).
  1. Remove the bracket from the ECM (D).
  1. Install the parts in the reverse order of removal.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Select the IMMOBILIZER SYSTEM with the HDS.
  1. Rewrite the immobilizer code with the ECM replacement procedure in the HDS; it allows you to start the engine.
  1. Select WRITE DATA in the REPLACE ECM menu with the HDS.
  1. Reset the ECM with the HDS.
  1. With DPF system: Do the DPF regeneration.
