HFT-Voice Control Switch Test/Replacement
  1. Remove the steering wheel.
  1. Remove the HFT-voice control switch.
  1. Measure resistance between the No. 3 and No. 4 terminals in each switch position according to the table.

    HFT-Voice Control Switch

    Position
    Resistance
    OFF
    About 1 kΩ
    HFT Talk button pressed
    About 185 Ω
    HFT Back button pressed
    About 45 Ω

  1. If the resistance is not as specified, replace the switch.
