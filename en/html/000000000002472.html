NOTE:
  • Coat all parts with MTF during reassembly.
  • Replace the tapered roller bearing and the bearing outer race as a set if either part is replaced.
  1. Remove the tapered roller bearing outer race (A) from transfer housing (B) by heating the cover to almost 100 °C (212 °F) using a heat gun (C). Do not heat the cover over 100 °C (212 °F).
Bearing Outer Race Locations
  1. Install the 57 mm tapered roller bearing outer race using the driver(A), attachment (B).
  1. Install the 62 mm tapered roller bearing outer race and 50 mm tapered roller bearing outer race using the installer shaft (A), installer nuts (B), taper bearing outer race attachment (C) and bearing installer attachment (D).
