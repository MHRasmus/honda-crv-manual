SRS Symptom Troubleshooting - Side airbag cutoff indicator does not come on
Side airbag cutoff indicator does not come on
NOTE: If the SRS indicator also stays on, go to SRS indicator stays on, but no DTCs are stored.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II), and check that the side airbag cutoff indicator comes on for about 6 seconds.
Does the side airbag cutoff indicator come on?
YES -
Go to the Symptom Troubleshooting ‘‘SRS indicator stay on.''
NO -
Go to Step 2 .
  1. Connect the HDS to the DLC.
  1. Make sure the HDS communicates with the vehicle and the SRS unit. If it does not troubleshoot the DLC circuit, for K24Z1 engine , for except K24Z1 engine.
  1. Do the self diagnostic function with the HDS.
Does the side airbag cutoff indicator come on?
YES -
Faulty SRS unit; replace the SRS unit.
NO -
Faulty gauge control module; replace the gauge control module.
