DTC Troubleshooting: 23-01, 32-0C, 34-01, 35-01, 35-02, 35-04, 35-05, 35-08, 35-0A, 36-02, 37-01, 38-01, 38-03, 71-11
DTC 23-01:
 CAN Data Stuck ON (Initial diagnosis)
DTC 32-0C:
 Battery Current Detect Circuit (Initial diagnosis)
DTC 34-01:
 Power Relay Stuck ON (Initial diagnosis)
DTC 35-01:
 EPS Control Unit Internal Circuit (CPU) (Initial diagnosis/Regular diagnosis)
DTC 35-02:
 EPS Control Unit Internal Circuit (EEPROM1) (Initial diagnosis/Regular diagnosis)
DTC 35-04:
 EPS Control Unit Internal Circuit (CPU Communication) (Initial diagnosis/Regular diagnosis)
DTC 35-05:
 EPS Control Unit Internal Circuit (A/D Conversion) (Initial diagnosis/Regular diagnosis)
DTC 35-08:
 EPS Control Unit Internal Circuit (EEPROM2) (Initial diagnosis/Regular diagnosis)
DTC 35-0A:
 EPS Control Unit Internal Circuit (CPU Operation Device) (Regular diagnosis)
DTC 36-02:
 EPS Control Unit Internal Circuit (Prohibition Circuit) (Initial diagnosis)
DTC 37-01:
 EPS Control Unit Internal Circuit (Step-up Circuit) (Initial diagnosis/Regular diagnosis)
DTC 38-01:
 EPS Control Unit Internal Circuit (Temperature Sensor) (Initial diagnosis/Regular diagnosis)
DTC 38-03:
 EPS Control Unit Internal Circuit (Temperature Sensor Correlation) (Regular diagnosis)
DTC 71-11:
 Motor Angle Sensor Signal (Initial diagnosis)
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Clear the DTC with the HDS.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Start the engine.
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.

YES -
Replace the EPS control unit. ■
