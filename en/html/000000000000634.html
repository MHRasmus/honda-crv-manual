DTC Troubleshooting: 11, 13, 15, 17
DTC 11, 13, 15, 17:
 Wheel Sensor (Open/Short to Body Ground/Short to Power)
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Clear the DTC with the HDS.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF, then turn it ON (II) again.
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC 11, 13, 15, and/or 17 indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 5 .
NO -
Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time. Check for loose terminals between the wheel sensor 2P connector and the ABS modulator-control unit 26P connector. Check for a loose connection at G202. Refer to intermittent failures troubleshooting. ■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Check for loose terminals between the wheel sensor 2P connector and the ABS modulator-control unit 26P connector.
Are their connections OK?
YES -
Go to Step 7 .
NO -
Reconnect the connector and retest.■
