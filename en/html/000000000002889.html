DTC Troubleshooting: B2980 (13)
DTC B2980 or DTC indicator 13:
 A Short in the A/C Pressure Sensor Circuit
  1. Clear the DTC with the HDS.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF and then ON (II).
  1. Do the self-diagnostic with the HDS or HVAC control unit.
  1. Check for DTCs.
Is DTC B2980 or 13 indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 5 .
NO -
Intermittent failure.■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect the A/C pressure sensor 3P connector.
  1. Disconnect the HVAC control unit 28P connector.
  1. Check for continuity between body ground and the HVAC control unit 28P connector terminals No. 1 and No. 6 individually.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Repair short to body ground in the wire(s) between the HVAC control unit and the A/C pressure sensor.■
NO -
Go to Step 9 .
  1. Check for continuity between the HVAC control unit 28P connector terminals No. 1 and No. 6.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Repair short in the wires.■
NO -
Go to Step 10 .
  1. Reconnect the HVAC control unit 28P connector.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Measure the voltage between the HVAC control unit 28P connector terminals No. 1 and No. 19, and between the HVAC control unit 28P connector terminals No. 6 and No. 19.
Is there about 5 V?
YES -
Replace the A/C pressure sensor.■
NO -
Substitute a known-good HVAC control unit, and recheck. If the symptom/indication goes away, replace the original HVAC control unit .■
