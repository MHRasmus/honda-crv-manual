Glove Box Striker Replacement
NOTE: Take care not to scratch the dashboard, and related parts.
  1. For some models: Remove the glove box light (A).
  1. Detach the hook of the glove box damper and release the glove box stop on each side from the dashboard by pushing them inside.
  1. Remove the screws, then remove the glove box striker (B).
  1. Install the striker in the reverse order of removal.
