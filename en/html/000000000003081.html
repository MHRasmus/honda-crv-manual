Brake Booster Replacement
RHD Type
NOTE: The illustrations show NISSIN type.
  1. K24Z1 engine model: Remove the cowl cover and the under-cowl panel.
  1. Remove the master cylinder: NISSIN type, BOSCH type.
  1. Disconnect the vacuum hose (A) from the brake booster (B).
  1. K24Z1 engine model: Remove the pump outlet hose clamp (C) and move the pump outlet hose (D) aside.
  1. R20A1 engine model: Remove the A/C line clamp (A). Do not disconnect the A/C line.
  1. Remove the brake pipes (B).
  1. Remove the lock pin (A) and the joint pin (B).
  1. NISSIN type: Loosen the nut (C).
  1. Disconnect the yoke (D) from the brake pedal.
  1. Remove the brake booster mounting nuts (E).
  1. NISSIN type: Remove the york from the brake booster rod (F).
  1. Pull the brake booster (A) forward, until it clears the cowl, then pull up on the bottom of the brake booster to turn it.
  1. Remove the brake booster upward from the engine comportment.
    • Be careful not to damage the booster surfaces and the threads on the booster stud bolts.
    • Be careful not to bend or damage the brake lines or other components hoses and lines.
  1. BOSCH type: Remove the vacuum pipe (A) and the check valve grommet (B) from the brake booster (C) if necessary.
    NOTE: During installation, be careful not to drop the grommet into the brake booster.
  1. Install the brake booster in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • Tighten all mounting hardware to the specified torque values.
    • Install the master cylinder after installing the brake booster: NISSIN type, BOSCH type.
    • Check the brake pedal height and free play after installing the master cylinder, and adjust it if necessary.
    • Bleed the brake system.
    • Keep grease off the vacuum hose and the vacuum pipe and the check valve grommet.
