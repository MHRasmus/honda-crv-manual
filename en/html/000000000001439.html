DTC Troubleshooting: 61-06
DTC 61-06:
 Open the Motor Harness (Regular diagnosis)
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Clear the DTC with the HDS.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Start the engine.
  1. Turn the steering wheel from lock-to-lock several times.
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC 61-06 indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 7 .
NO -
Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time. Check for poor connections or loose terminals at the EPS control unit.■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect EPS control unit connectors B (2P) and C (2P).
  1. Check for continuity between the following terminals of EPS control unit connector B (2P) and connector C (2P).
Is there continuity?
YES -
Check for loose terminals in the EPS control unit connectors, and repair if necessary. If no poor connections are found, replace the EPS control unit. ■
NO -
Go to Step 10 .
  1. Disconnect the motor 1P or 2P connectors.
  1. On the motor side, check for continuity between the following terminals of the motor 1P connector and the motor 2P connector.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Repair open in the wire between the EPS control unit and the motor.■
NO -
Replace the motor: LHD type, RHD type. The fault is an open in the motor wire harness, or motor internal circuit.■
