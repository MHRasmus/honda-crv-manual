Interior Trim Removal/Installation - Rear Door Sill Area
Rear Door Sill Area
NOTE:
  • Put on gloves to protect your hands.
  • Take care not to bend or scratch the trim and panels.
  • When removing components, use the trim tool or equivalent.
  1. Tip up the rear seat.
  1. Detach the hooks (A) from the cargo area side trim panel (B) and center pillar lower trim (C), and pull the rear door sill trim (D) up by hand to detach the clips (E, F).
  1. If necessary, pull the rear side outer trim (G) up to detach the clip (H, I), then remove trim.
  1. Pull out the rear door opening seal (A) from the trim hooks (B) and around the front door opening flange, then remove the seal.
  1. Install the trim in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • Check if the clips are damaged or stress-whitened, and if necessary, replace them with new ones.
    • Push the clips and hooks into place securely.
