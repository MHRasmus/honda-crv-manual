Engine Oil Replacement
  1. Warm up the engine.
  1. Remove the maintenance lid (M/T).
  1. Remove the drain bolt (A), and drain the engine oil.
  1. Reinstall the drain bolt with a new washer (B).
  1. Refill the engine with the recommended oil.
    Capacity
    At Oil Change: 4.0 L (4.2 US qt, 3.5 Imp qt)
    At Oil Change Including Filter: 4.2 L (4.4 US qt, 3.7 Imp qt)
    After Engine Overhaul: 5.3 L (5.6 US qt, 4.7 Imp qt)

  1. Run the engine for more than 3 minutes, then check for oil leakage.
  1. Install the maintenance lid (M/T).
