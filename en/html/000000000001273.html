Engine Oil Pan Removal
  1. If the engine is already out of the vehicle, go to Step 20 .
  1. Remove the drive belt.
  1. Remove the A/C compressor without disconnecting the A/C hoses.
  1. Raise the vehicle on the lift to full height.
  1. Remove the splash shield.
  1. Drain the engine oil.
  1. Remove the bolts (A) securing the automatic transmission fluid (ATF) cooler line.
  1. Remove exhaust pipe A.
  1. Separate the right side stabilizer link.
  1. Separate the right side knuckle from the lower arms.
  1. Remove the right side driveshaft. Coat all precision-finished surfaces with clean engine oil. Tie plastic bag over the driveshaft end.
  1. Support the oil pan with a jack.
  1. Remove the lower torque rod.
  1. Remove a jack.
  1. Remove the bolts securing the left steering gearbox mounting bracket.
  1. Remove the bolts securing the right steering gearbox mounting brackets.
  1. Support the rear side of the subframe with a jack. Remove the subframe mounting bolts (A) on both sides, then loosen the four subframe mounting bolts (B) on the rear side of subframe so they are about 10 mm (0.4 in.) from the mounting surface. Remove a jack.
  1. Support the front side of the subframe with a jack. Remove the two subframe mounting bolts. Lower the subframe so it is about 50 mm (2.0 in.) from the body.
  1. Remove the sift cable cover (A/T).
  1. Remove the oil level sensor cover (A), then disconnect the oil level sensor connector (B) (KE, KG, KS models).
  1. Remove the bolts securing the harness holder (KE, KG, KS models).
  1. Remove the lower torque rod bracket.
  1. Remove the A/C compressor bracket.
  1. Remove the intermediate shaft.
  1. Remove the clutch cover/torque converter cover (A), and remove the two bolts (B) securing the transmission.
  1. Remove the bolts securing the oil pan.
  1. Using a flat blade screwdriver, separate the oil pan from the block in the places shown.
  1. Remove the oil pan.
