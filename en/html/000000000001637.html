Radiator and A/C Condenser Fan, Motor, and Shroud Removal and Installation
Removal
  1. Remove the hood support rod, then use it as shown to prop the hood in the wide-open position.
  1. Remove the front grille cover.
  1. Disconnect the fan motor connectors (A) and hood switch connector (B), then remove the harness clamps (C).
  1. Remove the radiator upper brackets (A), then remove the front bulkhead (B).
  1. Remove the condenser fan shroud assembly (A), then remove the radiator fan shroud assembly (B) from condenser fan shroud side.
  1. Disassemble the fan shrouds.
Installation
  1. Assemble the fan shrouds.
  1. Install the radiator fan shroud assembly (A), then install the condenser fan shroud assembly (B).
  1. Install the front bulkhead (A), then install the radiator upper brackets (B).
  1. Apply body paint to the bulkhead mounting bolts.
  1. Connect the fan motor connectors (A) and hood switch connector (B), then install the harness clamps (C).
  1. Remove the front grille cover.
