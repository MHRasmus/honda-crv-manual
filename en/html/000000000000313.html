Gauge Control Module Replacement
NOTE: Before replacing the gauge control module, rewrite the ODO data on a new gauge control module.
  1. Remove the instrument panel.
    Place a clean shop towel under the gauge assembly to prevent scratching the steering column or dashboard.
  1. Remove the three mounting screws from the gauge control module (A).
  1. Disconnect the connectors (B), and remove the gauge control module.
  1. Install the gauge control module in the reverse order of removal.
