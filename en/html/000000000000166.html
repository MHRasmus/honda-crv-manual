SRS Symptom Troubleshooting - Faulty rear seat belt reminder light
Faulty Rear Seat Belt Reminder Light
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II), unbuckle the rear seat belt.
  1. Check that the rear seat belt reminder light in multi-infomation display.
Does the rear seat belt reminder light is unbuckle indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 3 .
NO -
Go to Step 7 .
  1. Buckle the rear seat belt.
  1. Check that the rear seat belt reminder light in multi-infomation display.
Does the rear seat belt reminder light is buckle indicated?
YES -
Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time.■
NO -
Go to Step 5 .
  1. Disconnect the side wire harness connector from the rear seat belt buckle switch.
  1. Check that the rear seat belt reminder light in multi-infomation display.
Does the rear seat belt reminder light is buckle indicated?
YES -
Faulty rear seat belt buckle switch. Replace the rear seat belt buckle assembly.■
NO -
Short to ground in the side wire harness or floor wire harness; replace the faulty wire harness.■
  1. Disconnect the side wire harness connector from the rear seat belt buckle switch.
  1. Install a jumper wire between No. 1 terminal and No. 2 terminal of right side wirer harness connector.
  1. Check that the rear seat belt reminder light in multi-infomation display.
Does the rear seat belt reminder light is unbuckle indicated?
YES -
Faulty rear seat belt buckle switch; replace the rear seat belt buckle assembly.■
NO -
Go to Step 10 .
  1. Remove a jumper wire from right side wire harness connector.
  1. Install a jumper wire between No. 2 terminal of side wire harness connector and body ground.
  1. Check that the rear seat belt reminder light in multi-infomation display.
Does the rear seat belt reminder light is unbuckle indicated?
YES -
Open in the side wire harness or floor wire harness; replace the faulty harness.■
NO -
Open in the BLK wire harness of side wire harness or faulty body ground terminal (G601), for LHD model , for RHD model. If the body ground terminal is OK, replace the side wire harness.■
