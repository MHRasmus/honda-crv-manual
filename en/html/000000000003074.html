Rear Driveshaft Reassembly

Exploded View
  1. Wrap the splines with vinyl tape (A) to prevent damaging the inboard boot.
  1. Install the new ear clamp bands (B) and inboard boot, then remove the vinyl tape. Be careful not to damage the inboard boot.
  1. Install the spider (A) onto the driveshaft by aligning the marks (B) you made on the spider and the end of the driveshaft.
  1. Fit the circlip (C) into the driveshaft groove. Always rotate the circlip in its groove to make sure it is fully seated.
  1. Fit the rollers (A) onto the spider (B) with their high shoulders facing outward, and note these items:
    • Reinstall the rollers in their original positions on the spider by aligning the marks (C) you made.
    • Hold the driveshaft pointed up to prevent the rollers from falling off.
  1. Pack the inboard joint with the joint grease included in the new inboard boot set.
    Grease quantity
    Inboard joint:
    80−90 g (2.8−3.2 oz)

  1. Fit the inboard joint (A) onto the driveshaft, and note these items:
    • Reinstall the inboard joint onto the driveshaft by aligning the marks (B) you made on the inboard joint and the rollers (C).
    • Hold the driveshaft so the inboard joint points up to prevent it from falling off.
  1. Fit the boot (A) ends onto the driveshaft (B) and inboard joint (C).
  1. Adjust the length of the driveshafts to the figure as shown, then adjust the boots to halfway between full compression and full extension. Make sure the ends of the boots seat in the grooves of the driveshaft and joint. Doing this prevents a vacuum or too much air in the boot, preventing it from compressing or extending properly.
  1. Make sure the ends of the boot are seated in the grooves in the driveshaft and joint, then install the boot bands.
  1. Install the new set ring (A).
