Front Seat Frame Replacement
Manual Seat
NOTE:
  • Put on gloves to protect your hands.
  • Apply oil to the pivot portions of the slide locks.
  • Apply multipurpose grease to the sliding portions of the seat tracks.
  • LHD is shown; RHD is similar.
  • If the side airbag has deployed, replace the seat frame and related parts with new ones.
  1. Remove the front seat.
  1. Remove these items:
    • Front seat recline covers
    • Front seat-back cover/pad
    • Front seat cushion cover/pad
    • OPDS unit
  1. Gently pull out the outer riser cover (A) and inner riser cover (B) by releasing the clips. Driver's seat is shown, passenger's seat is similar.
  1. Remove the clips, then remove the recline inner covers (A) from the seat frame (B). With side airbag; remove the module holder (C). Passenger's seat is shown; driver's seat is similar.
  1. If necessary, remove the member cover (A) from the front cushion rear member (B).
  1. Install the new seat frame in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • Check if the clips are damaged or stress-whitened, and if necessary, replace them with new ones.
    • Push the clips into place securely.
8-way power Seat (Driver's seat)
NOTE:
  • Put on gloves to protect your hands.
  • Apply oil to the pivot portions of the slide locks.
  • Apply multipurpose grease to the sliding portions of the seat tracks.
  • LHD is shown; RHD is similar.
  • If the side airbag has deployed, replace the seat frame and related parts with new ones.
  1. Remove the front seat.
  1. Remove these items:
    • Front seat recline covers
    • Front seat-back cover/pad
    • Front seat cushion cover/pad
  1. Remove the screws, then remove the recline inner covers (A) from the seat frame (B).
  1. Gently pull out the outer upper rail covers (A), then detach the clips, and release the hook (B).
  1. Detach the harness clips (A) and connector clips (B) to remove the seat subharness (C) from the front seat harness holder (D).
  1. Remove the screws (A), and remove the front seat harness holder (B) from the seat cushion frame (C). Remove the bolts (D), and remove the seat cushion frame front the front seat frame.
  1. Remove the push nuts (A), and remove the recline covers (B).
  1. If necessary, remove the member cover (C) from the front cushion rear member (D).
  1. Remove the bolts, then separate the front seat-back flame (A) and seat cushion flame (B).
  1. Remove the bolt (A) and push nut (B), then remove the connecting rod (C), rod cover (D), and recline motor (E) from the seat-back flame (F). Release the both lower springs (G) of the lumber support from the seat-back flame.
    NOTE:
    • Replace the push nut with new ones. Make sure the push nut is installed correctly.
    • Apply liquid thread lock to the motor mounting bolt before reinstallation.
  1. Remove the nuts, and remove the lumber motor (A). Release the upper springs (B) from the seat-back flame (C), then remove the lumbar support (D) and motor as an assembly.
  1. Disconnect the harness connectors and harness band, and remove the driver's seat harness (A). Remove the rear up-down motor (B), front up-down motor (C), slide motor/left side gearbox (D), right side gearbox (E), from the slid/up-down adjuster (F).
  1. Install the new seat frame in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • Make sure the OPDS unit and motor connectors are plugged in properly.
    • Check if the clips are damaged or stress-whitened, and if necessary, replace them with new ones.
    • Push the clips into place securely.
