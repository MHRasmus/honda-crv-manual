Steering Gearbox Removal and Installation
Special Tools Required

Ball joint remover, 28 mm
 07MAC-SL00201
Removal
Note these items during removal:
  • Use solvent and a brush to wash any oil and dirt off the end of the steering gearbox. Avoid any electrical parts. Blow dry with compressed air.
  • Make sure to remove the steering wheel before disconnecting the steering joint to avoid damaging the cable reel.
  • The illustrations show LHD type, RHD type is same procedure.
  • The illustrations show 2WD.
  1. Make sure you have the anti-theft code for the audio system (if equipped), then write down the audio presets.
  1. Make sure the ignition switch is OFF, then disconnect the negative cable from the battery.
  1. Raise the front of the vehicle, and support it with safety stands in the proper locations.
  1. Remove the front wheels.
  1. Remove the steering wheel.
  1. Remove the driver's dashboard undercover.
  1. Remove the steering joint cover (A).
  1. Remove the steering joint bolt (A), and disconnect the steering joint by moving the steering joint (B) toward the column. Hold the lower slide shaft (C) on the column with a piece of wire (D) between the joint yoke (E) on the lower slide shaft to the joint yoke on the upper shaft.
  1. Remove the center guide (A) (if equipped), and discard it. The center guide is for factory assembly only.
  1. Apply vinyl tape to the splines on the pinion shaft (B).
  1. Remove the cotter pin (A) from the 12 mm nut (B), and loosen the nut.
  1. Separate the tie-rod ball joint and the knuckle using the ball joint remover.
  1. Remove exhaust pipe A and exhaust pipe B.
  1. 4WD: Remove the propeller shaft and propeller shaft protectors.
  1. Remove the P/S heat shield (A) from the steering gearbox.
  1. Remove the P/S heat shield (A) (if equipped) from the gearbox brackets (B).
  1. LHD type: Disconnect the motor 1P connector (A), motor 2P connector (B), torque sensor 6P connector (C), and motor angle sensor 8P connector (D) from the steering gearbox. Wrap the connectors with vinyl tape to avoid the contamination from grease or water.
  1. RHD type: Disconnect the motor 1P connector (A), motor 2P connector (B), torque sensor 6P connector (C), and motor angle sensor 8P connector (D) from the steering gearbox. Wrap the connectors with vinyl tape to avoid the contamination from grease or water.
  1. RHD type: Remove the harness clip (E) from the connector bracket (F).
  1. Remove the 10 mm flange bolts (A) and washers (B) from the left side of the steering gearbox.
  1. Remove the 8 mm flange bolts (C), then remove the stabilizer bar bushing holder (D).
  1. Remove the 10 mm flange bolts (A) and washers (B) from the right side of the steering gearbox.
  1. Remove the 8 mm flange bolts (C), then remove the stabilizer bar bushing holder (D).
  1. Remove the 10 mm flange bolts (A) from the both sides of gearbox brackets (B).
  1. Disconnect both stabilizer links form the stabilizer bar, then remove the stabilizer bar.
  1. Remove the pinion shaft grommet (A) from the top of the torque sensor.
  1. Grasp the passenger's side tie-rod, and pull the rack all the way to the passenger's side.
  1. Move the steering gearbox (A) to the passenger's side, and rotate it so the pinion shaft points toward the downward.
  1. Slide the steering gearbox (A) between the body and front subframe toward the rear side, and remove the steering gearbox. Be careful not to damage these parts due to interference with the edge of the front subframe when extracting and inserting the steering gearbox.
    • Torque sensor housing
    • Boots
    • Serrations of the pinion shaft
    • Motor connectors
    • Torque sensor 6P connector
    • Motor angle sensor 8P connector
    • Tie-rod end ball joint boots
Installation
  1. Before installing the steering gearbox, make sure that no fluid or grease is on the mating surface of the steering gearbox and the front subframe. To prevent the gearbox mounting bolts from loosening after the installation, remove the fluid or grease from the mount cushions and bolt holes.
  1. Apply vinyl tape to the splines on the pinion shaft (A).
  1. Slide the steering gearbox (B) between the front subframe and body from the rear side.
  1. Rotate the steering gearbox (A) so the pinion shaft points upward, then move the steering gearbox to the in position on the front subframe.
  1. Install the pinion shaft grommet (A). Align the cutout portion (B) in the pinion shaft grommet with the cutout portion (C) on the torque sensor. The grommet must not have a gap at the mating surface of the grommet and the torque sensor.
  1. Set the stabilizer bar, then connect both stabilizer links to the stabilizer bar.
  1. Install the gearbox brackets (A), and loosely install the 10 mm flange bolts (B).
  1. Tighten the 10 mm flange bolts to the specified torque in the sequence shown.
  1. Loosely install the 10 mm flange bolts (A) and washers (B) to the left side of the steering gearbox.
  1. Install the stabilizer bar bushing holder (C).
  1. Loosely install the 10 mm flange bolts (A) and washers (B) to the right side of the steering gearbox.
  1. Install the stabilizer bar bushing holder (C).
    NOTE: Be sure that the pinion shaft grommet is in place securely. Check whether the pinion shaft grommet is not turning up. Incorrect installation can cause leakage of water, mud, and noise.
  1. Tighten the 10 mm flange bolts to the specified torque in the sequence shown.
  1. LHD type: Remove the vinyl tape, then connect the motor 1P connector (A), motor 2P connector (B), torque sensor 6P connector (C), and motor angle sensor 8P connector (D) to the steering gearbox. Make sure to push these connectors until you hear a click so that the connectors are secured.
  1. RHD type: Remove the vinyl tape, then connect the motor 1P connector (A), motor 2P connector (B), torque sensor 6P connector (C), and motor angle sensor 8P connector (D) to the steering gearbox. Make sure to push these connectors until you hear a click so that connectors are secured.
  1. RHD type: Install the harness clip (E) to the connector bracket (F).
  1. Install the P/S heat shield (A)(if equipped) to the gearbox brackets (B).
  1. Install the P/S heat shield (A) to the steering gearbox.
  1. 4WD: Install the propeller shaft and propeller shaft protectors.
  1. Install exhaust pipe A and exhaust pipe B.
  1. Wipe off any grease contamination from the ball joint tapered section and threads. Reconnect the tie-rod ends (A) to the steering knuckles. Install the 12 mm nut (B), and tighten it.
  1. Install the new cotter pin (C), and bend it as shown.
    NOTE: Make sure the boot (D) for damage and deterioration. If there is damage or deterioration, replace the boot.
  1. Center the steering rack within its stroke in steering joint connection, then remove the vinyl tape from the pinion shaft.
  1. With the rack in the straight ahead driving position, cut the wire (A) and slip the lower end of the steering joint onto the pinion shaft (B) in the range shown.
  1. Align the bolt hole (A) on the steering joint with the groove (B) around the pinion shaft, and loosely install the joint bolt (C). Be sure that the joint bolt is securely in the groove in the pinion shaft. Pull on the steering joint to make sure that the steering joint is fully seated. Tighten the steering joint bolt to the specified torque.
  1. Install the steering joint cover (A).
  1. Install the driver's dashboard undercover.
  1. Install the front wheels, then set the wheels in the straight ahead position.
  1. Install the steering wheel.
  1. With the tyres raised off the ground, check for the following symptoms by turning the steering wheel fully to the right and left several times.
    Symptom
    Probable cause
    Rubbing sound coming from the lower steering column area.
    Steering column joint is contacting the cover.
    Grating sound from the lower steering column area, or a rough feeling during steering.
    Poor engagement of the pinion shaft serrations.
    Noise from around the steering wheel during steering.
    Poor engagement of the SRS cable reel with the steering wheel, or a damaged cable reel.

  1. Reconnect the negative cable to the battery, and do these tasks and checks:
    • Turn the ignition switch ON (II); the SRS indicator should come on for about 6 seconds and then go off.
    • Enter the anti-theft code for the audio system (if equipped), then enter the audio presets.
    • Make sure the horn and turn signal switches work properly.
    • Make sure the steering wheel switches work properly.
    • Make sure the steering wheel is centered.
    • Set the clock.
  1. After installation, do these checks:
    • Start the engine, allow it to idle, and turn the steering wheel from lock-to-lock several times.
    • Check that the EPS indicator does not come on.
    • Check the steering wheel spoke angle. If steering spoke angles to the right and left are not equal (steering wheel and rack are not centered), correct the engagement of the joint/pinion shaft serrations, then adjust the front toe by turning the tie-rod ends, if necessary.
    • Check the wheel alignment, and adjust it if necessary.
