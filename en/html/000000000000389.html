Front Fog Lights Replacement
  1. Remove the front bumper. Remove the inner fender (right front fog light).
  1. Disconnect the 2P connector (A) from the front fog light (B).
  1. Remove the three screws from the front fog light.
With headlight washer model is shown:
  1. Turn the bulb socket (A) 45 ° counterclockwise to remove the bulb.
    Front Fog Light:
    55 W

  1. Install the light in the reverse order of removal.
    NOTE: Do not touch the glass of the bulb with your hands. If necessary, clean the glass area with alcohol.
