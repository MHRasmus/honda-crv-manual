Steering Rack Guide Adjustment
Special Tools Required

Locknut wrench, 40 mm
 07MAA-SL00100
Power Steering Type
  1. Set the wheels in the straight ahead position.
  1. Loosen the rack guide screw locknut (A) with the locknut wrench, then remove the rack guide screw (B).
    NOTE: The illustration shows LHD type/2WD.
  1. Remove the old sealant from the rack guide screw (A), and apply new sealant (Three Bond 1215 or Locktite 5699) to the middle of the threads (B).
    Loosely install the rack guide screw on the steering gearbox.
  1. Tighten the rack guide screw (A) to 25 N·m (2.5 kgf·m, 18 lbf·ft), then loosen it.
  1. LHD type: Retighten the rack guide screw to 7.8 N·m (0.8 kgf·m, 5.8 lbf·ft), then back it off to the specified angle.
  1. RHD type: Retighten the rack guide screw to 3.9 N·m (0.4 kgf·m, 2.9 lbf·ft), then back it off to the specified angle.
    Specified return angle:
    10 ° ±5 °

  1. Hold the rack guide screw stationary with a wrench, and tighten the locknut (B) by hand until it's fully seated.
  1. Install the locknut wrench on the locknut, and hold the rack guide screw stationary with a wrench. Tighten the locknut an additional 30 ° with the locknut wrench.
  1. Check for unusual steering effort through the complete turning range.
  1. Check the steering wheel rotational play, and the power assist.
EPS Type
  1. Set the wheels in the straight ahead position.
  1. Loosen the rack guide screw locknut (A) with the locknut wrench, then remove the rack guide screw (B).
    NOTE: The illustration shows LHD type/2WD.
  1. Remove the old sealant from the rack guide screw (A), and apply new sealant (seal End No. 22) to the middle of the threads (B).
    Loosely install the rack guide screw on the steering gearbox.
  1. Tighten the rack guide screw (A) to 4.0 N·m (0.4 kgf·m, 3.0 lbf·ft), then back it off to the specified angle.
    Specified return angle:
    6 °±1 °

  1. Hold the rack guide screw stationary with a wrench, and install the locknut (B) by hand until it's fully seated.
  1. Install the locknut wrench on the locknut, and hold the rack guide screw stationary with a wrench. Tighten the locknut an additional 30 ° with the locknut wrench.
  1. Check for unusual steering effort through the complete turning travel.
  1. Check the steering wheel rotational play, and the power assist.
