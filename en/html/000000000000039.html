Wheel Sensor Replacement
Front
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Release the connector holding clamps (A), then disconnect the wheel sensor connector (B).
  1. Remove the clips, the bolt and the wheel sensor (C).
  1. Install the wheel sensor in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • Install the sensor carefully to avoid twisting the wires.
    • If the wheel sensor comes in contact with the wheel bearing, it is faulty.
  1. Start the engine, and check that the ABS indicator goes off.
  1. Test-drive the vehicle, and check that the ABS indicator does not come on.
