Engine Oil Pan Removal
  1. If the engine is already out of the vehicle, go to Step 19 .
  1. Raise the vehicle on the lift to full height.
  1. Drain the engine oil.
  1. Remove the front wheels.
  1. Remove the splash shield.
  1. Disconnect the air fuel ratio (A/F) sensor connector and secondary heated oxygen sensor (secondary HO2S) connector, then remove the three way catalytic converter (TWC).
  1. Remove the shift cable (A/T) .
  1. Separate the knuckles from the lower arms .
  1. Remove the propeller shaft.
  1. Remove a bolt securing the P/S fluid line bracket, and unclamp the P/S fluid line clamps on the front subframe.
  1. Remove the bolts securing the left steering gearbox mounting bracket.
  1. Remove the bolts securing the right steering gearbox mounting brackets .
  1. Remove the bolt securing the automatic transmission fluid (ATF) filter (A/T).
  1. Remove the front mount mounting bolt (M/T).
  1. Remove the lower torque rod .
  1. Make the appropriate reference lines at both ends of the subframe that line up with the body.
  1. Remove the subframe mounting bolts on both side.
  1. Remove the subframe.
  1. Remove the lower torque rod bracket.
  1. Remove the clutch cover (A) and transmission mounting bolts (B) (M/T).
  1. Remove the torque converter cover (A/T).
  1. Remove the bolts securing the oil pan.
  1. Using a flat blade screwdriver, separate the oil pan from the block in the places shown.
  1. Remove the oil pan.
