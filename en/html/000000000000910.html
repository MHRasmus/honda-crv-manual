Rear Differential Oil Seal Replacement
Special Tools Required

Attachment, 52 x 55 mm
 07NAD-P200100

Oil seal driver 65
 07JAD-PL90100

Attachment, 78 x 80 mm
 07NAD-PX40100

Driver 15 x 135L
 07749-0010000
  1. Remove the oil seals (A) from the differential housing.
    NOTE: Be careful not to damage the differential carrier while prying out the seals.
  1. Install the right side oil seal (A) squarely using the driver (B) and attachment (C). Installation depth of the oil seal is 9 mm (0.35 in.) below the machined edge of the differential carrier assembly. Be careful not to damage the lip of the oil seals.
  1. Install the left side oil seal (A) squarely and flush with the machined edge of the differential carrier assembly using the driver (B) and attachment (C). Be careful not to damage the lip of the oil seal.
  1. Remove the companion flange.
  1. Remove the oil seal (A) from the torque control differential case (B).
    NOTE: Be careful not to damage the shaft or case while prying out the seal.
  1. Install the oil seal (A) squarely using the oil seal driver (B). Be careful not to damage the lip of the oil seal.
  1. Install the companion flange.
