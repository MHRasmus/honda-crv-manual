DTC Troubleshooting: B2375
DTC B2375:
 Infrared Ray Error
  1. Clear the DTCs with the HDS.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF, and then back ON (II).
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC B2375 indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 4 .
NO -
Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time.■
  1. Check for loose or poor connections at the automatic lighting/rain sensor 5P connector.
Is the connection OK?
YES -
Go to Step 5 .
NO -
Reconnect or repair the 5P connector.■
  1. Check for damage of the automatic lighting/rain sensor 5P connector terminals.
Are there any damage?
YES -
Repair the terminal.■
NO -
Go to Step 6 .
  1. Check for dirt or damage of the windshield glass.
Are there any dirt or damage?
YES -
Remove the dirt and, if necessary, repair or replace the windshield glass.■
NO -
Replace the automatic lighting/rain sensor.■
