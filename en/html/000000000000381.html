Headlight Leveling Motor Replacement
  1. Remove the headlight assembly.
  1. Turn the HID light cover (A) 45 ° counterclockwise to remove it from the headlight housing.
  1. Disconnect the 3P connector (A) and turn the leveling motor (B) 45 ° clockwise.
  1. Pull the headlight reflector (C) slightly, and remove the leveling motor.
  1. Install in the reverse order of removal.
  1. After installation, do the adaptive front lighting control unit learning procedure.
