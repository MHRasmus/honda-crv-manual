DTC Troubleshooting: B1170
DTC B1170:
 Gauge Control Module Lost Communication with the VSA Modulator-Control Unit/ABS Modulator-Control Unit (ABS message)
  1. Clear the DTCs with the HDS.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF, and then back ON (II).
  1. Wait for 6 seconds or more.
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC B1170 indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 5 .
NO -
Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time. Check for loose or poor connections.■
  1. Check for VSA system/ABS system DTCs with the HDS.
Is any DTCs indicated?
YES -
Go to the indicated DTCs, then recheck.
NO -
  • With VSA system: Go to Step 6 .
  • With ABS system: Go to Step 10 .
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect the gauge control module connector A (36P).
  1. Disconnect the VSA modulator-control unit 46P connector.
  1. Connect the gauge control module connector A (36P) No. 33 and No. 34 terminals and body ground with jumper wires.
  1. Check for continuity between the VSA modulator-control unit 46P connector No. 38 and No. 39 terminals and body ground individually.
Are there continuity?
YES -
Substitute a known-good VSA modulator-control unit, and recheck. If the indication goes away, replace the original VSA modulator-control unit. If the DTC is still present, replace the gauge control module. ■
NO -
Repair an open in the wire.■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect the gauge control module connector A (36P).
  1. Disconnect the ABS modulator-control unit 26P connector.
  1. Connect the gauge control module connector A (36P) No. 33 and No. 34 terminals and body ground with jumper wires.
  1. Check for continuity between the ABS modulator-control unit 26P connector No. 1 and No. 12 terminals and body ground individually.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Substitute a known-good ABS modulator-control unit, and recheck. If the indication goes away, replace the original ABS modulator-control unit. If the DTC is still present, replace the gauge control module. ■
NO -
Repair an open in the wire.■
