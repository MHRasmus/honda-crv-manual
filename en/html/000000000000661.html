DTC Troubleshooting: B2140
DTC B2140:
 Headlight Leveling Motor Power Signal Circuit Error
  1. Clear the DTCs with the HDS.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF, and then back ON (II).
  1. Enter the BODY ELECTRICAL, and then select the ADAPTIVE FRONT LIGHTING with the HDS.
  1. Enter the FUNCTIONAL TESTS, and then select the HEADLIGHT-UP/DOWN.
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTCs B2140 indicates?
YES -
Go to Step 6 .
NO -
Intermittent failure, the adaptive front lighting system is OK at this time. Check for loose or poor connections, between headlight leveling motor 3P connector and the adaptive front lighting control unit 40P connector.■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect the 3P connector from the right headlight leveling motor.
  1. Clear the DTCs with the HDS.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF, and then back ON (II).
  1. Enter the BODY ELECTRICAL, and then select the ADAPTIVE FRONT LIGHTING with the HDS.
  1. Enter the FUNCTIONAL TESTS, and then select the HEADLIGHT-UP/DOWN.
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC B2140 indicated?
YES -
Faulty right headlight leveling motor, replace it.■
NO -
Go to Step 13 .
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect the 3P connector from the left headlight leveling motor.
  1. Clear the DTCs with the HDS.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF, and then back ON (II).
  1. Enter the BODY ELECTRICAL, and then select the ADAPTIVE FRONT LIGHTING with the HDS.
  1. Enter the FUNCTIONAL TESTS, and then select the HEADLIGHT-UP/DOWN.
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC B2140 indicated?
YES -
Faulty left headlight leveling motor, replace it.■
NO -
Go to Step 20 .
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect the 40P connector from the adaptive front lighting control unit.
  1. Check for continuity between body ground and adaptive front lighting control unit 40P connector terminal No. 36.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Repair a short in the wire.■
NO -
Faulty adaptive front lighting control unit, replace it and do the adaptive front lighting control unit learning procedure. ■
