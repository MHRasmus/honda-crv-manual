Front Damper/Spring Removal and Installation
Removal
  1. Raise the front of the vehicle, and support it with safety stands in the proper locations.
  1. Remove the front wheel.
  1. Remove the wheel sensor harness guide (A), the harness clip (B), and the brake hose (C) from the damper (D). Do not disconnect the wheel sensor connector.
  1. Remove the flange nut (A), while holding the joint pin (B) with a hex wrench (C), and disconnect the stabilizer link (D) from the damper.
  1. Remove the damper pinch bolts (A) and self-locking nuts (B) from the damper.
  1. Remove the lid (A) by releasing the hooks (B).
  1. Remove the flange nuts (C) from the top of the damper.
  1. Remove the damper assembly (A).
    NOTE:
    • Damper springs are different, left and right. Mark the springs L and R before you continue.
    • Be careful not to damage the body.
Installation
  1. Install the damper assembly (A) on to the frame. Note the direction of the damper mounting base so that the stamp (B) on it is toward the outside of the vehicle.
    NOTE: Be careful not to damage the body.
  1. Loosely install the new flange nuts (A) to the top of the damper.
    NOTE: Install the lid (B) by pushing the hooks (C) into place securely after tightening the flange nuts to the specified torque value.
  1. Loosely install new damper pinch bolts (A) and new self-locking nuts (B) to the damper (C).
  1. Connect the stabilizer link (A) to the damper (B), and loosely install new flange nut (C), while holding the joint pin (D) with the hex wrench (E).
  1. Install the wheel sensor harness guide (A), the harness clip (B), and the brake hose (C) to the damper (D).
  1. Raise the front suspension with a floor jack to load the suspension with the vehicle's weight.
  1. Tighten the damper pinch bolts and the self-locking nuts to the specified torque value.
  1. Tighten the flange nuts on top of the damper to the specified torque value.
  1. Install the lid.
  1. Clean the mating surface of the brake disc and the inside of the wheel, then install the rear wheel.
  1. Check the wheel alignment, and adjust it if necessary.
