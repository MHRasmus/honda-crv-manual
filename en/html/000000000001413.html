EPS Control Unit Removal/Installation
NOTE: The illustrations shows LHD type, RHD type is symmetrical.
  1. Make sure you have the anti-theft code for the audio system (if equipped), then write down the audio presets.
  1. Make sure the ignition switch is OFF, then disconnect the negative cable from the battery.
  1. Remove the passenger's dashboard undercover.
  1. Remove the passenger's front door sill trim.
  1. Remove the passenger's side kick panel.
  1. Disconnect EPS control unit connector A (2P), connector B (2P), connector C (2P), and connector D (20P).
  1. Remove the nuts (E) and the bolt (F) from the EPS control unit (G).
  1. Remove the EPS control unit.
  1. Install the EPS control unit in the reverse order of removal.
  1. Reconnect the negative cable to the battery.
  1. Enter the anti-theft code for the audio system (if equipped), then enter the audio presets.
  1. After installation, start the engine, allow it to idle, and turn the steering wheel from lock-to-lock several times. Check that the EPS indicator does not come on.
