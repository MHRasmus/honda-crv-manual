DTC Troubleshooting: 71-14
DTC 71-14:
 Motor Angle Sensor (Initial diagnosis/Regular diagnosis)
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Clear the DTC with the HDS.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Start the engine.
  1. Turn the steering wheel from lock-to-lock several times.
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC 71-14 indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 7 .
NO -
Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time. Check for poor connections or loose terminals at the EPS control unit.■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect EPS control unit connector D (20P).
  1. Measure resistance between EPS control unit connector D (20P) terminals No. 7 and No. 17.
Is the resistance between 7−24 Ω?
YES -
Go to Step 12 .
NO -
Go to Step 10 .
  1. Disconnect the motor angle sensor 8P connector.
  1. On the sensor side, measure resistance between motor angle sensor 8P connector terminals No. 1 and No. 5.
Is the resistance between 7−24 Ω?
YES -
Repair open or short to body ground in the wire between the EPS control unit and the motor angle sensor.■
NO -
Replace the motor: LHD type, RHD type. The fault is a short in the motor wire harness, or motor internal circuit.■
  1. Measure resistance between EPS control unit connector D (20P) terminals No. 14 and No. 15.
Is the resistance between 22−73 Ω?
YES -
Go to Step 15 .
NO -
Go to Step 13 .
  1. Disconnect the motor angle sensor 8P connector.
  1. On the sensor side, measure resistance between motor angle sensor 8P connector terminals No. 2 and No. 3.
Is the resistance between 22−73 Ω?
YES -
Repair open or short to body ground in the wire between the EPS control unit and the motor angle sensor.■
NO -
Replace the motor: LHD type, RHD type. The fault is a short in the motor wire harness, or motor internal circuit.■
  1. Measure resistance between EPS control unit connector D (20P) terminals No. 4 and No. 5.
Is the resistance between 23−78 Ω?
YES -
Go to Step 18 .
NO -
Go to Step 16 .
  1. Disconnect the motor angle sensor 8P connector.
  1. On the sensor side, measure resistance between motor angle sensor 8P connector terminals No. 6 and No. 7.
Is the resistance between 23−78 Ω?
YES -
Repair open or short to body ground in the wire between the EPS control unit and the motor angle sensor.■
NO -
Replace the motor: LHD type, RHD type. The fault is a short in the motor wire harness, or motor internal circuit.■
  1. Check for continuity between body ground and EPS control unit connector D (20P) terminals No. 4, No. 5, No. 7, No. 14, No. 15, and No. 17 individually.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Go to Step 19 .
NO -
Check for loose terminals in the EPS control unit connectors, and repair if necessary. If no poor connections are found, replace the EPS control unit. ■
  1. Disconnect the motor angle sensor 8P connector.
  1. Check for continuity between body ground and the motor angle sensor 8P connector terminals No. 1, No. 2, No. 3, No. 5, No. 6, and No. 7 individually.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Replace the motor: LHD type, RHD type. The fault is a short in the motor wire harness, or motor internal circuit.■
NO -
Repair short to body ground in the wire between the EPS control unit and the motor angle sensor.■
