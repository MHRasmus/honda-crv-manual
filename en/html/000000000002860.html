A/C Magnet Valve Circuit Troubleshooting
NOTE: Do not use this troubleshooting procedure if the fans are also inoperative, or if the A/C compressor is inoperative. Refer to the symptom troubleshooting index.
  1. Check the No. 36 (10 A) fuse in the under-dash fuse/relay box.
Is the fuse OK?
YES -
Go to Step 2 .
NO -
Replace the fuse, and recheck. If the fuse blows again, check for a short in the No. 36 (10 A) fuse circuit.■
  1. Disconnect the magnet valve 2P connector.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Measure the voltage between the magnet valve 2P connector terminal No. 2 and body ground.
Is there battery voltage?
YES -
Go to Step 5 .
NO -
Repair open in the wire between the No. 36 (10 A) fuse in the under-dash fuse/relay box and the magnet valve.■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Reconnect the magnet valve 2P connector.
  1. Connect the magnet valve 2P connector terminal No. 1 to body ground with a jumper wire with the magnet valve connectors connected.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
Does the magnet valve operate?
YES -
Go to Step 9 .
NO -
Replace the magnet valve, and recheck.■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect the magnet valve 2P connector.
  1. Disconnect the HVAC control unit 28P connector.
  1. Check for continuity between the HVAC control unit 28P connector terminal No. 16 and the magnet valve 2P connector terminal No. 1.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Check for loose wires or poor connections at the HVAC control unit 28P connector and at the magnet valve 2P connector. If the connections are good, substitute a known-good HVAC control unit, and recheck. If the symptom/indication goes away, replace the original HVAC control unit .■
NO -
Repair open in the wire between the HVAC control unit and the magnet valve.■
