Front Seat Cushion Cover/Pad Replacement
NOTE:
  • Put on gloves to protect your hands.
  • When prying with a flat-tip screwdriver, wrap it with protective tape to prevent damage.
  • Take care not to tear the seams or damage the seat covers.
  • Do not repair any tear or frayed spots of the seat cover.
  • When removing components, use the trim tools or equivalent.
  • LHD is shown; RHD is similar.
  1. Remove the front seat.
  1. Remove these items:
    • Front seat under tray (for some models).
    • Subwoofer (for some models)
  1. From under the seat cushion, release the hook (A) from the seat cushion frame spring (B). Pull the seat-back cover (C) back.
Driver's seat
Passengers's seat
  1. Release the slits (A) in the seat cushion cover (B) from the seat cushion frame spring (C).
Driver's seat
Passengers's seat
  1. Pull the seat cushion cover back. Detach the connector(s), then pull it to out side of the seat.
    • Driver's seat:
      • -
      For some model: Side airbag connector (A)
      -
      For some model: Seat cushion heater connector (B)
    • Passengers's seat:
      • -
      For some model: Side airbag connector (A)
      -
      For some model: Seat cushion heater connectors (B)
      -
      Seat belt reminder switch connector (C)
  1. Detach the harness clip (D), and remove the wire ties (E). Driver's side: Pull the side airbag harness (F) out through slit (G) in the cover. Passenger's side: Remove the fastener (H), and release the side airbag harness from the seat cushion cover, and pull the cover back.
Driver's seat
Passengers's seat
  1. Remove the clip, then release the hook strips (A, B) from the seat frame (C). Release the loop bund (D) from the buckle.
Driver's seat
Passengers's seat
  1. Remove the seat cushion/ pad (A) from the seat frame (B).
  1. Release the hooks (A) from under the seat cushion (B).
  1. Pull back the edge of the seat cushion cover all the way around, and release the clips (A), then remove the seat cushion cover.
  1. Install the cover in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • To prevent wrinkles when installing a seat-back cover, make sure the material is stretched evenly over the pad before securing the clips, hooks, and hook strips.
    • Replace any clips you removed with new ones (A).
