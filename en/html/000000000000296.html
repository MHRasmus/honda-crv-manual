Rear Roof Side Trim Replacement
NOTE:
  • Put on gloves to protect your hands.
  • When removing components, use the trim tools or equivalent.
  • Take care not to bend the roof side trim.
  1. Remove the front roof side trim.
  1. Using a trim tool, pry up on the rear lid (A) of the rear roof side trim (B) to detach the hooks (C), and remove the nut.
  1. Pull up the rear roof side trim (A) and remove it. Take care not to scratch the body.
  1. Install the trim in the reverse order of removal.
