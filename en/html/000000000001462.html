M/T Assembly Removal
NOTE: Use fender covers to avoid damaging painted surfaces.
  1. Make sure you have the anti-theft codes for the radio and the navigation system, then write down the customer's audio presets. Disconnect the negative cable from the battery, first, then disconnect the positive cable. Remove the battery.
  1. Remove the air cleaner housing.
  1. Remove the engine coolant reservoir tank (A), harness clip (B) and the battery base (C).
  1. Remove the resonator chamber (A).
  1. Disconnect the ground cable (A). Remove the harness clip (B) and the clutch line bracket (C) then remove the battery base bracket (D).
  1. Remove the clutch line bracket (A), then carefully remove the slave cylinder (B) to avoid bending the clutch line. Do the press the clutch pedal once the slave cylinder has been removed.
  1. Remove the harness clip (A) and the cable bracket (B), then disconnect the cables (C) from the top of the transmission housing. Carefully remove both cables and the bracket together to avoid bending the cables.
  1. Disconnect the revers lockout solenoid connector (A), and harness clips (B).
  1. Disconnect the back-up light switch connector (A), the vehicle speed sensor connector (B), harness clip (C) and the bolts (D).
  1. Remove the harness holder (A) then remove the breather pipe (B) and the harness holder bracket (C).
  1. Attach the engine hanger (D) to the engine using the 8 x 1.25 mm bolt (E).
  1. Attach the chain hoist to the engine.
  1. Remove the two transmission upper mounting bolts.
  1. Remove the engine control module (ECM) stay (A), then move it aside.
  1. Disconnect the ground cable (A), then remove the transmission mount bracket bolts (B) and nuts (C). Remove the transmission mount bracket (D).
  1. Raise the vehicle, and make sure it is securely supported.
  1. Remove the splash shield.
  1. Drain the transmission fluid. Reinstall the drain bolt.
  1. Separate the lower arm.
  1. Remove the steering mount bracket bolts (A) and the stabilizer bush holder (B) from the front subframe (C).
  1. Disconnect the front suspension stroke sensor connector (A) (with HID).
  1. Remove the lower torque rod mounting bolts (A) from the lower torque rod mounting bracket (B).
  1. Support the subframe with a 4 x 4 x 40 in. piece of wood and a jack.
  1. Remove the front suspension subframe mounting bolts (A) and front suspension subframe (B).
    NOTE: Suspend the steering gearbox with an appropriate size wire.
  1. Pry the driveshaft inboard joint (A) from the differential case with a prybar.
  1. Drive the driveshaft inboard joint (A) off of the intermediate shaft (B) with a drift and hammer.
  1. Remove the lower torque rod bracket (A).
  1. Remove the flange bolts (A) and the intermediate shaft (B).
  1. Separate the propeller shaft from the transfer assembly (4WD model).
  1. Remove the clutch cover.
  1. Place the transmission jack under the transmission, and remove the transmission mounting bolts.
  1. Pull the transmission away from the engine until the transmission mainshaft clears the clutch pressure plate, then lower the transmission on the transmission jack.
  1. Remove the boot (A), the release fork (B), and the release bearing (C) from the transmission (D).
