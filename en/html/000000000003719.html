Cam Chain Inspection
  1. Remove the front wheels.
  1. Remove the splash shield.
  1. Remove the drive belt.
  1. Remove the cylinder head cover.
  1. Remove four spark plugs.
  1. Rotate the crankshaft pulley two turns clockwise.
  1. Set the No. 1 cylinder at TDC.
  1. Check the clearance between the cam chain (A) and the tensioner arm (B) by putting the pin driver 5.0 mm (P/N 07GAD-PG20100) or equivalent, commercially available into the gap (C). If the pin driver can go into the gap, replace the cam chain and the oil pump chain.
  1. Install four spark plugs, then torque them to 18 N·m (1.8 kgf·m, 13 lbf·ft).
  1. Install the cylinder head cover.
  1. Install the drive belt.
  1. Install the splash shield.
  1. Install the front wheels.
