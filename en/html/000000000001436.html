DTC Troubleshooting: 52-06
DTC 52-06:
 Torque Sensor (Offset) (Regular diagnosis)
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Clear the DTC with the HDS.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Start the engine.
  1. Turn the steering wheel from lock-to-lock several times.
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC 52-06 indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 7 .
NO -
Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time. Check for poor connections or loose terminals at the EPS control unit.■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect EPS control unit connector D (20P).
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Measure voltage between body ground and EPS control unit connector D (20P) terminal No. 10 and No. 19 individually.
Is there battery voltage?
YES -
Repair short to power in the wire between the EPS control unit and the torque sensor.■
NO -
Go to Step 11 .
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Check for continuity between body ground and EPS control unit connector D (20P) terminal No. 10 and No. 19 individually.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Repair short to body ground in the wire between the EPS control unit and the torque sensor.■
NO -
Go to Step 13 .
  1. Disconnect the torque sensor 6P connector.
  1. Check for continuity between torque sensor 6P connector terminal No. 4 and EPS control unit connector D (20P) terminal No. 10.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Go to Step 15 .
NO -
Repair open in the wire between the EPS control unit and the torque sensor.■
  1. Check for continuity between torque sensor 6P connector terminal No. 1 and EPS control unit connector D (20P) terminal No. 19.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Go to Step 16 .
NO -
Repair open in the wire between the EPS control unit and the torque sensor.■
  1. Substitute a known-good EPS control unit.
  1. Connect the torque sensor 6P connector.
  1. Start the engine.
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC 52-06 indicated?
YES -
Replace the steering gearbox. The fault is an open or short in the torque sensor internal circuit.■
NO -
Replace the original EPS control unit. ■
