Rear Driveshaft Removal
  1. Raise the vehicle on a lift, and remove the rear wheels.
  1. Lift up the locking tab (A) on the spindle nut (B), then remove the nut.
  1. Drain the differential fluid.
  1. Remove the rear driveshaft inboard joint from the rear differential assembly.
  1. Disconnect the rear driveshaft outboard joint from the trailing arm and rear wheel hub using a plastic hammer or a puller if necessary.
  1. Remove the rear driveshaft (A).
    NOTE:
    • Be careful not to damage the ABS wheel sensor (B).
    • Pull on the outer joint. Do not pull on the driveshaft because the joint may come apart.
