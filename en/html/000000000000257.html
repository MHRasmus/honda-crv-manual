Emblem/Sticker Replacement (Except KD, KW, KN, KU, KK, KM, KH models)
Except KD, KW, KN, KU, KK, KM models
NOTE: When removing the emblem/sticker, take care not to scratch the body.
  1. To remove the front ‘‘H'' emblem, remove the front upper grille.
  1. To remove the rear ‘‘H'' emblem, remove the rear license trim.
  1. Clean the body surface with a sponge dampened in alcohol. After cleaning, keep oil, grease, and water from getting on the surface.
  1. When installing the rear 4WD, i-VTEC sticker on the rear window, align the lower edge of the application tape and bottom of the sticker with the rear window edge, then press the sticker into place, and remove the application tape.
  1. After installing the front ‘‘H'' emblem, reinstall the front upper grille.
  1. After installing the rear ‘‘H'' emblem, reinstall the rear license trim.
