DTC Troubleshooting: 86
DTC 86:
 F-CAN Communication
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Clear the DTC with the HDS.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Start the engine.
  1. Wait at least 5 seconds.
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC 86 indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 7 .
NO -
Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time. Check for a loose connection at G202.■
  1. Check for the fuel and emissions systems DTCs with the HDS: R20A1 and R20A2 engine models, K24Z1 engine model.
Are any DTCs indicated?
YES -
Do the applicable troubleshooting for the ECM/PCM.■
NO -
Go to Step 8 .
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect the yaw rate-lateral acceleration sensor 4P connector.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Measure the voltage between yaw rate-lateral acceleration sensor 4P connector terminal No. 1 and body ground.
Is there battery voltage?
YES -
Go to Step 12 .
NO -
Repair open in the wire between No. 4 (7.5 A) fuse in the under-dash fuse/relay box and the yaw rate-lateral acceleration sensor.■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Check for continuity between yaw rate-lateral acceleration sensor 4P connector terminal No. 4 and body ground.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Go to Step 14 .
