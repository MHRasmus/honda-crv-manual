Drive Belt Auto-tensioner Removal/Installation
  1. Remove the drive belt.
  1. Remove the power steering (P/S) fluid reservoir from the holder.
  1. Remove the P/S pump (A) without disconnecting the P/S hoses, then remove the P/S hose from the clamp (B).
  1. Remove the tensioner pulley bolt (A), then remove the tensioner pulley (B).
  1. Remove the auto-tensioner.
  1. Install the auto-tensioner in the reverse order of removal.
