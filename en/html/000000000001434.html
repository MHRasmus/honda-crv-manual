Is DTC 52-01, 52-03, 52-04, or 52-05 indicated?

NO -
Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time. Check for poor connections or loose terminals at the EPS control unit.■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect EPS control unit connector D (20P).
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Measure voltage between body ground and EPS control unit connector D (20P) terminals No. 8, No. 10, No. 18, No. 19, and No. 20 individually.
Is there battery voltage?
YES -
Repair short to power in the wire between the EPS control unit and the torque sensor.■
NO -
Go to Step 10 .
