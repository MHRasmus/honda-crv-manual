NOTE:
  • Refer to the Exploded View as needed during this procedure.
  • These descriptions are R20A1 and R20A2 or K24Z1 engine models. The point of R20A1 and R20A2 engine models that should be noted; the bearing support shape is different from the following. However, the procedure how to remove is the same.
  1. Clean the disassembled parts with solvent, and dry them with compressed air. Do not wash the rubber parts with solvent.
  1. Press the intermediate shaft bearing (A) into the bearing support (B) using the driver (C), attachment (D), and a press.
  1. Install, then seat the internal snap ring in the groove of the bearing support.
  1. Press the intermediate shaft (A) into the shaft bearing (B) using the attachment (C), and a press.
  1. Install, then seat the external snap ring (A) in the groove of the intermediate shaft (B).
  1. Install the outer seal (A) into the bearing support (B) using the oil seal driver (C) and a press.
  1. Install the set ring.
  1. Install the heat shield (K24Z1 engine model only).
