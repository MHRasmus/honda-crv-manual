Transfer Assembly Removal
  1. Raise the vehicle, and make sure it is supported securely.
  1. Shift the transmission into the N position.
  1. Remove the drain plug (A), and drain the transmission fluid (ATF).
  1. Reinstall the drain plug with a new sealing washer (B).
  1. For K24Z1 engine model: Disconnect the A/F sensor connector and the secondary heated oxygen sensor connector.
    NOTE: Steps 5 thru 7 describe three-way catalytic converter removal for K24Z1 engine model, go to step 8 and remove exhaust pipe A for R20A1 and R20A2 engine models.
  1. For K24Z1 engine model: Remove the sensor harnesses from the harness clamps.
  1. For K24Z1 engine model: Remove the three-way catalytic converter.
  1. For R20A1 and R20A2 engine models: Remove exhaust pipe A.
  1. Make a reference mark (A) across the propeller shaft (B) and the transfer companion flange (C).
  1. Separate the propeller shaft from the transfer companion flange.
  1. Remove the transfer assembly mounting bolts (A) (4), and pull out the bolt (B) to the limit of travel.
  1. Remove the transfer assembly (C) and dowel pin (D) from the transmission.
