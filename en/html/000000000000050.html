Brake Master Cylinder Disassembly
BOSCH type: Replace the master cylinder as an assembly. Do not try to disassemble the master cylinder.
NOTE:
  • The illustrations show LHD/ABS type.
  • The illustrations show NISSIN type.
  1. Remove the rod seal from the master cylinder.
  1. Disconnect the reservoir tank hose (A).
  1. Remove the snap ring (A) while pushing in the secondary piston (B).
  1. Remove the reservoir tank (A) and the reservoir grommets (B) from the master cylinder.
    NOTE: When reservoir tank and master cylinder body are separated, replace the reservoir grommets with new ones.
  1. Remove the stop pin (C) while pushing in the secondary piston (D).
  1. Remove the secondary piston (A), primary piston (B) and piston guide (C).
  1. Remove the reservoir seal (A) from the reservoir cap (B).
  1. Remove the strainer (C) from the subreservoir tank (D).
