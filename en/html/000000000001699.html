VTEC Rocker Arm Oil Control Valve Removal/Installation
  1. Remove the power steering hose stay (A).
  1. Disconnect the rocker arm oil control valve connector (A) and the rocker arm oil pressure switch connector (KD, KQ, KU (New Zealand) models) (B).
  1. Remove the bolts (C).
  1. Remove the rocker arm oil control valve (D).
  1. Install the parts in the reverse order of removal with a new solenoid valve filter (E) and O-ring.
