Clutch Hose Replacement
NOTE:
  • Replace the clutch hose if the hose is twisted, cracked, or if it leaks.
  • Use fender covers to avoid damaging painted surfaces.
  • Do not spill brake fluid on the vehicle; it may damage the paint; if brake fluid does contact the paint, wash it off immediately water.
  • R20A1 and R20A2 engine models manual transmission model is shown, K24Z1 engine model manual transmission model is similar.
  1. Make sure you have the anti-theft codes for the radio and the navigation system, then write down the customer's audio presets. Disconnect the negative cable from the battery first, then disconnect the positive cable. Remove the battery.
  1. Remove the engine coolant reservoir tank (A), harness clip (B) and the battery base (C).
  1. Remove the resonator chamber (A).
  1. Disconnect the clutch hose (A) from the clutch line (B).
  1. Remove and discard the clutch hose clips (A) from the clutch hose (B).
  1. Remove the clutch hose from the body (C) and clutch hose bracket (D).
  1. Install the clutch hose (A) on the body (B) and clutch hose bracket (C) with the new clutch hose clips (D).
  1. Connect the clutch line (A) to the clutch hose (B).
  1. Install the resonator chamber (A).
  1. Install the battery base (A), engine coolant reservoir tank (B) and the harness clip (C).
  1. Install the battery. Connect the positive cable to the battery first, then connect the negative cable.
  1. Bleed the clutch hydraulic system.
  1. Do the following checks:
    • Check the clutch hose and line joint for leaks, and tighten if necessary.
    • Check the clutch hose for interference and twisting.
  1. Enter the anti-theft code for the radio, then enter the customer's radio station presets.
  1. Perform the power window control unit reset procedure.
