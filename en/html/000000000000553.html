Power Door Lock Switch Test
Driver's door
  1. Remove the power window master switch and disconnect its connector.
  1. Check for continuity between the power window master switch 22P connector terminals.
    • There should be continuity between the No. 22 [No. 12] and No. 16 [No. 17] terminals when the door lock switch is in the LOCK position.
    • There should be no continuity between the No. 22 [No. 12] and No. 16 [No. 17] terminals when the door lock switch is in the UNLOCK position.
    • There should be continuity between the No. 22 [No. 12] and No. 18 [No. 19] terminals when the door lock switch is in the UNLOCK position.
    • There should be no continuity between the No. 22 [No. 12] and No. 18 [No. 19] terminals when the door lock switch is in the LOCK position.
    [ ]:
    With driver's window AUTO UP/AUTO DOWN

  1. If the continuity is not as specified, replace the power window master switch.
Front passenger's door with front passenger's door lock switch
  1. Remove the front passenger's power window switch.
  1. Check for continuity between the front passenger's power window switch 8P connector terminals.
    • There should be continuity between the No. 1 and No. 2 terminals when the door lock switch is in the LOCK position.
    • There should be no continuity between the No. 1 and No. 2 terminals when the door lock switch is in the UNLOCK position.
    • There should be continuity between the No. 2 and No. 6 terminals when the door lock switch is in the UNLOCK position.
    • There should be no continuity between the No. 2 and No. 6 terminals when the door lock switch is in the LOCK position.
  1. If the continuity is not as specified, replace the front passenger's power window switch.
