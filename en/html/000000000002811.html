DTC Troubleshooting: 59
DTC 59:
 BRKDIAG High
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Check the brake lights without pressing the brake pedal.
Are the brake lights on?
YES -
Replace the TSA relay.■
NO -
Go to Step 3 .
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Check the brake lights without pressing the brake pedal.
Are the brake lights on?
YES -
Go to Step 5 .
NO -
Go to Step 13 .
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect the VSA modulator-control unit 46P connector.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Check the brake lights without pressing the brake pedal.
Are the brake lights on?
YES -
Go to Step 9 .
NO -
Check for loose terminals in the VSA modulator-control unit 46P connector. Check for a loose connection at G202. If necessary, substitute a known-good VSA modulator-control unit and retest.■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect the adaptive cruise control (ACC) unit 20P connector.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Check the brake lights without pressing the brake pedal.
Are the brake lights on?
YES -
Repair short to body ground in the wire between the VSA modulator-control unit 46P connector No. 37 and the TSA relay 5P connector terminal No. 3.■
NO -
Replace the adaptive cruise control (ACC) unit.■
  1. Clear the DTC with the HDS.
  1. Press the brake pedal for 3 seconds or more.
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC 59 indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 16 .
NO -
Check for loose terminals in the VSA modulator-control unit 46P connector. Check for a loose connection at G202. If necessary, substitute a known-good VSA modulator-control unit and retest.■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Short the SCS line with the HDS.
  1. Disconnect ECM connector No. 2 (58P).
  1. Check for continuity between brake pedal position switch 4P connector terminal No. 2 and ECM connector No. 2 (58P) terminal No. 38.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Replace the ECM. ■
NO -
Repair open in the wire between the ECM and the brake pedal position switch.■
