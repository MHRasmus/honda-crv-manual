Ignition Switch Test
SRS components are located in this area. Review the SRS component locations and precautions and procedures before performing repairs or servicing.
  1. Make sure you have the anti-theft codes for the audio system and navigation system (if equipped). Make sure the ignition switch is OFF.
  1. Disconnect the negative battery cable.
  1. Remove the driver's dashboard lower cover, and the steering column covers.
  1. Disconnect the 7P connector (A) from the ignition switch (B).
  1. Check for continuity between the terminals in each switch position according to the table.
  1. If the continuity checks do not agree with the table, replace the ignition switch.
  1. After reconnecting the battery, enter the anti-theft codes for the audio system and navigation system (if equipped), then reset the clock (if equipped).
