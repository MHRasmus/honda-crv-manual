DTC Troubleshooting: 69
DTC 69:
 BRKDIAG Low
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Clear the DTC with the HDS.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF, then turn it ON (II) again.
  1. After the TSA indicator goes off, press the brake pedal for 3 seconds or more.
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC 69 indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 6 .
NO -
Go to Step 19 .
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Remove the TSA relay.
  1. Disconnect the brake pedal position switch 4P connector.
  1. Check for continuity between the brake pedal position switch 4P connector terminal No. 2 and the TSA relay 5P connector No. 4.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Go to Step 10 .
NO -
Repair open in the wire between the brake pedal position switch and the TSA relay.■
  1. Disconnect the VSA modulator-control unit 46P connector.
  1. Check for continuity between the VSA modulator-control unit 46P connector terminal No. 6 and the TSA relay 5P connector No. 1.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Go to Step 12 .
NO -
Repair open in the wire between the VSA modulator-control unit and the TSA relay.■
  1. Substitute a known-good TSA relay.
  1. Connect the VSA modulator-control unit 46P connector.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Clear the DTC with the HDS.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF, then turn it ON (II) again.
  1. After the TSA indicator goes off, press the brake pedal for 3 seconds or more.
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC 69 indicated?
YES -
Check for loose terminals in the VSA modulator-control unit 46P connector. Check for a loose connection at G202. If necessary, substitute a known-good VSA modulator-control unit and retest.■
NO -
Replace the original TSA relay.■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Remove the TSA relay.
  1. Disconnect the VSA modulator-control unit 46P connector.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Connect the VSA modulator-control unit 46P connector terminal No. 37 to body ground with a jumper wire.
  1. Check the brake lights without pressing the brake pedal.
Are the brake lights on?
YES -
Go to Step 25 .
NO -
Go to Step 29 .
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Check for continuity between the VSA modulator-control unit 46P connector terminal No. 37 and the TSA relay 5P connector No. 3.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Go to Step 27 .
NO -
Repair open in the wire between VSA modulator-control unit and the TSA relay.■
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Measure the voltage between TSA relay 5P connector terminal No. 5 and body ground.
Is there battery voltage?
YES -
Replace the TSA relay.■
NO -
Repair open in the wire between the No. 22 (7.5 A) fuse in the under-dash fuse/relay box and the TSA relay.■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Check for continuity between the VSA modulator-control unit 46P connector terminal No. 6 and the TSA relay 5P connector No. 1.
