Headlight Washer Control Unit Input Test
  1. Remove the dashboard lower cover.
  1. Remove the under-dash fuse/relay box.
  1. Disconnect the 6P connector (A) from the headlight washer control unit (B).
  1. Inspect the connector and socket terminals to be sure they are all making good contact.
    • If the terminals are bent, loose or corroded, repair them as necessary, and recheck the system.
    • If the terminals look OK, go to Step 5 .
  1. Reconnect the all connectors to under-dash fuse/relay box.
  1. With the headlight waher control unit connector still disconnected, make these input tests at the connector.
    • If any test indicates a problem, find and correct the cause, then recheck the system.
    • If all the input tests prove OK, replace the headlight washer control unit.

    Wire
    Test condition
    Test: Desired result

    Check for voltage to ground: There should be battery voltage.

    • Check for continuity between the No. 5 terminal and the No. 17 terminal of the under-dash fuse/relay box connector S (20P): There should be continuity.
    • Check for continuity between the No. 5 terminal and the No. 1 terminal of the wiper/washer switch 8P connector: There should be continuity.

    Connect the No. 1 terminal to the No. 2 terminal with a jumper wire.
    Check motor operation:
    The headlight washer motor should run.

    • An open in the wire
    • Poor ground (G401)
