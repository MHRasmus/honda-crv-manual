Lower Front Grille Replacement
NOTE: Take care not to scratch the bumper and grille.
  1. Remove these items:
    • Front bumper
    • Front upper grille
  1. Remove the clips and push nuts (A), then release the hooks (B) and remove the lower front grille (C) from the front bumper (D).
  1. Release the hooks (A), then remove the upper front lower grille moldings (B), center front lower grille molding (C), and lower front lower grille molding (D) from the front lower grille base (E).
  1. Install the grille in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • Check if the clips are damaged or stress-whitened, and if necessary, replace them with new ones.
    • Push the clips and hooks into place securely.
