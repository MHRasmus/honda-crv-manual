Fuel Filter Water Bleeding
  1. Remove the air cleaner.
  1. Place a suitable container under the fuel filter to catch up the water.
  1. Connect the hose (A) to water screw (B).
  1. Loosen the water screw, and bleed the water from the fuel filter.
  1. Tighten the water screw securely, and disconnect the hose.
  1. Start the engine, and then check the water in diesel filter warning indicator (A) is on, or showing WATER IN FUEL FILTER (B) in the information display (C).
    • If the water in diesel filter warning indicator or the WATER IN FUEL FILTER display is on, go to Step 7 .
    • If the water in diesel filter warning indicator or the WATER IN FUEL FILTER display is not on, fuel filter water bleeding is complete.
  1. Disconnect the fuel filter water level switch 2P connector, and then recheck the water in diesel filter warning indicator or the WATER IN FUEL FILTER display.
    • If the water in diesel filter warning indicator or the WATER IN FUEL FILTER display is on, repair short in the GRN wire between the fuel filter water level switch and ECM connector No. 1 terminal No. 86. If wire is OK, substitute a known-good ECM, then recheck. If the symptom/indication goes away with a known-good ECM, replace the original ECM.
    • If the water in diesel filter warning indicator or the WATER IN FUEL FILTER display is not on, replace the fuel filter water level switch.
  1. Bleed the fuel system.
