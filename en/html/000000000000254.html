Rear Door Rear Sash Trim Replacement
NOTE: Put on gloves to protect your hands.
  1. Lower the glass fully.
  1. Remove the quarter inner trim.
  1. From inside the door, remove the screw (A).
  1. Pull up the rear of the door weatherstrip (B), and remove the screw (C) from the sash. Do not remove the weatherstrip at this time, and take care not to damage the weatherstrip.
  1. Push the front edges of the rear door quarter outer trim (A) to rearward, then release the rear edges (B) by hand in the sequence shown.
  1. Install the quarter trim in the reverse order of removal.
