FTP Sensor Replacement (KD model)
KD model
  1. Lift the vehicle, and support it with jackstands.
  1. Remove the cover.
  1. Remove the EVAP canister baffle cover.
  1. Disconnect the FTP sensor connector (A).
  1. Disconnect the hose (B), remove the retainer (C), and remove the FTP sensor (D).
  1. Install the parts in the reverse order of removal with a new O-ring (E) and a new retainer.
