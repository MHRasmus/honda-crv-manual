Rear Strake Replacement
  1. Remove the clip (A) and screw (B) from the body, and remove the screw (C) from the side sill panel (D), then remove the rear strake (E) from the body.
  1. Install the strake in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • Check if the clips are damaged or stress-whitened, and if necessary, replace them with new ones.
    • Push the clips into place securely.
