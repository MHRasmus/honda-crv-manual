DTC Troubleshooting: 44-1x - SIEMENS SRS Unit
DTC 44-1x (‘‘x'' can be 0 thru 9 or A thru F):  
No Signal from the Right Side Impact Sensor (first) (SIEMENS SRS unit)
Special Tools Required

SRS inflator simulator
 07SAZ-TB4011A

SRS simulator lead L
 070AZ-SNA0300
NOTE: Before doing this troubleshooting procedure, review SRS Precautions and Procedures.
  1. Erase the DTC memory.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II), and wait for 10 seconds.
  1. Read the DTC.
Is DTC 44-1x indicated?
YES -
Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time.
NO -
Go to Step 4 .
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Check the damage of a floor wire harness and side wire harness between the SRS unit and right side impact sensor (first), and right side impact sensor (first) and right side impact sensor (second).
Are the damages OK?
YES -
Go to Step 6 .
NO -
Replace the floor wire harness or side wire harness.■
  1. Disconnect the negative cable from the battery, and wait for 3 minutes.
  1. Substitute a known-good right side impact sensor (first).
  1. Erase the DTC memory.
  1. Reconnect the negative cable to the battery.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II), and wait for 10 seconds.
  1. Read the DTC.
Is DTC 44-1x indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 12 .
NO -
Faulty right side impact sensor (first); replace the right side impact sensor (first). ■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect the negative cable from the battery, and wait for 3 minutes.
  1. Disconnect both seat belt tensioner 4P connectors and both seat belt outer lap tensioner 2P connectors.
  1. Disconnect SRS unit connector B (28P) from the SRS unit.
  1. Disconnect floor wire harness 4P connector (A) from the right side impact sensor (first).
  1. Reconnect the negative cable to the battery.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Check for voltage between the No. 2 terminal of right side impact sensor (first) 4P connector and body ground, and between the No. 4 terminal and body ground. There should be 1 V or less.
Is the resistance as specified?
YES -
Go to Step 20 .
NO -
Short to power in the floor wire harness or side wire harness; replace the faulty harness.■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Check resistance between the No. 2 terminal of right side impact sensor (first) 4P connector and body ground, and between the No. 4 terminal and body ground. There should be an open circuit (ohmmeter reads OL) or at least 1 MΩ.
Is the resistance as specified?
YES -
Go to Step 22 .
NO -
Short to ground in the floor wire harness or side wire harness; replace the faulty harness.■
  1. Check resistance between the No. 2 and No. 3 terminal of the right side impact sensor (first) 4P connector. There should be an open circuit (ohmmeter reads OL) or at least 1 MΩ.
Is the resistance as specified?
YES -
Go to Step 23 .
NO -
Short in the floor wire harness; replace the floor wire harness.■
  1. Install a jumper wire between the No. 27 terminal and No. 28 terminal of the SRS unit connector B.
  1. Check resistance between the No. 2 and No. 3 terminal of right side impact sensor (first) 4P connector. There should be 0−1.0 Ω.
Is the resistance as specified?
YES -
Reconnect the right side impact sensor (first) 4P connector. If DTCs indicated, replace the SRS unit. ■
NO -
Open in the floor wire harness; replace the floor wire harness.■
