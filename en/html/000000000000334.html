Dashboard Driver's Undercover Removal/Installation
NOTE: Take care not to scratch the dashboard and related parts.
  1. Remove the driver's dashboard undercover (A).
    -1.
    Turn the lock knob (B) 90 °.
    -2.
    Gently pull down the rear edge to detach the clip.
    -3.
    Pull the cover away to release the pin (C) from the holder (D).
  1. Install the undercover in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • Check if the clip is damaged or stress-whitened, and if necessary, replace it with a new one.
    • Push the clip into place securely.
