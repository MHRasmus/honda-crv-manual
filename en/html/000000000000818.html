DTC Troubleshooting: 134
DTC 134:
 Steering Angle Sensor (Software)
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC 91 indicated with DTC 134 at the same time?
YES -
Do the DTC 91 troubleshooting. ■
NO -
Go to Step 3 .
  1. With the vehicle on the ground, set the front wheels in the straight ahead driving position.
  1. Check Z-PHASE in the VSA DATA LIST with the HDS.
Is there about 1.0 V or less with the steering wheel straight ahead?
YES -
Go to Step 5 .
NO -
Go to Step 7 .
  1. Start the engine.
  1. Turn the steering wheel left and right at 90 degrees or more. Check the STEERING ANGLE in the VSA DATA LIST with the HDS.
Is there ±90 ° or more?
YES -
Go to Step 7 .
NO -
Replace the steering angle sensor. ■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Check the installation of the steering angle sensor.
Is the steering angle sensor installed correctly?
YES -
Go to Step 15 .
NO -
Go to Step 9 .
  1. Reinstall the steering angle sensor.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Clear the DTC with the HDS.
  1. Test-drive the vehicle around a number of corners.
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC 134 indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 14 .
NO -
The system is OK at this time.■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Substitute a known-good steering angle sensor.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Clear the DTCs with the HDS.
  1. Test-drive the vehicle around a number of corners.
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC 134 indicated?
YES -
Check for loose terminals in the VSA modulator-control unit 46P connector. Check for a loose connection at G202. If necessary, substitute a known-good VSA modulator-control unit and retest.■
NO -
Replace the original steering angle sensor. ■
