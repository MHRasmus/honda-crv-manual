A/T Interlock System - Shift Lock Solenoid Replacement
  1. Remove the shift lever.
  1. Remove the shift lock solenoid connector.
  1. Release the lock tabs retaining the shift lock solenoid using thin blade screwdrivers.
  1. Insert a 6 mm pin (A) into the guide hole (B), and push the shift lock solenoid (C) out.
  1. Replace the shift lock solenoid, solenoid plunger (D), and plunger spring (E).
  1. Apply silicone grease to the tip (F) of the shift lock stop and solenoid plunger.
  1. Install the shift lock solenoid by aligning the joint of the shift lock solenoid plunger with the tip of the shift lock stop, then push the shift lock solenoid into the shift lever securely.
  1. Install the shift lock solenoid connector.
  1. Install the shift lever.
