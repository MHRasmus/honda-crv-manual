Door Lower Trim Replacement
NOTE:
  • Take care not to scratch the door.
  • Put on gloves to protect your hands.
  1. Remove these items:
    • Door panel, front, rear
    • Door speaker
  1. From the front or the rear door, remove the screw.
  1. Using a utility knife, carefully cut the double-sided adhesive tape (A) along the edge of the door lower trim (B).
Front door
Rear door
  1. With the help of an assistant, slide the door lower trim (A) rearward, and remove it. The clips (B, C) will stay in the body. Turn the clips (B) 45 ° counterclockwise and remove them.
  1. Check if the clips are damaged or stress-whitened, and if necessary, replace them with new ones.
  1. Install the clips on the trim.
  1. Scrape off the remaining double-sided adhesive tape from the trim and door, then clean the trim and door surfaces with a sponge dampened in isopropyl alcohol.
  1. Apply primer to the area where the panel will be applied.
Front door
Rear door
  1. Attach the double-sided adhesive tape (A) to the trim (B), and fold the edge of each adhesive backing from the double-sided adhesive tape.
Front door
Rear door
  1. Hold the trim (A) up, and fit all the clips (B, C) into the holes in the door, then push on the trim until the clips snap into place.
  1. Carefully pull the adhesive backing (D) away, and push the trim into place securely.
  1. Set the screw into the back of clip, then reinstall the door speaker.
