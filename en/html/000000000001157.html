A/T Differential Carrier, Final Driven Gear, and Transfer Drive Gear Replacement
4WD
  1. Remove the final driven gear (A) and transfer drive gear (B) from the differential carrier (C).
    NOTE: The final driven gear bolts have left-hand threads.
  1. Install the final driven gear and transfer drive gear on the differential carrier in the direction shown.
  1. Secure the final drive gear, differential carrier, and transfer drive gear with the bolts. Tighten the bolts to the specified torque in a crisscross pattern in two or more steps.
