A/T Shift Cable Replacement
  1. Lift the vehicle up on a lift or apply the parking brake, block rear wheels, and raise the front of the vehicle. Make sure it is securely supported.
  1. Remove the following parts:
    • Heater control panel; HVAC control unit (without climate control) or Climate control unit (with climate control)
    • Dashboard center panel
    • Center lower covers
  1. Shift the shift lever into the R position.
  1. Remove the nut securing the shift cable end.
  1. Unlock the retainer (A).
  1. Rotate the socket holder retainer (A) counterclockwise (B) until it stops, and push the retainer lock (C) into the socket holder retainer to lock the retainer.
  1. Lower the socket holder (A), then slide it to remove the shift cable (B) from the shift cable bracket (C).
  1. Remove the shift cable cover (A).
    NOTE: The illustration shows K24Z1 engine model; R20A1 and R20A2 engine models are similar.
  1. Remove the spring clip (B) and control pin (C), and separate the shift cable end (D) from the control lever (E). Remove the bolts securing the shift cable brackets (F) (G).
  1. Remove the nuts securing the shift cable grommet (A).
  1. Remove the shift cable grommet, and pull out the shift cable (B).
  1. Insert the new shift cable through the grommet hole (C), and install the grommet in its hole. Do not bend the shift cable excessively.
  1. Secure the shift cable grommet with the nuts.
  1. Apply molybdenum grease to the bore hole of the synthetic resin bushing (A) in the shift cable end (B), and attach the shift cable end to the control lever (C). Insert the control pin (D) into the control lever hole through the shift cable end, and secure the control pin with the spring clip (E). Do not bend the shift cable excessively.
    NOTE: The illustration shows the K24Z1 engine model; R20A1 and R20A2 engine models are similar.
  1. Secure the shift cable brackets (F) (G) with the bolts.
  1. Install the shift cable cover (H).
  1. Install the shift cable on the shift lever, and adjust the shift cable.
