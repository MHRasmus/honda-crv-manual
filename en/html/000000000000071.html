VSA Off Switch Test
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Remove the power mirror switch (A), then disconnect the VSA OFF switch 5P connector (B) or VSA OFF switch 17P connector (C).
  1. Push out the VSA OFF switch (D) from the back of the switch panel.
  1. Check for continuity between the VSA OFF switch 5P connector terminal No. 1 and No. 2 or VSA OFF switch 17P connector terminal No. 10 and No. 11. There should be continuity when the switch is pressed, and no continuity when the switch is released.
  1. Check for continuity between the VSA OFF switch 5P connector terminal No. 4 and No. 5 or VSA OFF switch 17P connector No. 3 and No. 5. There should be continuity at all times.
