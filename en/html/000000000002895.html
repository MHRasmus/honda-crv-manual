Front Driveshaft Removal
  1. Raise the vehicle on a lift, and remove the front wheels.
  1. Lift up the locking tab (A) on the spindle nut (B), then remove the nut.
  1. If the driveshaft is removed, drain the transmission fluid. Reinstall the drain plug using a new washer:
    It is not necessary to drain the transmission fluid when the right driveshaft is removed.
    • Manual transmission
      • -
      K24Z1 engine model
      -
      R20A1 and R20A2 engine models
    • Automatic transmission
  1. Remove the front suspension stroke sensor (with HID).
  1. Remove the nuts and bolt, then separate the lower arm with a prybar.
  1. Disconnect the driveshaft outboard joint from the front wheel hub using a plastic hammer.
  1. Pull the knuckle outward, and remove the driveshaft outboard joint from the front wheel hub.
  1. Left driveshaft: Pry the inboard joint (A) from the differential case with a prybar. Pull the driveshaft straight out to avoid damaging the oil seal.
    Right driveshaft: Drive the inboard joint (A) off of the intermediate shaft with a drift and hammer. Remove the driveshaft as an assembly. Do not pull on the driveshaft (B), because the inboard joint may come apart.
