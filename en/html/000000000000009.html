Front Damper/Spring Disassembly, Inspection, and Reassembly

Special Tools Required

Disassembly
  1. Remove the cap (A) from the top of the damper.
  1. Compress the damper spring, then remove the self-locking nut while holding the damper shaft with a hex wrench (A). Do not compress the spring more than necessary to remove the nut.
  1. Release the pressure from the strut spring compressor, then disassemble the damper as shown in the Exploded View.
Inspection
  1. Reassemble all the parts, except for the upper spring mounting cushion, the bump stop, and the damper spring.
  1. Compress the damper assembly by hand, and check for smooth operation through a full stroke, both compression and extension. The damper should extend smoothly and constantly when compression is released. If it does not, the gas is leaking and the damper should be replaced.
  1. Check for oil leaks, abnormal noises, or binding during these tests.
Reassembly
NOTE: Assemble each of the disassembled parts by referring to the Exploded View.
  1. Install the upper spring mounting cushion (A) on the upper spring seat (B) by aligning the ledge portion (C).
  1. Install the damper spring (A) on the upper spring mounting cushion (B), then align the upper end (C) of the damper spring with the cushion stop (D).
  1. Compress the damper spring.
  1. Install all the parts except the damper mounting washer and self-locking nut onto the damper unit (A) by referring to the Exploded View.
  1. Align the bottom of the spring (A) with the stepped part (B) of the lower spring seat.
  1. Align the center of raised portion (A) on the upper spring seat and the damper unit.
  1. Align the damper bracket (B) and the damper mounting base (C) so that ‘‘△'' stamp (D) on it is toward the outside of the vehicle.
  1. Install a new self-locking nut (A).
  1. Hold the damper shaft using a hex wrench (B), and tighten the new self-locking nut to the specified torque value.
