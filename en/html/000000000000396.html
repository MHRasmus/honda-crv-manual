HID Unit Replacement
  1. Turn the combination light switch OFF. Make sure you have the anti-theft codes for the audio system and navigation system (if equipped). Make sure the ignition switch is OFF.
  1. Disconnect the negative battery cable, then disconnect the positive cable.
  1. Remove the headlight assembly.
  1. Remove the bulb socket from the HID bulb.
  1. Remove the three mounting screws and the HID unit (A).
  1. Disconnect the 4P connector (B) from the HID unit.
  1. Inspect the gasket for damage. If damaged, replace it.
  1. Install in the reverse order of removal. After reconnecting the battery, enter the anti-theft codes for the audio system and the navigation system (if equipped).
  1. Do the headlights learning procedure (with HID) and adjust the headlights to local requirements.
